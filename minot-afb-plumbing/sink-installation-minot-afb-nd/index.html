    Sink Replacement and Upgrades in Minot AFB

    Pedestal Sink Installation in Minot AFB, ND

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. The supply stops and the trap sit right behind the column, so we center the drain stub, use chrome or polished brass trap parts, and set the stops tight to the wall where the pedestal can hide them. Blocking in the wall is a must. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.

    Drop-In Sink Installation in Minot AFB, ND

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim covers small flaws in the cutout, so a laminate counter can take a new self-rimming sink with only a check of the hole size and a bead of silicone or putty as the maker specifies. Mounting clips snug it down. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Pedestal Sink Mounting in Minot AFB

    The pedestal is mostly for looks. The basin itself hangs on a bracket lagged into 2x blocking behind the drywall, and if the old wall has no blocking we open it, add a board between the studs, and patch it back. Anchors in drywall will fail. A loose basin cracks the trap.

    Bathroom Vanity Sink Installation in Minot AFB, ND

    Vanity tops with integrated bowls are the easiest bath install. We set the top in silicone, mount the faucet and drain first, and adjust the trap arm to meet the tailpiece. The backsplash gets sealed last. We caulk it to the wall.

    Faucet and Disposal Hookup in Minot AFB, ND

    New sinks often get a new disposal. We mount the disposal on a fresh flange with putty, connect the dishwasher knockout so the hose drains through it, and confirm the outlet sits above the trap inlet so it drains properly. An unpunched knockout backs up the dishwasher. We run both before leaving.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Minot AFB

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. We set the bowl with the clips or anchors the stone shop provides, run a continuous bead of 100 percent silicone around the rim, and leave it braced while the adhesive cures overnight. Glue alone is not enough. Plumbing goes on the next day.
