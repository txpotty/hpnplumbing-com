    Causes of Sewer Line Failure in Minot AFB, ND

    Shifting soil is one of the main reasons sewer joints separate, crack or drop out of grade over the years. Heavy, expansive clay soils are common in North Dakota, and they swell and shrink with moisture and frost, stressing foundations and buried pipe. Roots are the other big one, since a hairline gap at a clay joint is enough for a feeder root to get in and then swell into a mat that catches paper. Age does the rest. Cast iron scales and thins, and clay bells crack under the weight of soil and traffic.
