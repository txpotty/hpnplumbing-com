    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Minot AFB

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. They are not interchangeable.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Minot AFB

    Grinder pumps chop waste into slurry before pumping it, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. They are the right choice when the sewer is far away, uphill or under pressure, like on a pressure sewer system where the pump feeds a shared force main in the street. They cost more. For a basement bath near the drain, a standard ejector usually works.
