    Where We Find Leaks in Minot AFB, ND

    Sewer Line Leak Detection in Minot AFB, ND

    A cracked drain line leaks wastewater into the soil, which can show up as a sewer smell in the yard, a soggy patch or slow drains. Cameras find these. The camera goes in at the cleanout and runs to the main, recording cracks, offset joints and root entry in PVC, clay and cast iron, so you see the exact distance and depth of the break.

    Slab Leaks From Ground Movement in Minot AFB

    Pipe set in concrete cannot flex when the ground moves, so it cracks at elbows and where it rises through the slab. Frost heave can shift soil around sewer lines and crack joints, which often shows up as slow drains or backups. We isolate the line first. Then acoustic listening and thermal imaging find the leak without breaking up the floor to look, and you get a marked spot plus repair options before the saw comes out.

    Commercial Building Leaks in Minot AFB, ND

    Restaurants, offices and apartment complexes in Minot AFB lose money every day a leak runs.     Irrigation Leak Detection in Minot AFB, ND

    Sprinkler laterals crack and zone valves stick open, and either can lose thousands of gallons before the bill shows it. Check the yard first.     Pool Leak Detection in Minot AFB

    A pool losing more than a quarter inch a day beyond evaporation likely has a leak somewhere. We start with a bucket test. Then we pressure test the suction and return lines, listen for leaks with a hydrophone underwater, and dye test around the skimmer, lights and main drain to find the exact crack or fitting that is losing water.

    Preventive Leak Inspections in Minot AFB, ND

    Checking for leaks once a year catches the small stuff. We test static pressure, read the meter with everything off and look at supply lines, water heater fittings and shutoff valves.
