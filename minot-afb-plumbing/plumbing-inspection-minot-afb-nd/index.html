    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in Minot AFB, ND

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Minot AFB, ND

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. Our plumber identifies the supply pipe, whether copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, poly or Kitec, and explains how each usually fails. Some need replacing soon. Poly and Kitec fail suddenly and galvanized chokes off with rust, so we flag them with replacement options.

    Water Pressure and Supply Pipe Review in Minot AFB, ND

    We measure static water pressure and check the pressure reducing valve if one is installed. Too much pressure wears parts. Pressure above 80 psi shortens the life of fixtures, supply hoses and water heaters, and we also identify the supply pipe material, from copper and CPVC to polybutylene, Kitec and galvanized.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Minot AFB, ND

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. We test each valve. Angle stops and the main shutoff valve often seize over the years, and we also listen for gurgling drains that point to venting problems, since small fixes like these are easy to plan once you know about them.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Minot AFB

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. Venting and gas connections get checked. We also look at the expansion tank, drain pan and softener, and a hardness test shows whether the softener is working or just eating salt.

    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Minot AFB, ND

    Strong winds and extreme cold can drive frost into walls and crawlspaces, reaching pipes that seem well protected. A seasonal check looks at the parts of the system that weather affects most. We look at hose bibbs, exposed pipe in garages and crawlspaces, irrigation backflow devices and the water heater's relief valve and drain. Insulation gets checked too. Fixing a cracked hose bibb or adding insulation is a small job compared to a burst pipe in the wall behind it.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Minot AFB

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. Warm spots are another clue. Meter tests, pressure loss checks and a look for damp baseboards come first, and slab homes get acoustic and thermal follow-up. Catching a slab leak early limits damage to floors and foundations.

    Pre-Purchase Plumbing Inspections in Minot AFB

    A house can pass a general inspection and still have a sewer full of roots. Buyers use our inspection to learn the pipe type, the sewer condition and the heater's age before the option period ends. Findings help with negotiations. Old galvanized, a damaged sewer or an aging heater can all become a repair request or a closing credit.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Minot AFB

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. We test the T and P valve, confirm its discharge line runs to a safe place, look for an expansion tank where one is needed and check the drain pan. Gas units get a CO check. A heater that backdrafts can spill combustion gases into the house, so we test the draft at the vent hood and note any problems right away.
