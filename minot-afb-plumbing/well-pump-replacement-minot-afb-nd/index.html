    Control Box and Pressure Switch Replacement in Minot AFB, ND

    Not every dry faucet means a dead pump. A pressure switch with pitted contacts or a plugged sensing tube can stop the pump even though nothing is wrong down the well. A new switch runs about $30 in parts. We show you the burned contacts.

    Grundfos Well Pump Replacement in Minot AFB, ND

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have a built-in soft start and dry-run protection, and they run on two wires with no control box at the surface. They cost more up front. Add the CU 301 controller to an SQE and you get constant pressure.

    Submersible Well Pump Replacement in Minot AFB

    A submersible pump hangs on drop pipe inside a 4-inch or 6-inch casing, often 100 to 400 feet down, so pulling one is a two-person job with a pump hoist. Most fail from sand chewing up the impellers or a lightning surge that cooks the windings. Before pulling, we test the windings with a megohmmeter. That tells us whether the problem is down the hole or up top.

    Well Pump Efficiency Upgrades in Minot AFB, ND

    Older 1 HP pumps running against a 60 psi switch use more power than most people expect, especially on a well with a long horizontal run to the house. Right-sizing the pump or adding a variable-speed drive often trims the electric bill. The math does not always work. We run the numbers with you.

    Jet Pump Replacement in Minot AFB, ND

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. A two-pipe deep-well jet can reach further down at the cost of efficiency. The common failure is losing prime. Usually a leaking foot valve or a cracked suction line lets the water fall back down the well, so we check both before we blame the pump.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Replacement in Minot AFB

    Franklin Electric builds a large share of the submersible motors sold in the US, including ones sitting under other brands' pump ends. Replacement control boxes are easy to source. We match the motor and the wire gauge to the depth of your well.

    Solar Well Pump Systems in Minot AFB, ND

    Solar well pumps make sense for livestock tanks and cabins where running power from the meter would cost more than the pump itself. A few panels feed a DC pump through a controller while the sun is up. You store water, not electricity. We size panels to your daily gallons.

    Constant Pressure Well Pump Installation in Minot AFB, ND

    Constant pressure systems hold the house at one pressure, say 50 psi, whether one faucet is open or the shower and the irrigation are running together. A variable frequency drive speeds the pump up and slows it down to match demand. No more pressure dip mid-shower.
