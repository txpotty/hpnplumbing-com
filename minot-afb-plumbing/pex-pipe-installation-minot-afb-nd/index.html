    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in Minot AFB, ND

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a secure seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can be repaired from kinks with a heat gun. It is our preferred choice for many full repipes.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Minot AFB

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Water in North Dakota is often very hard, with high mineral and sulfate content that leaves deposits in water heaters and fixtures. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more usually follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and reduces the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

    Hydronic Heating with PEX in Minot AFB

    PEX is also a leading choice for hydronic heating systems, including radiant floor heating.     PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Minot AFB
