    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Wahpeton, ND

    A cracked or poorly sloped shower pan often causes wet baseboards and stained ceilings below. We test the pan by plugging the drain and flooding it to verify the leak. Then we rebuild the pan with correct pre-slope, a new liner or bonded membrane and a properly clamped drain. A second flood test confirms the pan is watertight before tile.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Wahpeton, ND

    Older homes, especially those built before modern waterproofing standards, are particularly prone to water intrusion. Older homes may still have galvanized supply lines or cast iron drains, which can corrode and restrict flow with age. Many older foundations may lack adequate exterior drainage or proper sealing. We specialize in assessing and upgrading waterproofing systems for these properties, often combining exterior excavation with interior drainage solutions. We take care to respect the existing structure while installing modern, effective waterproofing, with attention to the challenges of older brick and stone foundations.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Wahpeton, ND

    Block and stem walls often show efflorescence, which is the white mineral crust left when water passes through masonry. Irrigation and sprinklers aimed at the house are frequent culprits. We adjust or reroute irrigation, clean the wall and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating. Proper grading and drip line placement help keep the wall dry.

It's an effective way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.
