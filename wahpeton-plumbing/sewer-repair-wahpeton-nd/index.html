    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Wahpeton

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful cleaning method that uses high-pressure water streams to clear stubborn blockages, grease buildup and even tree roots from sewer lines. It's much more effective than snaking for thorough cleaning and preventing recurring clogs. This service is particularly helpful for restaurants and commercial properties in Wahpeton dealing with heavy grease accumulation, as well as homes with recurring drain issues.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Wahpeton

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to recurring clogs. We excavate at the marked spot, cut out the damaged length and install new Schedule 40 PVC with shielded couplings rated for the transition. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it holds.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Wahpeton, ND

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive option. This method, which includes pipe bursting or pipe lining, requires minimal excavation, saving your yard and landscaping from extensive disruption. We use specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one or insert a resin-coated liner that cures in place, creating a new, durable pipe inside the old one. It's an effective and popular choice for preserving property appearance in Wahpeton.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Wahpeton, ND

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair method where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without major digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an excellent option for preserving landscapes in Wahpeton.
