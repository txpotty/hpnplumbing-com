    Gas Appliance Connector Repair in Wahpeton, ND

    Weak flames or a smell near one appliance often point to the connector or shutoff behind it.     Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Wahpeton

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age.
