    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Replacement in Wahpeton

    The cost for toilet replacement in Wahpeton, ND can range from about $350 to $800, including the new toilet unit and labor. Factors like the type of toilet (standard, high-efficiency, comfort height), any necessary plumbing modifications, and the complexity of the old toilet's removal can influence the final price. We provide upfront estimates.

    The rough-in measurement refers to the distance from the finished wall behind the toilet to the center of the drainpipe (the bolts that secure the toilet). Standard rough-ins are typically 12 inches, but 10-inch and 14-inch options exist. Knowing your rough-in is crucial to make sure your new toilet fits correctly in the existing space.

    Older toilets can use 3.5 to 7 gallons per flush, while WaterSense toilets use 1.28 gallons or less. For a typical household, that can mean thousands of gallons saved each year.

    We frequently recommend and install reliable brands like American Standard, Kohler, Toto, and Delta. These brands offer a range of durable, water-efficient models that perform well across a wide range of water conditions and are known for their long service life.

    While it is possible to DIY a toilet replacement, we strongly recommend hiring a professional. Improper installation can lead to leaks, water damage, and costly repairs. Our plumbers make sure of correct sealing, proper flange connection, and adherence to all plumbing codes.

    Most standard toilet replacements can be completed by a professional plumber in about 2 to 4 hours. This includes the removal of the old unit, preparation of the flange, installation of the new toilet, and testing for leaks. More complex installations might take a bit longer.

    Manufacturers usually warranty parts for 1 to 5 years and porcelain longer. We guarantee our workmanship and will return if a leak is caused by the installation.

    A like-for-like toilet replacement in the same location usually does not require a permit. If drains are moved or a new bathroom is added, a permit is usually required.
