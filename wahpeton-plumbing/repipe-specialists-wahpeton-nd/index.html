    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Wahpeton, ND

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Brown water after the house sits overnight, weak flow at upstairs showers and leaks at threaded joints are the usual signs that galvanized is near the end. New PEX or copper takes its place. Most homeowners notice the pressure difference the first time they turn on the shower.

    Water in North Dakota is often very hard, with high mineral and sulfate content that leaves deposits in water heaters and fixtures. Hard water deposits calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe over time. Pressure falls as scale builds. In metal pipe, scale also speeds up corrosion, so repiping with PEX-A and adding a softener or filter during the job can protect the new plumbing and your fixtures for years.

    Our repipes come with a written workmanship warranty on parts and labor, usually covering several years. Manufacturer coverage adds more. When certified installers use the full PEX-A system, the maker's long-term warranty applies, and both sets of terms are listed on your estimate.
