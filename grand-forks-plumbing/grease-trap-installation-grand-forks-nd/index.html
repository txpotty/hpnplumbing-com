    Under-Sink Grease Trap Setup in Grand Forks, ND

    Small cafes and coffee bars often get by with a 10 to 25 GPM hydromechanical trap tucked under the prep sink or beside it. It sits on the floor or in a recess, with the flow control fitting on the inlet side. Clearance matters most. We leave room to pull the lid and baffle without unhooking the sink, because a trap nobody can open is a trap nobody cleans.

    Commercial Kitchen Grease Trap Installs in Grand Forks

    Every commercial install starts with a fixture list. The tech walks the kitchen and writes down every fixture that drains to the trap and how fast each one empties. That number decides between an indoor unit and an outdoor tank, and where it can go with clean pipe runs to the sewer. Fewer bends, fewer clogs.

    Emergency Grease Trap Services in Grand Forks, ND

    A grease trap backing up into the floor sinks at the start of Friday dinner service means a closed kitchen and a health inspector who may not let you reopen until it is fixed. We send a tech with a jetter and a camera to find out whether the trap or the line on either side is the problem. Often it is the outlet. If the trap itself has failed, we can usually set a temporary unit and schedule the permanent replacement.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in Grand Forks, ND

    Hydromechanical traps use baffles and a vented flow control to pull grease out of water moving at a set rate, usually 20 to 100 gallons per minute. They fit indoors, under a sink or in a floor pit. The flow control is not optional. Skip it and a full sink drain overruns the rating, which sends grease right past the baffles. We look for units certified to PDI G-101 or ASME A112.14.3.

    Automatic Grease Removal Units in Grand Forks

    An automatic grease removal unit heats the trapped grease and skims it into a container on a timer, so staff empty a bucket instead of scraping a tank. They cost more up front. Plan for a dedicated circuit near the unit, which your electrician will need to run. Fry-heavy kitchens like them most.
