    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in Grand Forks, ND

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Grand Forks

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most dependable way to stop a basement leak, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing to carry the water away from the wall. Heavy, expansive clay soils are common in North Dakota, and they swell and shrink with moisture and frost, stressing foundations and buried pipe. Backfill goes in last.

    Commercial Wall Waterproofing in Grand Forks

    Restaurant kitchens, gym locker rooms and salon wash stations put water on walls all day. We waterproof commercial showers, mop sink walls and restroom wet walls with membranes listed for continuous wet use, and we repair the supply and drain piping behind them if demolition turns up a leak. After-hours scheduling is available.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Grand Forks

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. A horizontal crack often signals soil pressure pushing the wall in, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Most vertical cracks get sealed in one visit.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Grand Forks, ND

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the waterline and check it a day later. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a clamping drain tightened onto the liner. The new pan gets its own flood test before tile.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in Grand Forks, ND

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head spraying the wall instead of the plants, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. Then we clean the wall, let it dry and apply a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.

    Waterproofing Costs vs. Repeat Water Damage in Grand Forks, ND

    Paying once beats paying every two years. Waterproofing a shower wall usually lands in the low thousands, while replacing drywall, cabinets and flooring again and again costs far more, and mold remediation on top of that can double the bill. The price for exterior block sealing depends on how much wall there is. Your quote lists each option with its price.

    Not every wet wall needs a $15,000 excavation. Sometimes the fix is a downspout extension and regrading the soil so it slopes away from the house, a drop of about six inches in the first ten feet, and that costs a few hundred dollars. We start with the cheapest fix that will actually work. Every option comes written out with its price.
