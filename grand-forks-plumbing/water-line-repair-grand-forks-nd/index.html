    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Grand Forks, ND

    Galvanized service lines were common in houses built before the 1970s, and a lot of them are still in the ground.     PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Grand Forks

    Copper and PEX fail in different places. Copper tends to pinhole where a rock rubs it or where the water chemistry eats the wall from inside. PEX usually fails at a fitting. We cut out the bad section of copper and sweat or press in a new length, while PEX gets a fresh crimp ring or expansion fitting with the right tool for the brand.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Grand Forks

    Buried copper is tough, but it is not forever. Pinholes show up where stones press on the pipe, and acidic soil or high water velocity can thin the wall from the inside.     Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Grand Forks

Old gate valves that will not close get replaced with full-port ball valves. That way you can actually shut the water off.
