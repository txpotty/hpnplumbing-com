    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Grand Forks, ND

    The float switch is a key component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to support reliable operation for your home in Grand Forks, ND.

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Grand Forks, ND

    The float switch is the most common reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get hung up.

    Sump Pump Cleaning and Debris Removal in Grand Forks

    Over time, sump pits can accumulate dirt, gravel and other debris, which can clog the pump's intake or jam the float switch. Our maintenance process includes carefully cleaning the sump pit and removing any obstructions. This lets the pump operate freely and efficiently, preventing breakdowns and extending its lifespan for your Grand Forks property.
