    Dripping Faucet Repair in Grand Forks

    A dripping spout typically means a worn cartridge, washer or valve seat. Hard water speeds up this wear by leaving scale that scratches seals and keeps them from closing tight. We disassemble the faucet, replace the worn parts and clean the valve body. Fixing a drip right away keeps a small leak from building up on your water bill.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Grand Forks

    Weak or sputtering flow is usually caused by mineral buildup in the aerator or cartridge. We remove and clean or replace the aerator, flush the lines and check the cartridge for scale blockage. On pull-down faucets, the spray head and check valves can also clog. If flow is low at every fixture, we test whole-home water pressure.

    Compression Faucet Repair in Grand Forks

    Compression faucets, often found in older Grand Forks homes, are known for their separate hot and cold handles. When these faucets leak, it's typically due to worn-out rubber washers or packing. Our team is skilled in disassembling these classic fixtures, replacing the necessary seals and washers, and reassembling them to stop leaks for good. We respect the character of older homes and make sure these repairs are done carefully.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Grand Forks

    Ceramic disc faucets are known for their durability and are a popular choice in many contemporary Grand Forks bathrooms. While generally reliable, they can develop leaks if the ceramic discs become scratched or misaligned, or if the inlet and outlet seals wear out. Our plumbers have the expertise to carefully disassemble these precise mechanisms, clean or replace the ceramic discs, and reseal the faucet to eliminate leaks and restore full performance.

    Faucet Handle Repair & Replacement in Grand Forks, ND

    A loose, broken, or stripped faucet handle makes it difficult to control water flow and temperature. Over time, daily use can wear down the internal mechanisms or cause the handle itself to break. We offer expert repair for various handle types, from single-handle mixers to separate hot and cold handles. If a repair isn't feasible, we can recommend and install a suitable replacement handle that matches your existing fixture and style, extending its lifespan.
