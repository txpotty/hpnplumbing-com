    Frequently Asked Questions About PEX Pipe Installation in Grand Forks

    A whole-home PEX repipe typically costs $4,500 to $12,000 depending on home size, bathroom count and access. Smaller jobs like a single bathroom remodel rough-in often run $800 to $2,500.

    PEX can be used for outdoor applications like irrigation lines or outdoor spigots, but it needs to be protected from direct UV light, which can degrade the material over time. When used outdoors, PEX should consistently be buried or otherwise shielded.

    Small repairs are possible for handy homeowners, but a full repipe requires proper sizing, support spacing and code compliance. Mistakes behind walls can cause hidden leaks and failed inspections.

    While PEX installation can look straightforward, professional installation is strongly recommended, especially for whole-home repipes or main line work. Proper tools, techniques and adherence to local plumbing codes are essential for a leak-free and safe system. DIY mistakes can lead to costly water damage and future repairs, so trusting HPN Plumbing for your Grand Forks installation gives you peace of mind.

    Properly installed PEX is expected to last several decades. Manufacturers often offer 25 year warranties when their pipe and fittings are used together.

    Multiple pinhole leaks, discolored water, low pressure throughout the house and gray polybutylene pipe are typical signs. Recurring slab leaks are also a good reason to repipe overhead.

    We mainly use high-quality, reputable PEX brands such as Uponor (PEX-A) and Viega (PEX-B), which are known for their durability and reliability. These brands meet or exceed industry standards and are approved for use in North Dakota plumbing applications, providing a long-lasting and dependable plumbing system for your home.

    PEX resists scale and corrosion, flexes with temperature changes and has fewer joints. Copper is very durable but can develop pinhole leaks in some water conditions and costs more to install.
