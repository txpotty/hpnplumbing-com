    Underground and Under-Slab Plumbing in Grand Forks

    The underground phase sets the drain, waste and vent layout that every fixture in the house will depend on. We compact bedding carefully and avoid rigid points that could stress the pipe as the ground moves. Each line is water tested and left exposed for the inspector before your concrete contractor pours.

    Final Trim and Fixture Setting in Grand Forks

    Rough-In and Top-Out Plumbing in Grand Forks, ND
