    Sinks, Disposals and Drains in Grand Forks, ND: How We Handle Them

    Burst Supply Lines in Grand Forks, ND

    Supply lines with plastic coupling nuts split more often than braided stainless, especially after years of pressure swings. A failed line under the sink can put several gallons a minute into the cabinet and the floor below before anyone notices it. We replace both sides. If one line cracked from age, the other one is about the same age.

    Workstation and Farmhouse Sinks in Grand Forks

    Farmhouse sinks need a cabinet built to carry them, since a 30 inch fireclay bowl can weigh over 100 pounds before water. The support frame gets checked first. Workstation sinks with ledges and accessories often have an offset drain, so the trap and disposal have to be planned around that drain location before the counter crew arrives.

    InSinkErator Models We Service in Grand Forks

    Badger units are the basic line, with a single grind stage and a 1/3 to 3/4 horsepower motor. Evolution models add grind stages and sound insulation, which you notice in an open floor plan, and some carry longer in-home warranties than the basic line does. We stock the common sizes. Older InSinkErator units with a good motor can usually be repaired.

    New Disposal Installation in Grand Forks

    Adding a disposal where there never was one means a switched outlet or circuit in the cabinet, and that part is electrical work. We handle the plumbing and can line up the electrician. The trap often has to drop a couple of inches because the disposal outlet sits lower than the sink tailpiece did, and the wall stub has to allow it.

    Grease Trap Installation in Grand Forks, ND

    Small kitchens can usually get by with an under-sink trap, while full restaurants often need an in-ground interceptor sized to the local sewer authority's rules. The local rule gets checked before we price it. Sizing is based on fixture flow, and an undersized trap fills fast and lets grease pass straight into the sewer line.

    Disposal Jams and Hums in Grand Forks, ND

    Humming with no grinding means the motor is getting power but the plate is stuck. Usually a ten minute fix. We free the plate from below with a hex key, clear the hopper with pliers, never a hand, and reset the overload before we test it with water. Silence with no hum points to the switch, the outlet or the breaker instead.

    Kitchen Drain Clog Removal in Grand Forks, ND

    Water rising in the second bowl when the first drains tells us the clog is past the trap, in the branch line inside the wall. We run a 1/4 or 3/8 inch cable from the cleanout or the trap arm, then jet the line if the cable comes back coated in grease. Drain chemicals do little to grease. They sit in the trap and soften PVC glue joints over time.

    Recurring Kitchen Clogs in Grand Forks, ND

    Grease, rice, pasta and coffee grounds build up in the trap and branch line over months, not days. We clear the line with a cable sized for kitchen pipe and can camera it when the same clog returns. The pipe is sometimes the cause. A low spot in the line or a drain arm without enough fall will collect grease however often it is cleared.
