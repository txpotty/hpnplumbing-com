    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Grand Forks

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. A cable cuts a channel through it. A spinning nozzle removes the grease all around the pipe so it flushes away cleanly.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Grand Forks, ND

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. Jetting costs more up front. Fewer repeat visits often make jetting cheaper over a few years.

    Water-Only Drain Cleaning in Grand Forks, ND

    Hydrojetting uses only water, so nothing caustic goes into your pipes or septic tank. Chemical cleaners can damage pipe. The debris flushes away as normal waste, leaving nothing behind to harm pipe or septic bacteria.

    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Grand Forks

    Preventive jetting makes sense when the same line clogs more than once a year. Camera findings set the interval. A line with heavy grease might need jetting every six months, while a sewer with a few root entry points might only need it every 18 to 24 months, and the footage tells us which.
