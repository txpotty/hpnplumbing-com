    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Grand Forks, ND

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life.     Black Iron Pipe Repair in Grand Forks, ND

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Years of vibration from a furnace or a house settling can loosen threaded fittings, and pipe in a damp garage or an exterior wall can rust from the outside in. We reseal or replace the bad sections. Outdoor runs get coated or painted after the repair so rust does not start again.

    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in Grand Forks

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. With every appliance off, a moving test dial on the meter tells us gas is escaping somewhere, and then we isolate branches to narrow it down. A combustible gas detector narrows it further. Soap solution at the suspect fitting confirms it with bubbles, and then we repair the joint, replace the section or swap the connector before testing again.
