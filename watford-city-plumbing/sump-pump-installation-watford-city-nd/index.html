    New Sump Pit Installation in Watford City

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can create one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, compacted clay or rock and set a durable basin.     Strong storms can knock out power right when your pump is needed most. Battery backup pumps take over automatically when the main pump fails or power goes out.
