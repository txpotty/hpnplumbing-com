    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Watford City, ND

    On a double sink, the disposal goes on one bowl and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. We lay out the drain so both bowls drain freely and the disposal outlet does not push water into the other bowl, which happens when the tee sits too close to the disposal discharge. We use heavy-wall fittings.

    Dishwasher Connection With Disposal Installation in Watford City

    Every new disposal has a plug sealing the dishwasher inlet. We knock it out with a screwdriver and hammer, fish the plug out of the chamber and connect the hose with a new clamp. A plug left in backs up the dishwasher. We also install an air gap or high loop, so dirty sink water cannot flow back into the dishwasher while the disposal is draining.

    Switching From Batch-Feed to Continuous-Feed in Watford City, ND

    A continuous-feed unit needs a switch, which a batch-feed kitchen may not have. That can be a wall switch, an air switch button on the sink deck or a switched outlet under the sink, and the choice depends on how hard it is to run wire to the spot you want. An air button skips the wall wiring. The drain usually stays the same.

    Same-Day Disposal Installation in Watford City, ND

    When the old disposal dies and the sink is full of water, we can usually install a new one the same day.     First-Time Disposal Installation in Watford City

    If your kitchen has never had a disposal, we start by checking the sink flange, drain height and electrical supply.     Air Switch Disposal Installation in Watford City, ND

    An air button on the sink deck or counter turns the disposal on. Pressing the button sends a pulse of air through a small tube to a control box under the sink, so there is no electricity at the button and no need to drill for a wall switch. Islands use them often.

    High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in Watford City

    A 3/4 or 1 HP unit takes up more room and weighs more than a basic unit, so we measure the cabinet first.
