    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in Watford City

    A standard disposal replacement with the unit typically costs $275 to $650. Premium models like InSinkErator Evolution usually run $500 to $1,000 installed.

    In most cases, a simple like-for-like garbage disposal replacement in Watford City does not require a permit. However, if significant plumbing modifications or electrical work beyond a simple swap are needed, a permit might be required. We'll advise you if this is the case.

    Most disposals last 8 to 12 years. Hard water, heavy use and grease can cut that life.

    If your disposal is old, frequently leaking, or making grinding noises without grinding food, replacement is usually more cost-effective and provides a longer-lasting solution. Minor issues like a simple jam can usually be repaired.

    For light use in a single-person household, 1/3 HP might suffice. Most homeowners opt for 1/2 HP or 3/4 HP for general use, which handles common food waste well. For large families, frequent cooking, or tougher waste like small bones, a 1 HP unit offers the best performance and durability.
