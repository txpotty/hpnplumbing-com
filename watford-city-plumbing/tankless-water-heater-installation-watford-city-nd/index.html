    Converting from a tank requires more than swapping units.     Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Watford City, ND

    Calcium scale is the most common cause of tankless heater failure. Water in North Dakota is often very hard, with high mineral and sulfate content that builds scale in water heaters and fixtures. Scale coats the heat exchanger, reducing efficiency and triggering error codes. We install isolation valves so the unit can be flushed with a vinegar or descaling solution once a year. A scale inhibitor cartridge or whole-home softener provides added protection.
