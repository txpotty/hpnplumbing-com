    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Watford City

    The cost of water heater replacement in Watford City, ND usually ranges from $1,200 to $2,500 for a standard tank unit, including installation. Tankless units can range from $3,000 to $5,000 or more, depending on the model and necessary plumbing modifications. Factors like unit type, capacity, brand and labor complexity influence the final price.

    Most standard tank-style water heater replacements can be completed in 3 to 5 hours, assuming no major complications or plumbing reconfigurations. Tankless or hybrid installations might take a bit longer, often 5 to 8 hours, due to more complex venting or gas line changes.

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and A.O. Smith water heaters. Each offers dependable performance and a range of warranties.

    Tank water heaters often last about 8 to 12 years, and hard water and sediment buildup can shorten that. Water in North Dakota is often very hard, with high mineral and sulfate content that leaves deposits in water heaters and fixtures. Annual flushing and anode rod checks can help lengthen that life.

    Tankless offers endless hot water and a longer lifespan but costs more upfront. We can compare both options based on your household and budget.

    Many households of two to three people do well with a 40 gallon tank, while larger families often need 50 gallons or more. We size based on your bathroom count and usage.

    Choosing the right size depends on the number of people in your household, their hot water usage habits and peak demand times. Our technicians will calculate the right GPM (gallons per minute) for tankless or gallon capacity for tank models so you never run out of hot water, especially in multi-bathroom Watford City homes.

    Generally, if your water heater is over 10 years old, frequently breaks down or has a major issue like a leaking tank, replacement is often more cost-effective in the long run. For newer units with minor, easily fixable issues, repair might be a viable option. Our technicians can provide an honest assessment.
