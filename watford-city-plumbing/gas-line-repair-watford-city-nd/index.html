    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Watford City

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. We back out the leaking joint, clean the threads, apply gas-rated thread sealant and reassemble it, or replace the fitting or valve if the threads are damaged. Every repair gets a soap test and a system pressure test before we leave.

    Gas Line Repair Costs in Watford City, ND

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Watford City

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. We replace corroded sections with polyethylene pipe laid with tracer wire, using an approved transition riser where the line comes up out of the ground at the house. Above-ground runs stay in steel. We test before backfilling so any problem joint is fixed while the trench is still open.

    Gas Line Extensions and Relocations in Watford City

    Adding a gas fireplace, moving a laundry room or switching a range from electric to gas usually means extending the gas line. Sizing comes first. After that comes routing, the tie-in and the test.

    Buried Yard Line Repair and Replacement in Watford City, ND

    Many older homes have steel gas lines running from the meter to the house or out to a pool heater. The wrap fails eventually. The new line is polyethylene with tracer wire, or coated steel where it has to be exposed, and we backfill and clean up the yard when the test passes.

    Gas Appliance Connector Repair in Watford City, ND

    Weak flames or a smell near one appliance often point to the connector or shutoff behind it.
