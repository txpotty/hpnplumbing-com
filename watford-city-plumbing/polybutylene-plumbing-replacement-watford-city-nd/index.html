    Why Polybutylene Fails in Watford City

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. The inner wall of the pipe and fittings slowly turns brittle, and small cracks grow until the pipe splits. Many rural homes get water from private wells or rural water systems, and iron and sulfur odors are common complaints. Heat makes it worse. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.
