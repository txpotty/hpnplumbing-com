    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Watford City

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. Instead of removing hardness, they convert it into a form that resists sticking, which reduces scale without salt or brine waste. Little upkeep is needed. They suit moderate hardness.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Watford City

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. Install follows the manufacturer manual for bypass, drain and overflow, then we set hardness and regeneration. We install store-bought units. We set it up for you.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Watford City, ND

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HPN Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. Most effective option.

    Pelican Water Softener Installation in Watford City, ND

    Pelican sells both salt-based softeners and salt-free conditioners, often direct to homeowners.
