    PEX must be protected from sunlight because UV light degrades it. Outdoors it needs to be buried, sleeved or fully covered.

    PEX can be used for outdoor applications like irrigation lines or outdoor spigots, but it needs to be protected from direct UV light, which can break down the material over time. When used outdoors, PEX should consistently be buried or otherwise shielded.

    Properly installed PEX is expected to last many decades. Manufacturers commonly offer 25 year warranties when their pipe and fittings are used together.

    PEX is well suited for a wide range of climates because of its flexibility and resistance to freezing. Long, bitterly cold winters are the defining climate stress in North Dakota, putting heavy demand on water heaters and heating systems. While rigid pipes can burst when water freezes, PEX can expand and contract, significantly reducing the risk of burst pipes during cold snaps. It also performs well in high temperatures, making it a reliable choice year-round in Devils Lake.
