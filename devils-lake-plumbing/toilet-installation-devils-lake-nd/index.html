    Wall-Mounted Toilet Installation in Devils Lake

    Wall-mounted toilets hang off a steel carrier bolted to the floor inside the wall. The tank sits behind an access plate. The carrier holds the whole load, so its feet get anchored to slab or framing and it gets plumbed before any wall finish goes on. Plan on a remodel, not a swap.

    Smart Toilet Installation in Devils Lake, ND

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most require a GFCI outlet within reach of the cord. The water connection and mounting are ours, and when an outlet has to be added we schedule a licensed electrician instead of doing that wiring. Then we test every button.

    ADA-Compliant Toilet Installation in Devils Lake

    ADA toilets put the seat 17 to 19 inches off the floor, and the centerline sits 16 to 18 inches from the side wall. That spacing leaves room for a grab bar. Before anything is ordered we measure the drain, because shifting it to meet the ADA centerline means cutting into the floor. Elongated bowls are standard here.

    Comfort-Height and ADA Toilets in Devils Lake

    Aging in place usually starts in the bathroom. A taller toilet and a grab bar go a long way.
