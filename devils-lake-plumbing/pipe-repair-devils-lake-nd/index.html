    Preventive measures include insulating pipes in unheated areas, maintaining proper water pressure, addressing hard water issues that can lead to mineral buildup and regular plumbing inspections. For homes in North Dakota, maintaining proper drainage around foundations can also prevent soil shifts that damage underground pipes.

    Common signs include a sudden drop in water pressure, visible water stains on walls or ceilings, musty odors, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use, unusually high water bills or damp spots in your yard.     Homeowner policies frequently cover sudden water damage from a burst pipe, but not the cost of fixing worn or corroded pipe. Check your policy, and we will provide documentation to support any claim.

    Most pipe repairs take one to three hours. Repairs requiring wall or ceiling access may take longer, and drywall repair is typically a separate step after the plumbing is done.
