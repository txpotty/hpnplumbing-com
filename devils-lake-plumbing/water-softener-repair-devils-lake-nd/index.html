    Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in Devils Lake, ND

    Age and parts availability decide whether a softener is worth repairing. HPN Plumbing gives honest advice on both counts. A sound tank with a rebuildable valve is worth saving, but an obsolete head without parts usually is not. We lay out both options.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Devils Lake

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. This is common with older systems on treated city water. HPN Plumbing pulls the old resin and fills the tank with new media. Capacity comes back right away.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Devils Lake, ND

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Its small ports clog with sediment and salt. A clogged injector leaves the brine tank full of water and the resin never gets recharged. We clean or replace it and check the brine line for air leaks.

    Control Valve Rebuild in Devils Lake

    Service, backwash, brine and rinse are the four cycles the control valve directs every time the softener regenerates, usually in the middle of the night. Hard water and chlorine wear out the seals, spacers and pistons. Leaks at the valve or water running to the drain are common signs. The valve gets rebuilt with the kit made for it.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Devils Lake

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. An old timer head regenerates by the calendar, used or not. We retest hardness and reprogram the valve, or upgrade the head to a metered control that only regenerates when the resin is actually close to exhausted. Salt bills drop.
