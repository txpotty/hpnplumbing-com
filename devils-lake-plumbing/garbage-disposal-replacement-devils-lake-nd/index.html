    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in Devils Lake

    The cost for garbage disposal replacement in Devils Lake, ND usually ranges from $200 to $600. This includes the new unit itself, which can vary from $100 for a basic 1/3 HP model to over $300 for a high-end 1 HP unit, plus labor costs. Factors like the disposal's horsepower, brand, and any necessary plumbing modifications can influence the final price.

    For light use in a single-person household, 1/3 HP might be enough. Most homeowners opt for 1/2 HP or 3/4 HP for general use, which handles common food waste well. For large families, frequent cooking, or tougher waste like small bones, a 1 HP unit offers the best performance and durability.

    To prolong the life of your new disposal, avoid putting fibrous foods like celery, corn husks, or onion skins down it. Also, do not dispose of grease, coffee grounds, or non-food items. Always run cold water before, during, and after use, and grind small amounts of citrus peels or ice cubes now and then to clean the unit.

    New garbage disposals usually come with manufacturer warranties ranging from 1 to 10 years, depending on the model and brand. HPN Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation services for your peace of mind.
