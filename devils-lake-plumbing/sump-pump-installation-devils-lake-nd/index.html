    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Devils Lake

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. Because everything comes assembled on a single riser, they fit into an 18-inch pit that would be too tight for two separate pumps and two check valves. Install time is shorter too. We set them in homes that want two layers without a bigger pit.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Devils Lake, ND

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. A smart charger keeps the battery topped off and sounds an alarm if the battery weakens, the primary fails or the water rises past the backup float. Expect a battery swap every four to five years. Finished basements get the most out of them.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Devils Lake

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. The pit lid sits flush. We seal the cord grommet and the lid on every install.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Devils Lake, ND

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. We install their M53 and M98 models often. Backup and combo units are available too. Pit size and lift decide which model we recommend.

    Plan on seven to ten years for a well-kept pump. A pump that cycles every few minutes through a wet spring wears out faster than one that runs a few times a month, and cheap switches fail first. A quick inspection tells us where it stands.
