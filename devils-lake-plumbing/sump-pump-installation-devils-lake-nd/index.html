    New Sump Pit Installation in Devils Lake

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can build one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, hardpan or rock and set a durable basin. We add gravel around the basin to help water flow in. Then we patch the concrete and install the pump and cover. A new pit gives you a reliable place to collect and remove water.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Devils Lake, ND

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to reach for maintenance and repair. They are often a good choice for shallow sump pits or areas where space is tight. Our team is skilled in installing these pumps, making sure of proper pit depth and discharge line setup for reliable performance in Devils Lake, ND.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Devils Lake

    Submersible sump pumps sit directly inside the sump pit, operating quietly and taking up less space. They are typically more powerful and durable, making them a popular choice for many homeowners. We handle the complete installation of submersible units, including proper sealing and electrical connections, matched to the specific needs of your Devils Lake property.
