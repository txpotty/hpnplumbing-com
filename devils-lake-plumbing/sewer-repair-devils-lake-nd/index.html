    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Devils Lake, ND

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive option. This method, which includes pipe bursting or pipe lining, requires minimal excavation, saving your yard and landscaping from extensive disruption. We use specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one or insert a resin-coated liner that cures in place, creating a new, durable pipe inside the old one. It's an effective and popular choice for protecting property appearance in Devils Lake.

    Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Devils Lake, ND

    While often overlooked, the sewer vent pipe is crucial for proper drainage and preventing sewer gas buildup. Issues with vent pipes, such as blockages from animal nests or damage from strong winds, can lead to slow drains, gurgling noises and foul odors. HPN Plumbing diagnoses and repairs vent pipe problems, ensuring your entire drainage system works correctly and safely and protecting good indoor air quality.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Devils Lake, ND

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair technique where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without extensive digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an excellent option for preserving landscapes in Devils Lake.
