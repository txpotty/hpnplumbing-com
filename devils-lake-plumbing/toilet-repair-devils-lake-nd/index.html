    Running Toilet Fixes in Devils Lake

    A toilet that constantly runs or refills itself intermittently is not only annoying but also a major water waste. This issue in Devils Lake is typically caused by a faulty flapper valve, an improperly adjusted float, a worn-out fill valve, or sediment buildup. Our technicians diagnose the specific component causing the problem and replace it with durable, long-lasting parts. We ensure proper alignment and adjustment of all internal mechanisms to restore your toilet's efficient operation and stop wasted water consumption.

    Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Devils Lake, ND

    The gaskets and seals within your toilet tank and at its base are crucial for preventing leaks. Over time, these rubber components can degrade, crack, or become brittle due to age and exposure to water, especially where the water is hard. We specialize in replacing worn-out flapper seals, tank-to-bowl gaskets, and the wax ring at the toilet's base, which is essential for preventing leaks onto your bathroom floor and maintaining a watertight seal.

    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Devils Lake, ND

    A hairline crack in the tank or a bulging braided supply line can let go without warning and flood a bathroom. We inspect the tank, tank-to-bowl bolts and gasket, and the supply line during every visit. If the porcelain is cracked we will price a replacement tank or new toilet, and we upgrade old plastic or vinyl supply lines to braided stainless.

    Leaky Toilet Repair in Devils Lake

    A leaky toilet can waste hundreds of gallons of water annually, increasing your utility bills and potentially causing water damage. Our technicians in Devils Lake are experts at identifying the source of leaks, whether it's a cracked tank or bowl, a faulty wax ring, or a worn-out fill valve. We use specialized dyes and pressure tests to reliably locate hidden leaks and provide durable repairs, often involving the replacement of seals, gaskets, or the entire flushing mechanism. Ignoring a leak can lead to structural damage or mold growth, so prompt repair is crucial.

    Fill Valve Replacement in Devils Lake

    A noisy, slow or hissing tank often points to a worn fill valve. Mineral scale clogs the small ports inside, which causes long refill times and phantom refills in the night. We replace old ballcock valves with modern adjustable fill valves from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the water level to the manufacturer mark. The job takes about 20 minutes and makes an older toilet noticeably quieter.

    Weak Flush and Clog Repair in Devils Lake, ND

    If your toilet needs two flushes or clogs often, the problem could be scale in the rim jets, a partial blockage in the trapway or a flush valve that does not open fully. We auger the trap, check the vent if gurgling is present and clean the jets with a descaling treatment. Some low-flow toilets from the 1990s simply flush poorly, and we will tell you honestly if replacement is the better fix.

    Leaking Toilet Repair in Devils Lake

    Toilet leaks can cause serious water damage to your bathroom floor, subfloor, and even the ceiling below. Leaks can start from the base of the toilet, the tank, or the supply line. Common culprits include a worn-out wax ring, loose bolts, or cracked porcelain. We thoroughly inspect all potential leak points, replacing seals, tightening connections, or recommending bowl replacement if the porcelain is damaged, preventing further water intrusion.
