    Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in Devils Lake

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. The carrier itself sometimes needs new bolts or a coupling. We match the new bowl to the existing carrier whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.

    Toilet Rough-In Adjustments in Devils Lake, ND

    Rough-in is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, usually 12 inches, sometimes 10 or 14 in older homes. Take the measurement from the wall to the closet bolts, not from the baseboard trim. If the new toilet's rough-in does not match, it either sits too far out or will not fit. An offset flange can correct an inch or two without moving the drain.
