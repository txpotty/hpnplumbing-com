    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Devils Lake

    The cost of hydro jetting in Devils Lake, ND usually ranges from $300 to $800, depending on the severity of the clog, the length and diameter of the pipe, and ease of access. More complex jobs, like main sewer line descaling or extensive root removal, may be on the higher end. We provide a clear quote after an initial inspection.

    Drain snaking uses a cable with an auger that punches a hole through a blockage to restore some flow. Hydro jetting, however, uses high-pressure water to completely scour the inside of the pipe, removing all buildup and restoring the pipe to a near-new condition. Hydro jetting offers a more thorough and longer-lasting clean.

    Repeat clogs in the same line, slow drains after snaking, sewer odors and known grease or root problems are good reasons to consider jetting.

    While hydro jetting effectively removes existing tree roots from your sewer line, it does not prevent new roots from growing back. For a long-term solution to recurring root intrusion, we might recommend trenchless sewer repair or pipe replacement, which we can discuss during our assessment.

    Hydro jetting is very effective against a wide range of stubborn clogs that traditional snaking cannot fully clear. This includes heavy grease buildup, mineral scale, sludge, soap scum, hair clogs, and even invasive tree roots that have gotten into your sewer lines. It is designed to scour the entire pipe surface.

    HPN Plumbing backs our hydro jetting in Devils Lake, ND. We typically offer a warranty on our workmanship, with the length depending on the service and nature of the clog. We will discuss all warranty details with you before starting any work.

    Usually, a permit is not required for standard hydro jetting aimed at clearing blockages in existing lines. However, if the hydro jetting is part of a larger repair or replacement project that alters the plumbing system, a permit from the local building department may be necessary. We will tell you if a permit is needed for your situation.

    While hydro jetting is very powerful, it is generally safe for most sound plumbing systems, including PVC, cast iron, and concrete pipes. However, it is not recommended for severely damaged, corroded, or fragile pipes, which is why a pre-jetting camera inspection by HPN Plumbing is crucial to assess pipe integrity and prevent further damage.
