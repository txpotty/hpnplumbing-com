    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Devils Lake

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. Sump pumps push groundwater out to the yard, ejectors push sewage to the sewer, and only the ejector pit must be sealed and vented against gas. Never mix the two.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Devils Lake

    Grinder pumps chop waste into slurry before pumping it, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. A grinder makes sense when the sewer is distant, uphill or pressurized, such as a pressure sewer where each home pumps into a shared main. They cost more. A standard ejector often covers a basement bath.

    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in Devils Lake

    A high water alarm belongs on every ejector system, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly. Audible alarms on their own power or Wi-Fi alarms that text you give warning while the pit is still filling, not after waste reaches the floor. A working sump pump with a battery backup is important for basement homes during the spring thaw. Battery backup is an option.
