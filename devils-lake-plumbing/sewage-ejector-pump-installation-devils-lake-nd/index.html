    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Devils Lake

    While both sump pumps and sewage ejector pumps remove water, their applications are different. HPN Plumbing provides clear guidance and installation for both in Devils Lake. A sump pump handles groundwater and storm runoff, usually at a basement's lowest point to prevent flooding. A sewage ejector pump, however, is designed for wastewater containing solids from toilets, sinks and showers in below-grade plumbing. We make sure you get the correct pump for your drainage needs, preventing costly misinstallations.

HPN Plumbing can help you determine if a grinder pump is the right solution, handling the whole installation from start to finish, including the specialized electrical and discharge piping.
