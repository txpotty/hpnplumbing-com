    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Devils Lake

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. We jet the full main to the street tap. On a 4-inch line we use a heavier nozzle with more flow, which moves the loosened debris downstream instead of leaving it to settle in a low spot.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Devils Lake, ND

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. Jetting costs more up front. Because jetting removes the buildup that causes repeat clogs, many homeowners spend less over a few years than they would on repeated snaking visits for the same line.

    Storm and Yard Drain Jetting in Devils Lake, ND

    Yard drains, channel drains and downspout lines fill with sand, silt and leaves between storms. A working sump pump with a battery backup is essential for basement homes during the spring thaw. A clogged yard drain sends that water back toward the house, so we jet the lines from the outlet end and flush the catch basins before the wet season starts. Pop-up emitters get checked too.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Devils Lake, ND

    Older cast iron drains corrode and build scale on the inner wall, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. A jet clears that crust. Badly corroded cast iron with thin walls or cracks needs gentler settings or no jetting at all, and the camera tells us which before we start, along with whether the pipe is a candidate for lining.

    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Devils Lake

    Preventive jetting makes sense when the same line clogs more than once a year. Camera findings set the interval. A line with heavy grease might need jetting every six months, while a sewer with a few root entry points might only need it every 18 to 24 months, and the footage tells us which.

    Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in Devils Lake

    Jetting a broken pipe can make the break worse. We camera every line before we set up the jetter. Footage shows the material and joint condition, which sets our nozzle and pressure.

    Emergency Hydrojet Services in Devils Lake, ND

After cutting, the jet flushes the root pieces down to the main, and the camera shows which joints let the roots in so you know where a future repair or liner would go.
