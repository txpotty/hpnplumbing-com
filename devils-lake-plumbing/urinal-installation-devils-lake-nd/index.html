    Drainage and Venting for Urinals in Devils Lake, ND

    Urinal drains clog with scale, not paper. Low-flow and waterless units leave mineral buildup in the line, so proper slope and a cleanout matter. A proper vent keeps odor out. We tie in within code distance.

    Waterless Urinal Installation in Devils Lake

    Waterless urinals save the most water and need the most discipline. They use a cartridge or a layer of sealing liquid that lets urine pass while blocking sewer gas, and the cartridge has to be swapped on schedule or the restroom starts to smell. Cleaning staff need a quick lesson. We show them how.

    Old Urinal Removal and Replacement in Devils Lake

    Old urinals come off harder than they went on. Rusted bolts and seized valves often have to be cut, so we protect the tile while working. New gaskets always go in. The flange gets cleaned to bare metal.

    Urinal Permits and Compliance in Devils Lake, ND

    New urinals usually need a permit. New fixtures mean rough-in and final inspections, and commercial jobs often need plans. We pull the permit. Like-for-like swaps may not need one.

    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Devils Lake, ND

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. Tankless flushometers deliver a set volume from a 3/4 inch line and recover instantly for the next user. We set the volume to the bowl. Wrong settings stain bowls.

    Waterless Urinal Upgrades in Devils Lake
