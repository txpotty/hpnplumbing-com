    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Dickinson

    The cost for water heater expansion tank installation in Dickinson generally ranges from $250 to $500, including parts and labor. This can vary based on the tank size, the complexity of the installation and any necessary plumbing changes. We provide transparent, upfront pricing after an on-site assessment.

    Typically, installing a water heater expansion tank takes about 1 to 2 hours. This includes assessment, preparation, installation and testing. Our experienced plumbers work efficiently to minimize any disruption to your hot water supply.

    When water heats up, it expands. Without an expansion tank, this expanded water has nowhere to go, increasing pressure within your closed plumbing system. This constant pressure can prematurely wear out water heater components and relief valves and even cause leaks or ruptures. The expansion tank absorbs this excess volume, protecting your whole system.

    Signs of a failing expansion tank include water dripping from the tank's air valve, rust or corrosion on the tank's exterior or a water heater that frequently cycles on and off. You might also notice fluctuating water pressure or a banging sound in your pipes, which points to excessive pressure buildup.

    We install potable-rated tanks from Watts, Amtrol and A.O. Smith. Each is approved for drinking water and made with corrosion-resistant connections.
