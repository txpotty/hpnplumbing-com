    Bathroom Sink Drain Replacement in Dickinson, ND

    Bathroom drains wear out at the pop-up. The pivot nut packing leaks, the drain body corrodes around the overflow holes, and the stopper stops sealing, so a sink that will not hold water usually needs a whole new pop-up assembly. Half-fixes do not hold here. The new drain matches your faucet finish.

    Push-Button Drain Installation in Dickinson, ND

    Push-button drains get rid of the lift rod. A press on the cap opens and closes them, and a slotted body only goes in sinks with an overflow. Cleaning is simpler with no linkage. Hair still collects, so pull the cap now and then.

    P-Trap Replacement in Dickinson

    Thin chrome traps are the ones we replace most. They rust from the inside, the plating flakes off, and a slip nut that will not loosen often twists the whole trap apart, so we cut it out and install heavy-wall PVC or polished brass that matches the room. Then it gets a full flow test. Total time is often under an hour.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Replacement in Dickinson

    Kitchen drains start failing at the basket strainer. The putty under a 3-1/2 inch strainer dries out, the locknut rusts, and water seeps down the outside of the tailpiece where it looks like the trap is leaking even when it is not. We replace the strainer and trap together. Disposal sinks get a new flange instead.

    Code Updates During Drain Replacement in Dickinson

    Old drains sometimes break current rules. S-traps and drum traps were common decades ago, and when we replace the drain we bring it up to a vented P-trap with the right trap arm length. That can mean an air admittance valve. Local rules decide.

    Utility and Basement Sink Drain Replacement in Dickinson

    Basement sinks often drain to a pump. Sinks below the sewer line drain through a pump, so we check its valve and discharge line during the job. Pumps fail quietly. A gurgling pit is a clue.

    Corroded Drain Pipe Replacement in Dickinson, ND

    Galvanized drain arms rust from the inside. An old steel stub can crumble at the threads, so we cut it back and join new PVC with a shielded coupling. Cast iron gets the same treatment. Sometimes that means opening the wall.

    Trap Arm and Wall Drain Repair in Dickinson, ND

    Leaks right at the wall point to the trap arm or stub. A cracked arm or rusted stub drips down the back panel, out of sight, until the wood swells or smells. The bad section gets cut back. Then new PVC gets tied in.
