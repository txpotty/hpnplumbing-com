    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Dickinson

    Tankless units are particularly sensitive to hard water scale, which clogs the heat exchanger and triggers error codes. We isolate the unit with its service valves and circulate a descaling solution with a pump for the recommended time. We also clean the inlet water filter and check the unit's error history. Most manufacturers recommend descaling at least once a year in hard water areas. Regular service keeps flow rates and temperatures consistent.

    Thermostat and Heating Element Checks in Dickinson

    For electric water heaters, we test the thermostat and heating elements to make sure they work correctly and hold an accurate temperature. Faulty elements or thermostats can lead to too little hot water or wasted energy use. For gas water heaters, we inspect the burner assembly and pilot light. Big swings between summer heat and winter cold make pipes expand and contract, which slowly loosens fittings over the years. Properly working components are vital for both efficiency and safety.

    Emergency Water Heater Inspection in Dickinson, ND

    If your water heater is acting up and you're unsure why, our emergency inspection service in Dickinson, ND can diagnose the problem fast. While not a full maintenance visit, we can identify issues like strange noises, sudden temperature drops, or leaks that point to an immediate need for repair. We'll then provide an straight assessment and options for repair or replacement.

    T&P Valve and Safety Testing in Dickinson, ND

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building dangerous pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets replaced. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge if you have one.

    Attic and Garage Water Heater Maintenance in Dickinson

    Many homes have water heaters in garages or attics, where heat, cold and tight access add stress to the unit. Attic units need working drain pans and drain lines to prevent ceiling damage if the tank leaks. We check the pan, drain line, venting and access platform. Garage units should be raised and protected where required for gas appliances. We make sure your setup meets local code.
