    Sewer Pipe Relining in Dickinson, ND

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair technique where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without major digging.
