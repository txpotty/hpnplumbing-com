    Frequently Asked Questions About Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in Dickinson

    The cost for electric tankless water heater installation in Dickinson, ND typically ranges from $2,500 to $6,000. This includes the unit itself, labor and any necessary electrical upgrades, which are often a significant part of the cost for electric models. Complex installations or higher-capacity units can be at the higher end of this range.

    Whole-home units can require 100 amps or more, so many homes need a panel review. We assess your panel and coordinate upgrades when required.

    HPN Plumbing stands behind our work with a full labor warranty on all electric tankless water heater installations. Additionally, the units themselves come with manufacturer warranties, typically ranging from 5 to 10 years on the heat exchanger. We'll provide full details for your particular unit.

    With proper installation and regular maintenance, an electric tankless water heater can last 20 years or more, which is significantly longer than the 10 to 15 year lifespan of a traditional tank unit. Their modular design also means components can often be replaced, extending their service life further.

    Installation requires plumbing and high-amperage electrical work. Professional installation provides safety and protects your warranty.

    Installing an electric tankless water heater is a demanding job involving high-voltage electricity and plumbing, making it unsuitable for DIY. Incorrect installation can lead to electrical hazards, water damage and voided warranties. It's always best to hire a licensed and experienced plumber like HPN Plumbing for safe and proper installation.

    Water softeners and filters are popular around Dickinson because they protect water heaters, dishwashers and faucets. Hard water can lead to mineral buildup (scale) inside electric tankless water heaters, which can reduce efficiency and lifespan.
