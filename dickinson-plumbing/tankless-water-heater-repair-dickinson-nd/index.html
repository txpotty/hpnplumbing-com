    Electric Tankless Water Heater Repair in Dickinson, ND

    Whole-house electric tankless units pull a lot of current, often 100 to 150 amps on two or three dedicated breakers. We check element resistance and supply voltage. Scorched wire gets cut back.

    Emergency Tankless Water Heater Repair in Dickinson, ND

    A tankless heater that dies on a cold morning in Dickinson is an emergency for a family of five.     Ignition Failure Repair in Dickinson, ND

    No ignition codes have four usual causes: a dirty flame rod, a bad igniter, low gas pressure or a blocked intake. The gas line is the sneaky one. A tankless burner can need 150,000 to 199,000 BTU, and the half-inch line that fed a 40,000 BTU tank often cannot carry that, which is why it fires at low flow and quits when two showers are on.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Repair in Dickinson

    Pressure at full fire matters on a gas tankless burner, not just at the moment it lights. We put a manometer on the inlet test port and run every hot tap in the house, because pressure that drops below the manufacturer's minimum under load causes flame loss and cold slugs. Then we check the exhaust. Codes come off when the numbers are right.

    Igniter & Flame Rod Repair in Dickinson, ND

    Gas tankless heaters light with a hot surface or spark igniter, then prove flame with a flame rod. A rod coated in oxide reads low on a microamp meter and the board shuts the gas valve after a few seconds. We clean or replace the rod, check the igniter for cracks or weak spark, and confirm the gas valve opens on call. Five-minute fix sometimes.

    Same-Day Tankless Repair in Dickinson

    Water dripping from inside the cabinet, or a fault you cannot reset, needs a look the same day. We bring sensors, igniters, flame rods and filters, so a good share of visits end with hot water.
