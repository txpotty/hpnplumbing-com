    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Dickinson

    Electric water heaters in Dickinson usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. A bad element can be replaced.     Gas Water Heater Replacement in Dickinson, ND

    Gas water heaters recover faster than electric, and in Dickinson, ND homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Then we leak test.
