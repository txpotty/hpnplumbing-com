    Flow Control Device Repair in Dickinson

    Hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, like 20 or 50 gallons per minute, and the flow control fitting is what holds the water to that rate. Take it out and a three-compartment sink dump blows grease right past the baffles. Inspectors look for it. We also clear the air intake, since a plugged intake on the flow control fitting lets the trap siphon.

    Commercial Grease Trap Repair in Dickinson

    Restaurants, school cafeterias, hospital kitchens and bakeries all push more grease than a small trap can hold. Undersized traps fail in predictable spots. The inlet tee clogs, the outlet tee gets coated, and grease starts showing up in the sewer manhole down the street, which is usually how the city finds out before the owner does. We fix the failed parts and tell you plainly if the trap is too small for the kitchen.

    Lid, Gasket and Riser Repair in Dickinson, ND

    Outdoor risers take a beating from garbage trucks and the occasional forklift. Once a riser cracks, rainwater and grit wash straight into the tank. We replace damaged risers and set traffic-rated covers, H-20 rated where vehicles cross, and seal the riser joints with butyl rope so the tank stays tight. A light-duty lid in a drive aisle will break eventually.

    Preventative Grease Trap Maintenance & Repair in Dickinson, ND

    Most trap repairs we do trace back to pumping intervals that stretched too long. Pretreatment programs commonly set the limit at 25 percent of the liquid depth for grease and solids combined. After a repair, we measure the grease cap and solids with a sludge judge and suggest a pumping schedule based on how fast your trap actually fills. Busy fryers fill a trap fast.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Dickinson

    Inside a concrete gravity interceptor the baffle is usually a poured or precast wall with a crossover opening. A crumbling wall lets grease slip into the second compartment and out the outlet. We can rebuild the wall with form work and a sulfate-resistant mix or set a polyethylene baffle panel, depending on the tank. Both require a full pump-out and a dry tank.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Dickinson

    A cable machine punches a hole through grease. A jetter scrubs the whole pipe wall instead. We run a commercial jetter at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI through the line feeding the trap and the line leaving it, working from the cleanout toward the main. On old cast iron we lower the pressure and watch the camera so we do not open up a thin spot.
