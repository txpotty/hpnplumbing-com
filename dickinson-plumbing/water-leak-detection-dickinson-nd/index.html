    Pinhole Leak Detection in Dickinson, ND

    Pinhole leaks, often found in copper or PEX piping, are small but can cause significant damage over time, especially behind walls or under floors. We use non-invasive moisture meters to detect elevated humidity levels and thermal cameras to identify cold spots that indicate water. Our plumbers are skilled at tracing these elusive leaks without tearing apart your entire home. Swift action on pinhole leaks prevents extensive drywall damage, mold growth and compromised structural integrity.     Slab Leak Detection in Dickinson

    Slab leaks are common in homes on shifting soil and moving foundations. These leaks occur in pipes beneath your concrete slab, often indicated by warm spots on the floor, sudden spikes in your water bill or the sound of running water when all fixtures are off. HPN Plumbing uses dedicated acoustic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to precisely locate these hidden leaks without destructive guesswork. Early detection is essential to preventing major foundation damage.

    Water Meter Leak Testing in Dickinson

    The water meter is the first and easiest leak test. With every fixture and appliance off, a moving leak indicator means water is escaping somewhere.
