    Sewer Line Video Inspection in Dickinson

    Before any trenchless repair, we perform a thorough video inspection of your sewer line.     Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Dickinson, ND

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is particularly useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add decades of service.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Dickinson, ND

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is well suited for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a fast and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by North Dakota homeowners.

    Sectional Point Repair in Dickinson

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are common on otherwise healthy lines.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Dickinson, ND

    Tree and shrub roots go after moisture in sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense masses that cause repeat backups. We cut roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro jet, then line the pipe to seal the joints. That breaks the cycle of cleaning the line every few months.

    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Dickinson, ND

    Sewer lines in Dickinson, ND can suffer damage from several factors, including shifting soils that cause pipes to crack or separate. Frost heave can shift soil around sewer lines and crack joints, which often shows up as slow drains or backups. Tree root intrusion is another major cause, as roots seek out moisture in pipes and grow, causing blockages and structural damage. Age and corrosion, especially in older cast iron or clay pipes, also add to deterioration. Our trenchless solutions address these common issues effectively.

    Trenchless Drain Pipe Repair in Dickinson, ND

    Beyond main sewer lines, trenchless techniques can also be applied to repair damaged drain pipes on your property. Whether it is a cracked kitchen drain or a clogged laundry line, we can often reline or burst smaller diameter pipes without tearing up your walls or floors in Dickinson, ND. This preserves your home's interior and minimizes renovation costs.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Dickinson

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot penetrate. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their form.
