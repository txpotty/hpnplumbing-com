    Strong storms can knock out power right when your pump is needed most. Battery backup pumps take over automatically when the main pump fails or power goes out.     New Sump Pit Installation in Dickinson

    If your basement or crawl space has no pit, we can create one. We cut the slab, excavate through soil, compacted clay or rock and set a durable basin. We add gravel around the basin to help water flow in. Then we patch the concrete and install the pump and cover. A new pit gives you a dependable place to collect and remove water.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Dickinson, ND

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to reach for maintenance and repair.
