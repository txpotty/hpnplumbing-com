    Cracked Bowl or Tank Replacement in Dickinson

    Sometimes only the tank is cracked, and if the model is still in production, a matching tank costs less than a whole new toilet. We check the model number first. A cracked bowl always means a full replacement, since the bowl carries the seal to the floor and cannot be patched.

    Gravity-Flush Toilet Replacement in Dickinson

    Gravity-flush toilets are the simplest and most forgiving, and a well-made one with a 3 inch flush valve will outflush most of the older toilets it replaces. Parts are easy to find. If the floor is uneven, we shim it level, then caulk the base and test several full flushes.

    Rental and Multi-Unit Toilet Replacement in Dickinson, ND

    Landlords with several units do better standardizing on one toilet model with common parts, because the next flapper or fill valve is then the same part in every unit. We can replace several toilets in one visit. Tenants keep a working bathroom. We coordinate entry directly with them, so you do not have to be there.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Dickinson

    Chair height toilets put the seat about 17 to 19 inches off the floor, roughly two inches higher than a standard bowl, and that makes a real difference for knees and hips. The drain stays the same. Many people pair it with an elongated bowl and a soft-close seat, and the swap takes the same couple of hours as any other.

    Replacing Pressure-Assisted Toilets in Dickinson, ND

    Pressure-assisted toilets use a sealed vessel in the tank that stores air under line pressure and pushes water through the bowl hard, which helps in houses with long, low-slope drain runs. They are loud. When we replace one, we pick a vessel brand with parts that are easy to get later, and we check that house pressure is in the range the unit needs.

    Water-Saving Toilet Upgrades in Dickinson, ND

    Older toilets are one of the largest indoor water users in a house, and replacing a 3.5 gallon model with a 1.28 gallon one cuts toilet water use by more than half. Dual-flush toilets add a lighter flush for liquids. Our recommendations lean on high MaP scores so the savings do not come with double flushing.

    Emergency Toilet Replacement in Dickinson

    A toilet that cracked overnight and is still seeping into the floor cannot wait for next week, so we bring a standard round and elongated model on emergency calls. First, close the valve behind the toilet. Most of the time the new toilet goes in that same day, and we check the subfloor for soaked plywood before setting it.

    Standard Toilet Replacement in Dickinson

    Two-piece toilets are what most houses have, and after twenty or thirty years the tank bolts corrode, the porcelain gets crazed and the flush never quite clears the bowl. At that point a swap makes more sense than another repair. We pull the old toilet, scrape the flange and check it for cracks, then set the new one on a fresh wax ring with new closet bolts.
