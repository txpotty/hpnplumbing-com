    Annual Sump Pump Check-ups in Dickinson

    Regular annual maintenance is key for the longevity and reliability of your sump pump. Our technicians perform a thorough inspection, checking the motor, impeller, float switch and discharge lines for any signs of wear or blockage. We also test the system's operation by filling the pit with water to confirm it activates correctly and pumps water away efficiently, protecting your Dickinson home from unexpected flooding.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Dickinson

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to premature wear. We make sure these vital parts are functioning correctly to maintain an effective water removal system for your Dickinson residence.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Dickinson, ND

    A power outage during a heavy storm can render your primary sump pump useless. Our maintenance service includes thorough testing of your battery backup sump pump system. We check the battery charge, verify the charger's function and confirm the backup pump activates when the main power is interrupted. This dual protection is vital for homes in Dickinson, ND where power interruptions can coincide with severe weather.
