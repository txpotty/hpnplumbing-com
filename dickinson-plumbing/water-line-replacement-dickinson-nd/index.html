    Main Water Line Replacement in Dickinson

    The main water line is the primary pipe delivering water from the municipal supply to your home. When this critical line fails due to age, shifting soil, or severe leaks, a full replacement is often the most cost-effective long-term solution. We expertly excavate, remove the old line, and install new, durable piping, ensuring proper connection to your home's plumbing system and the city's main.

    PEX Water Line Replacement in Dickinson

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a popular and cost-effective choice for water line replacement in Dickinson. It's flexible, resistant to freezing, and less prone to scaling and corrosion than some traditional materials. Its flexibility allows for simpler installation, often requiring fewer connections, which reduces the potential for leaks. PEX is also a good option for homes with corrosive soil conditions, offering a reliable alternative to metal pipes.

    Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Dickinson

    Deciding between repair and full replacement depends on the extent of the damage. If your main water line in Dickinson has multiple leaks, severe corrosion, or is nearing the end of its lifespan, replacement is often the more cost-effective long-term solution. Our technicians will perform a complete inspection, including pressure testing and possibly video inspection, to recommend the best course of action. We consider factors like the age of your home, the material of the existing pipe, and the nature of the damage before making a recommendation.

    Copper Water Line Installation in Dickinson

    Copper has long been a popular material for water lines due to its durability, corrosion resistance, and longevity. HPN Plumbing specializes in installing new copper water lines, known for their ability to withstand high water pressure and limit bacterial growth. While a higher upfront investment, copper provides a reliable and long-lasting water supply system, a great choice for many homes in Dickinson, ND.

    Galvanized Main Line Replacement in Dickinson

    Galvanized steel pipe was common in homes built before the 1970s. Over time, it corrodes inside and narrows the pipe. This causes low pressure and discolored water. We replace galvanized lines with modern PEX or copper. The difference in water pressure is often noticeable right away.

    Traditional Water Line Excavation in Dickinson

    Sometimes, traditional open-trench excavation is the most practical method for water line replacement in Dickinson, especially for severe damage, complex pipe layouts, or when the existing pipe material is incompatible with trenchless techniques. Our team carefully excavates the area, removes the old pipe, and installs a new, high-quality main water line, typically using copper or PEX. We ensure proper grading and compaction of the soil during backfilling to avoid future issues.
