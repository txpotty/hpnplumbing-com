    Copper to PEX Conversions in West Fargo

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Many rural homes get water from private wells or rural water systems, and iron and sulfur odors are common complaints. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more often follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and lowers the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

    PEX-A Expansion Fitting Systems in West Fargo, ND

    PEX-A, such as Uponor, uses an expansion tool to stretch the pipe and a ring over the fitting, which then shrinks back into a secure seal. The fittings have a larger inner diameter, so flow restriction is lower. PEX-A is also more flexible and can be repaired from kinks with a heat gun. It is our preferred choice for many full repipes.

    PEX Repipe Cost Comparison in West Fargo, ND

    PEX repipes typically cost less than copper repipes because the material is less expensive and installs faster. The total depends on the number of bathrooms, the home's size and how accessible the attic and walls are. Drywall patching and texture matching are often included or quoted separately. We give you a detailed estimate so you can compare PEX-A, PEX-B and copper options side by side.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in West Fargo, ND

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, much like a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when multiple fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to weigh cost and performance.
