    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in West Fargo, ND

    The service line from the meter to the house can leak for months without surfacing, especially in old galvanized pipe. Green grass in a dry spell is a clue.     Main Water Line Leak Detection in West Fargo

    Soggy grass near the front walk or a sinking spot over the line can mean the service pipe is leaking. Old galvanized and copper lines fail most. We map the pipe with a line tracer and listen for the leak with a ground microphone, which keeps the dig to a single hole rather than a trench. Then we patch or replace.

    PEX and Copper Leak Detection in West Fargo, ND

    Copper leaks are loud on a ground microphone. PEX is quieter, since plastic pipe does not carry sound well, so we lean more on thermal scans, moisture readings and tracer gas when the house is plumbed in it. Both show up on our schedule every week. Pinholes in copper and failed crimp fittings in PEX are the two leaks we find most.
