    What Our Repipe Crews Do in West Fargo, ND

    Type L Copper Repiping in West Fargo

    Copper has been plumbing homes for generations, and done right it lasts a long time. We use Type L, not the thinner Type M, and we support it with the right hangers so it does not rub or rattle in the walls. Lead-free solder only. Because some water chemistries eat copper, we look at your leak history before recommending it over PEX.

    Drain Line Repiping in West Fargo

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Repeat clogs, sewer smells and several slow drains at once often mean the drain pipe itself is failing. We camera the lines first. New PVC or ABS, sloped and vented properly, replaces the bad sections and drains better than the old pipe.

    Commercial Repiping in West Fargo, ND

    Commercial buildings in West Fargo, ND often have larger pipe, risers that serve several floors and tenants who cannot lose water for a full day. We phase commercial repipes by floor, suite or riser so most of the building stays in service while we work. Night work is possible. We use Type L copper or commercial-grade PEX sized from fixture units and installed to commercial code.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in West Fargo, ND

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Flexible PEX soaks up some of the pressure spike behind water hammer, and its long fitting-free runs leave fewer weak points. Attic PEX gets kept away from flues and out of UV.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in West Fargo

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. Type L tolerates UV, stays rigid and has decades of field history. It costs more. We sweat joints with lead-free solder or press them, hang the pipe at proper support intervals and test the whole system before patching.

    Drywall Patching and Restoration in West Fargo, ND

    Repiping needs small openings in walls and ceilings to reach fixtures and pull pipe. We cut clean, square openings, protect the area around them and patch every hole once the plumbing passes its test. We match the texture. Knockdown, orange peel and smooth finishes are all routine, and your walls are ready for primer and paint when we leave.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in West Fargo, ND

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Brown water after the house sits overnight, weak flow at upstairs showers and leaks at threaded joints are the usual signs that galvanized is near the end. We replace it with PEX or copper. Most homeowners notice the pressure difference the first time they turn on the shower.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in West Fargo

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water and long attic runs. Our crews use Uponor or similar PEX-A, where the pipe end gets expanded and shrinks back over the fitting for a full-diameter connection. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. Two or three days covers most houses.
