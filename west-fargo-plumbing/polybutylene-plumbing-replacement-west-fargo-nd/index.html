    Frequently Asked Questions About Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in West Fargo

    Most PEX replacements of polybutylene cost $5,000 to $12,000 for an average home. Copper costs more, and yard service lines often add $1,500 to $4,000.

    Gray, blue or black plastic pipe marked PB2110 at the water heater or under sinks is the clearest sign. The build year matters. If your home was built between about 1978 and 1995, has this pipe and has had even one leak at a fitting, replacement is strongly recommended before the next one.

    A full poly repipe in West Fargo, ND usually takes three to seven days. Bigger homes take longer. We give you a clear day-by-day timeline at the estimate, including which rooms we will work in each day and when the drywall finish will happen.

    We commonly use Uponor PEX-A and Type L copper with Viega ProPress or soldered fittings. We recommend materials based on your home and water conditions.

    For PEX, we use trusted brands like Uponor, Viega and Zurn. We use Uponor most often. Copper replacements use Type L instead of thin Type M, since the heavier wall lasts longer.

    Every poly replacement we do comes with a written warranty on materials and labor. PEX makers add their own. Both warranties come in writing with the photos and permit record, all in one place for later.

    Water softeners and filters are popular around West Fargo because they extend the life of water heaters, dishwashers and faucets. Hard water does not cause poly to fail the way chlorine does.
