    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in West Fargo

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Basements are standard in most homes, and many rely on sump pumps to manage groundwater during wet springs. We confirm it with a meter test and pressure test, then use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to pinpoint the leak within a foot or two. Then we decide. A spot repair through the floor makes sense for some, and an overhead reroute makes more sense for others. We price both so you can compare.

    Backflow Assembly Testing and Rebuilds in West Fargo

    Most water providers require annual tests on commercial backflow assemblies, and many send late notices that come with fines. Test early. We test reduced pressure zone, double check and pressure vacuum breaker assemblies with gauges calibrated every year and submit the results online or on paper, whichever your provider uses. Failed devices get rebuilt on the spot when we have the kit.

    Commercial Sewer Line Repair in West Fargo, ND

    The building sewer on a commercial property carries everything from every tenant, so a root intrusion or a belly in the line affects the whole property. We camera the line, locate the problem depth with a sonde and then decide between a spot repair, pipe lining or pipe bursting. Trenchless saves parking lots. Lining a 6-inch lateral can often be done in a day without tearing up asphalt or closing an entrance.

    Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in West Fargo

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. We test RPZ, double check and PVB assemblies with calibrated gauges and send the results straight to the water provider. Failed devices usually need a rebuild kit, not a new valve. We carry Watts, Wilkins and Febco kits for that reason.

    Grease Trap Installation in West Fargo

    A new restaurant or a kitchen expansion usually needs a grease interceptor sized from the fixtures that drain to it. Under-sink traps work for small kitchens, while larger operations need in-ground interceptors of 500 gallons or more. Sizing is set by code. We work from your kitchen plan, set the unit at the right depth and vent it so it passes the pretreatment inspection on the first try.

    Commercial Water Heater Repair and Installation in West Fargo, ND

    A commercial water heater is judged by recovery rate, the gallons it can reheat per hour, not by tank size alone. Undersized heaters run cold by mid-shift. Our techs work on AO Smith, Rheem, Bradford White and State commercial heaters and check flue draft, gas valves and the T and P valve each visit. For a replacement, we size from fixture count and peak demand. Proper sizing is what keeps a busy kitchen in hot water all night.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in West Fargo

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. Our tester opens each test cock in order, records the check valve and relief valve readings on a calibrated gauge and compares them against the pass limits printed on the test form. Passing devices get tagged. The provider gets the report from us, and we keep a copy for your next test.
