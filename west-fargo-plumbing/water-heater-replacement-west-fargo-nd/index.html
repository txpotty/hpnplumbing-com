    Gas Water Heater Replacement in West Fargo, ND

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in West Fargo, ND homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes.     Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in West Fargo

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is a popular move, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. Since tankless can pull four times the gas of a tank, the meter line usually gets bigger, and the old vent is swapped for stainless or PVC. You see both numbers.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in West Fargo, ND

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.

    Water Heater Replacement Cost Comparison in West Fargo, ND

We price all three.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in West Fargo

    Electric water heaters in West Fargo usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. Elements are cheap to swap. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Most swaps take a few hours.
