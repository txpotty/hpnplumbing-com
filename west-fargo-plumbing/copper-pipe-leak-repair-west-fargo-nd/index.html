    Pinhole Leak Repair in West Fargo

    Pinholes start inside the pipe where the copper wall pits and thins. You see a drip or a stain. They are small and easy to miss behind drywall, so we locate them with a thermal camera and moisture meter before cutting a single opening in the wall or ceiling. We replace the pitted length with Type L. We look at the copper around it.

    Underground Copper Pipe Leak Repair in West Fargo

    Buried copper service lines can leak for months before anyone notices. Bills tell the story. We locate the leak with acoustic gear and tracer gas, and either spot repair it or swap in a new service line with minimal digging. Every repair gets a pressure test.

    Under Slab Copper Leak Repair in West Fargo, ND

    Copper lines under a slab can wear through where they rub concrete or gravel. Warm floors are a clue. We isolate hot and cold lines with pressure tests and locate the leak with acoustic and thermal equipment, then choose between a small slab cut at the leak and rerouting the line through the walls or attic. Both options get a price.

    Burst Copper Pipe Repair in West Fargo, ND

    Copper in an exterior wall or attic can split when the water inside freezes. Homeowners around West Fargo should insulate exposed pipes, drain outdoor spigots and keep the heat on when away in winter. Shut the main first. The split often does not leak until the ice thaws, so a pipe that froze overnight may start spraying the next afternoon once the house warms up. We cut out the split and solder in new pipe. Insulation goes on after.

    Solder Joint Leak Repair in West Fargo, ND

    Older solder joints can fail from poor original work, thermal expansion or vibration. Look for white or green crust. A leaking joint usually weeps for a while before it drips, so we either resolder it or replace the fitting and a short length of pipe. Then we pressure test it.

    Copper Fitting and Valve Repair in West Fargo

    Angle stops, shutoff valves and copper fittings under sinks and behind toilets can weep or seize. Scale locks them up. Hard water buildup inside a valve can keep it from closing when you need it most, so we replace failing valves and fittings with quarter-turn ball valves that shut off fully and are easy to turn. We test each one.

    Preventive Copper Pipe Inspection in West Fargo, ND

    An inspection catches thin spots before they leak. It takes about an hour. We look at exposed copper for green crust and pitting, test static pressure, check the bond wire at the water heater and main, and run a thermal scan on walls where pipe runs if the house has had leaks before. You get a written summary.

We price each option.
