    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in West Fargo

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. Frost goes deep into the ground here, so water and sewer lines must be buried well below the surface to avoid freezing. Sun breaks down plastic traps and supply lines left outside, and yard dirt and grass clippings clog the drain faster than kitchen scraps ever do. Hose bib leaks are part of the same call. A frost-free sillcock is worth it on exposed walls.

    Office and Restaurant Sink Repair in West Fargo

    Office breakroom sinks get abused in ways a home sink never sees. Coffee grounds and paper towels go down all day, and nobody checks the trap until water is on the carpet. A strainer basket goes in after we clear it. For restaurants, we keep three-bowl sinks running between the lunch and dinner rush.

    Pop-Up Stopper Repair in West Fargo, ND

    Stoppers that will not hold water have a linkage problem. Either the clevis slipped on the lift rod or hair has wrapped the stopper so it will not seat. Half the time, a cleaning does it. The other half get a new pop-up drain in a matching finish.

    Kitchen Sink Repair in West Fargo

    Kitchen sinks take the most abuse in the house. Strainers lose their putty seal, sprayer hoses split inside the cabinet, and disposal flanges vibrate loose over the years. The wet part is the one we fix. A cracked composite or porcelain bowl is a replacement, and we will say so.

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in West Fargo, ND

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. In a warm cabinet that core can fail after five to ten years and burst with no warning, and the multi-turn stop above it often will not close when you need it. We swap both in one visit. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Bathroom Sink Repair in West Fargo, ND

    Bath sinks have small parts that wear. The pop-up linkage sits in a damp cabinet for years, and the pivot nut where the rod enters the drain is the most common leak we find on a bathroom sink. Pedestal and wall-hung sinks get the same checks. Vanity sinks just hide the leak better.

    Sink Parts Replacement in West Fargo, ND

    Most sinks do not need replacing. A new tailpiece or pop-up assembly costs a fraction of a new sink and installs in an hour, and the bowl and faucet you already like stay right where they are. Finishes get matched when the part exists. Discontinued faucets sometimes force a full faucet swap.

    Garbage Disposal Repair in West Fargo

    A disposal that hums but will not spin is usually jammed. From below we turn the flywheel with a 1/4 inch hex key, hit the reset button and look for a bent impeller. A leaking unit is another matter. A cracked housing means a new disposal, usually an InSinkErator or a Waste King.
