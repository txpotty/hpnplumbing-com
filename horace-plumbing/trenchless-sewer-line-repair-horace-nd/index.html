    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Horace, ND

    Shifting soil is one of the main reasons sewer joints pull apart or crack. Frost heave can shift soil around sewer lines and misalign joints, which often shows up as slow drains or backups. Roots are the other big one, since a hairline gap at a clay joint is enough for a feeder root to get in and then swell into a mat that catches paper. Then age finishes the job. Cast iron scales and thins, and clay bells crack under load.

    Trenchless vs Traditional Sewer Repair Pricing in Horace, ND

    Compare total cost, not just the plumbing line on the invoice. An open-trench replacement can look cheaper on paper, until you add saw-cutting the driveway and pouring new concrete over a trench that may run 50 feet. Trenchless avoids most of that. Sod and sprinkler repair add up too.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Horace, ND

    CIPP stands for cured-in-place pipe. We wet out a felt tube with epoxy resin, invert it into the cleaned line with air pressure, and hold it against the old pipe wall until the resin hardens. Ambient cure takes a few hours, and hot water or steam shortens it. The finished liner seals cracks and leaking joints in the original pipe.
