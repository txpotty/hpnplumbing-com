    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Horace

    CIPP lining is a popular trenchless method where we insert a resin-saturated felt tube into the existing damaged pipe.     Pipe Bursting Trenchless Repair in Horace, ND

    Pipe bursting is another trenchless method used for replacing severely damaged pipes. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward at the same time. This method is well suited for completely replacing a pipe segment with a new, stronger material, such as HDPE. It's often chosen when the existing pipe is collapsed or too damaged for lining. HPN Plumbing uses specialized bursting heads and winches for a smooth and efficient replacement, reducing the overall project time and cost for homeowners in Horace, ND.
