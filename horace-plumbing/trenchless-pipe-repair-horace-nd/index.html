    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Horace

    A CIPP liner is a felt tube soaked in resin. It hardens inside the old pipe. We pull or invert the tube into the cleaned line, inflate it against the pipe wall and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light, which leaves a jointless pipe inside the old one. It fixes cracks, breaks and roots without digging up your Horace yard.

    Lateral and Spot Lining in Horace

    Not every pipe needs lining end to end. Short damage gets a short liner. A sectional liner is cheaper and quicker for a short stretch of damage, though we camera the rest of the line first to be sure it is sound.
