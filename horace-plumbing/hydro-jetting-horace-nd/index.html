    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Horace

    Residential hydro jetting in Horace typically costs $350 to $800, depending on line length, access and how heavy the buildup is. Commercial jetting is often priced by the hour and may run higher.

    HPN Plumbing uses professional-grade hydro jetting equipment from established manufacturers like Spartan Tool and MyTana. These brands are known for their reliability, powerful performance, and safety features, so we can deliver the most thorough cleaning for your pipes.

    Most residential jobs take 1 to 3 hours including inspection. Long commercial lines or heavy grease can take longer.

    Small consumer jetters exist, but they lack the pressure and flow of professional units. High-pressure water can also injure untrained users and harm fragile pipe.

    No permit is needed for jetting. If the inspection shows damage that needs repair, permits may be required and we handle them.

    Repeat clogs in the same line, slow drains after snaking, sewer odors and known grease or root problems are good reasons to consider jetting.

    Generally, a permit is not required for standard hydro jetting aimed at clearing blockages in existing lines.
