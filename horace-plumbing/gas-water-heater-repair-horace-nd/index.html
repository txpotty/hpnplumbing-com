    Pilot Light & Burner Issues in Horace

    A frequent problem with gas water heaters is a pilot light that won't stay lit or a burner that fails to ignite. This can be caused by a faulty thermocouple, a dirty pilot tube or issues with the gas control valve. Our technicians in Horace are skilled at diagnosing these components and performing the necessary repairs, whether it is cleaning, adjusting or replacing parts. We ensure proper gas flow and ignition for consistent hot water.

    Burner Assembly and Venting System Repair in Horace

    A malfunctioning burner assembly can result in insufficient heating or unusual noises. We carefully inspect the burner for blockages, corrosion or wear. We also check the venting system for proper draft and obstructions, which is essential for safe operation and preventing carbon monoxide buildup in your Horace home. Proper venting is a key safety consideration.

    Gas Control Valve Replacement in Horace, ND

    The gas control valve regulates temperature and shuts off gas if the pilot goes out. Newer valves from Honeywell and White-Rodgers use status lights that blink codes when something fails. If the valve itself has failed, we replace it with an exact match or approved universal model. We leak test and confirm temperature control afterward.

    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Horace, ND

    A yellow, lazy or noisy flame points to a dirty burner or poor combustion air. We remove and clean the burner, check the orifice size for natural gas or propane and adjust as needed. A clean blue flame heats more quickly and produces less carbon monoxide. We also check for rust flakes falling from the flue.

    Water Heater Tank Leaks and Pressure Issues in Horace, ND

    While gas water heaters are robust, tank leaks or pressure problems can occur. We can often fix small fitting leaks or replace relief valves if pressure is too high. If the tank itself is compromised due to rust or old age, a replacement might be the more economical long-term solution. We give you an honest assessment of the repair options for your Horace, ND home.

    Gas Water Heater Noise Troubleshooting in Horace

    Odd noises coming from your gas water heater, such as rumbling, popping or whistling, often indicate sediment buildup in the tank or a burner problem. Hard water can add to sediment accumulation. Our repair service includes flushing the tank and inspecting for sediment, addressing pressure issues or checking other components that might be causing the noise, restoring efficiency and quiet operation.

    Propane Water Heater Repair in Horace

    Rural properties often run water heaters on propane instead of natural gas. Propane units use different orifices and regulator pressures, and improper conversions cause sooting and poor performance. We confirm the correct parts are installed and repair pilots, valves and burners. We also check tank regulators for correct pressure.
