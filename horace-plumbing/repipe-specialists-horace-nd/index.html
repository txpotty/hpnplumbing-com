    Slab Leak Reroutes in Horace

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab, which costs far less than a repipe and avoids breaking the floor. It suits a first-time leak. After multiple leaks, we will show you why a full repipe may cost less over the next ten years.

    Drain Line Repiping in Horace

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Slow drains in several fixtures, sewer odors and repeated clogs in the same line are common signs of a failing drain system. We camera the lines first. Failing sections are replaced with PVC or ABS sloped and vented to code, which drains faster and resists buildup much better than the pipe it replaces.

    Emergency Pipe Failures and Repipes in Horace

    Sometimes a house reaches the repipe point all at once, with a burst line and two more leaks right behind it.     Drywall Patching and Restoration in Horace, ND

    Condo, Townhome and Multi-Family Repipes in Horace

    Shared walls and HOA rules make repiping attached homes more involved.     PEX Repiping in Horace, ND

    PEX is flexible plastic tubing that can run long distances without a fitting, which means fewer joints hidden in your walls. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. It resists scale and chlorine, handles a freeze better than copper and costs less to install, which is why most of our repipes use it. We use PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe open. Flow stays strong at the last shower.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Horace

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. It holds up well, does not mind sunlight in an exposed run and has a long track record behind it. It costs more. Our plumbers solder joints with lead-free solder or use press fittings, support the pipe at the spacing the code calls for and pressure test the system before any wall is closed.
