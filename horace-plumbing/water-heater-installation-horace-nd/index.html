    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Horace, ND

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match voltage and breaker size to the panel, and since there is no vent, an electric tank can sit in spots a gas unit cannot. Closets and attics get a pan. Quiet and simple.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Horace

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Every job gets a fresh connector, a drip leg, a shutoff and a vent connector with the right rise toward the chimney or flue. Then a draft test. Gas suits busy households.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Horace

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. North Dakota enforces a statewide plumbing code based on the Uniform Plumbing Code, and plumbers must hold a state license. A small expansion tank on the cold line absorbs that extra volume, which keeps the relief valve from weeping and saves wear on faucets and fittings. We set the air charge to match your water pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Gas tankless models usually need a bigger gas line than the old tank had, plus a dedicated vent run through the wall or roof. Upfront cost is higher. We check gas capacity before quoting.
