    Waterproofing Costs vs. Repeat Water Damage in Horace, ND

    Waterproofing a shower or wall costs much less than repeated drywall, cabinet and flooring repairs. Ignoring moisture also invites mold, which can be expensive to remediate. A typical shower wall waterproofing project falls in the low thousands, while exterior block sealing varies with wall size. We give you a written quote so you can compare options side by side.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Horace

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall. We remove failing tile, install cement board or foam board and apply a membrane such as Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban. Seams, corners and niches get extra attention before new tile is set.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Horace, ND

    A cracked or poorly sloped shower pan often causes wet baseboards and stained ceilings below. We test the pan by plugging the drain and flooding it to confirm the leak. Then we rebuild the pan with correct pre-slope, a new liner or bonded membrane and a properly clamped drain. A second flood test proves the pan is watertight before tile.

    Waterproofing for Older Homes in Horace, ND

    Older homes, especially those built before modern waterproofing standards, are particularly prone to water intrusion. Older homes may still have galvanized supply lines or cast iron drains, which can rust and restrict flow with age. Many older foundations may lack adequate exterior drainage or proper sealing. We specialize in assessing and upgrading waterproofing systems for these properties, often combining exterior excavation with interior drainage solutions. We take care to respect the existing structure while installing modern, effective waterproofing, with attention to the challenges of older brick and stone foundations.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Horace

    A slow drip from a shower valve, supply stub or drain fitting can soak a wall for months before you notice. We locate the leak with moisture meters, thermal imaging and pressure tests. The damaged section of pipe or valve is replaced, and wet drywall is cut out and dried. Once the cavity is dry, we restore the wall and seal any wet-area surfaces.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Horace

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most effective method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Frost heave can shift soil around sewer lines and misalign joints, which often shows up as slow drains or backups. Proper drainage solutions, such as French drains, are also added to divert water away.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Horace

    Even small cracks in your foundation walls can allow water to seep into your basement or crawl space. Our walls waterproofing service includes foundation crack repair using specialized epoxy or polyurethane injection techniques. These materials fill and seal cracks, preventing future water entry and often strengthening the foundation. Addressing cracks early in Horace homes is important to avoid larger, more costly structural issues.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in Horace

    The cost of walls waterproofing in Horace, ND can vary a great deal based on the method used, the extent of the damage, and the size of your foundation. Basic crack sealing might range from $300 to $800 per crack, while a full exterior foundation waterproofing project could be $8,000 to $20,000 or more. Interior drainage systems with a sump pump usually fall between $4,000 and $12,000. We provide clear, itemized quotes after a thorough inspection.

    We commonly install Schluter Kerdi, Custom RedGard, Laticrete Hydro Ban and similar systems. The right choice depends on your shower design and tile. We follow each manufacturer's installation steps to keep the warranty valid.

    While minor DIY fixes like patching small cracks are possible, complete walls waterproofing is best left to professionals. It involves specialized knowledge of drainage, foundation mechanics, and proper material application. Improper DIY work can lead to poor results, wasted money, and worse water damage over time.

    A shower wall waterproofing project typically takes two to four days, including drying time between coats. Exterior block sealing can often be completed in a day. Larger foundation jobs may take a few days.

    Walls waterproofing is generally not recommended as a DIY project due to its complexity and the high importance of a sound foundation. It often involves heavy excavation, specialized equipment, precise material application, and knowledge of drainage principles and local building codes. Improper waterproofing can lead to more severe and costly damage down the line, so professional help is highly advisable.

    Interior waterproofing addresses water after it has entered the foundation, typically managing it with sealants and drainage systems inside the basement or crawl space. Exterior waterproofing prevents water from reaching the foundation walls in the first place, usually by excavating and applying membranes or coatings to the outside of the foundation.

    Waterproofing prevents the moisture mold needs to grow. Existing mold should be removed and the area dried before sealing. We remove affected materials when needed and recommend remediation for larger growth.

    The duration of a walls waterproofing project varies significantly. Smaller jobs like interior crack sealing might take a day or two, while extensive exterior waterproofing with excavation and drainage system installation could take several days to over a week.
