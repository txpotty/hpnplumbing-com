    Pilot Light Repair in Horace

    A pilot that lights and then dies when you let go of the knob almost always means a weak thermocouple. Sometimes the cause is a clogged flame arrestor screen starving the burner of air. We test the thermocouple output in millivolts. A good reading points us to the gas valve instead.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Horace, ND

    The anode rod sacrifices itself so the steel tank does not rust. In hard water it can be eaten away within a few years. We pull and inspect the rod, then install a magnesium, aluminum or powered anode depending on your water and any odor problems. Rotten-egg odor often clears up.
