    Upgrading Old Sewer Pipe Materials in Horace

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all fail in their own ways. Clay joints let roots in every few feet, cast iron scales and rusts through along the bottom, and Orangeburg tar paper pipe slowly flattens into an oval. Modern plastic pipe avoids all three problems. HDPE is fused into one continuous piece with no joints for roots to find.

    How Pipe Bursting Works in Horace

    A winch pulls the bursting head. The head is slightly bigger than the old pipe, so it splits clay or cast iron outward into the soil while fused HDPE trails behind it in one continuous piece. Only two pits get dug. The fused pipe has no joints, so roots cannot get in again.
