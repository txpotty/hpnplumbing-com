    Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement in Horace, ND

    To minimize disruption, trenchless sewer line replacement replaces pipes without extensive digging. We offer pipe bursting, where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward. This is ideal when protecting landscaping or driveways is a priority. The method works well for many homes in Horace and can often be completed faster than traditional methods, using high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipe known for its long life.

    Partial Sewer Line Repair in Horace, ND

    Not every damaged sewer needs full replacement. If the camera shows a single break or one root-damaged joint, a spot repair may fix the problem. We locate the exact spot and depth, excavate a small area and replace that section. This costs far less than replacing the whole lateral. We only recommend it when the rest of the line is in sound condition.

    Sewer Pipe Lining as an Alternative in Horace

    Cured-in-place pipe lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. After cleaning, a resin-soaked liner is pulled in and cured in place. This works when the host pipe still holds its shape without major sags or collapse. Lining can be a good option under slabs or finished areas. We tell you honestly if your line is a candidate.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Horace, ND

    Traditional excavation is sometimes the best option, especially when the old line has a severe belly or the slope must be corrected. We dig along the line, remove the old pipe and set new PVC on compacted bedding at the proper grade. Rocky or hard-packed soil can slow digging, and we account for that in the estimate. Each joint is glued and checked before backfill. The inspector sees the open trench before we close it up.

    Sewer Line Replacement Cost in Horace, ND

    Cost depends on length, depth, soil type, access and the method used. Open-trench replacement in an open yard can be reasonably priced per foot, while trenchless methods may cost more per foot but save on restoration. Hardscape, landscaping and driveways change the math fast. We give you a written estimate for each option after a camera inspection. That way you can compare total cost, not just the headline price.

    Once your sewer line is replaced, we can recommend steps to extend its lifespan.
