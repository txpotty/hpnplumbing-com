    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Horace, ND

    Older cast iron drains rust and scale from the inside, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. A jet clears that crust. Badly corroded cast iron with thin walls or cracks needs gentler settings or no jetting at all, and the camera tells us which before we start, along with whether the pipe is a candidate for lining.

    Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in Horace

    Jetting a broken pipe can make the break worse. The camera always goes in first. The video shows the pipe material, the condition of each joint, and any cracks, offsets or sags, which tells us the nozzle and pressure to use or whether we should not jet that line at all.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in Horace

    Roots find their way into sewer lines through cracked joints, and older clay pipe has a joint every few feet. We use a spinning root-cutter nozzle. The jet then flushes the debris to the main, and the camera marks the joints that let the roots in.

    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Horace

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. A cable cuts a channel through it. A spinning nozzle removes the grease all around the pipe so it flushes away cleanly.

    Hard Water Scale Removal in Horace, ND

    Scale builds slowly inside drain pipe, narrowing it a little more each year. Many rural homes get water from private wells or rural water systems, and iron and sulfur odors are common complaints. Scouring nozzles remove the scale, and badly scaled cast iron may get a descaling pass too. You can see the change on video.

    Root Removal Jetting in Horace

    Shade trees and shrubs send roots toward small cracks in clay and cast iron pipe. Root-cutting nozzles clear them. Jetting does not stop roots from returning, because the crack is still there, but it clears the pipe completely and lets us see on camera whether a spot repair or a liner would stop the regrowth.

    Hydrojetting vs Snaking Cost Comparison in Horace, ND

    Snaking a main line typically costs $200 to $450, while residential hydrojetting usually runs $400 to $900 with a camera inspection. Jetting costs more up front. Because jetting removes the buildup that causes repeat clogs, many homeowners spend less over a few years than they would on repeated snaking visits for the same line.

    Commercial and Restaurant Hydrojetting in Horace

We set up jetting plans for each building's needs, schedule work during slow hours, and keep the camera footage on file so property managers can show tenants or owners the condition of the lines.
