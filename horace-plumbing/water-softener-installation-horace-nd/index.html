    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Horace

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. That protects every faucet, shower, water heater and appliance downstream, which is where hard water does most of its damage over the years. We size before we sell. Hardness and household use set the grain capacity.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Horace

    Ion exchange is the only method that truly removes hardness from water. We install salt-based softeners with a resin tank sized to your grains per gallon, a brine tank, a bypass valve and a drain line that ends at a code-approved air gap. Then we program it. We set regeneration overnight.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Horace, ND

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HPN Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. Most effective option.

    Softener Loop Installation in Horace

    No loop? A softener loop is a pair of capped stubs on the main line, usually in the garage, that lets a softener be connected without cutting pipe later. We add the loop. Where we can, we keep hose bibs and irrigation on hard water to save salt. Future service gets easier too.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Horace

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. Instead of removing hardness, they convert it into a form that resists sticking, which reduces scale without salt or brine waste. Maintenance is light. They work best on moderate water.
