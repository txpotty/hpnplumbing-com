    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Fargo

    An air switch puts a push button on the countertop or sink deck instead of a wall switch. The button pushes a puff of air down a thin tube.     Evolution Series Installation in Fargo

    Evolution units grind in two or three stages and carry extra sound insulation around the motor housing, which is why they sit taller and weigh more than a Badger. Cabinet clearance matters.     Upgrading Your InSinkErator in Fargo, ND

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. The new unit is taller. Because of that height, the discharge can end up below the trap arm, and we measure first so we can lower the trap if needed. We also confirm the circuit can carry the larger motor.

    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Fargo

    Nearly every InSinkErator has a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. We drive the plug out and fish it out of the chamber.     Standard InSinkErator Installation in Fargo
