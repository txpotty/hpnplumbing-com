    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Fargo

    Hard Water and Mineral Considerations in Fargo

    Water in North Dakota is often very hard, with high mineral and sulfate content that leaves deposits in water heaters and fixtures. Hard water leaves scale on disposal grind rings, sink flanges and drain tailpieces. Over time that buildup can make a disposal noisy and cause slow drainage. We inspect the drain line for scale and debris before connecting a new unit. If heavy buildup is present, we recommend cleaning or replacing the trap and tailpiece at the same time. Stainless steel grind components on Evolution models resist corrosion better in these conditions.
