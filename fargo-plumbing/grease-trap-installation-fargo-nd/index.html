    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Fargo

    Hydromechanical grease traps sit under or near sinks and use baffles and flow control to separate grease from water.     Hydro-mechanical grease interceptors, often called HGI units, are compact and designed for indoor use, usually under a sink or on the floor. These units use baffles to slow down wastewater flow, allowing grease to separate and accumulate.
