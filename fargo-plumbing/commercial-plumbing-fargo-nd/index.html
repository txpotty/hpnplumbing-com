    Commercial Plumbing Work We Handle in Fargo, ND

    Kitchen and Floor Drain Cleaning in Fargo

    Floor drains in commercial kitchens collect grease, rice, mop water and the odd utensil. They clog on a schedule you can almost set your watch by. A sectional machine opens the line, and a jetter cleans grease off the walls of the 2-inch and 3-inch branches so it stays open longer. Trap primers get checked too.

    Commercial Water Heater Repair and Installation in Fargo, ND

    A commercial water heater is judged by recovery rate, the gallons it can reheat per hour, not by tank size alone. Undersized heaters run cold by mid-shift. Our techs work on AO Smith, Rheem, Bradford White and State commercial heaters and check flue draft, gas valves and the T and P valve each visit. New installs get sized from your peak hour demand. Proper sizing is what keeps a busy kitchen in hot water all night.

    Sewer Camera Inspection for Businesses in Fargo

    A camera inspection shows the inside of a drain line on a monitor and records it so you can share it with a landlord or contractor. Guesswork goes away. We push the camera from a cleanout, note the footage at every break, root mass or sag, and use a locator to mark depth and position on the ground above. You get the video file. That footage helps you budget repairs and explain them to owners or a board.

    Commercial Restroom Fixture Repair in Fargo, ND

    Restroom calls are the most common commercial work we do. A flushometer that runs, a urinal that will not stop, or a sensor faucet that drips are usually fixed with a diaphragm kit, a solenoid or a new battery pack. We stock those. Wall-hung toilets that keep clogging or rocking get a carrier check, since a loose bowl can leak inside the wall. Low pressure on one fixture usually points to a clogged supply stop.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Fargo

    Each backflow test takes about 30 minutes per assembly and requires a short water shutoff to that line. Plan for a brief outage. We read each check valve and the relief valve on a calibrated gauge and record the numbers on the standard test form. We tag every passing device. We file the report with your water provider and keep a copy on our side for next year.

    Grease Trap Installation in Fargo

    A new restaurant or a kitchen expansion usually needs a grease interceptor sized from the fixtures that drain to it. Small kitchens can get by with an under-sink trap, but bigger operations often need an in-ground interceptor of 500 gallons or more. Sizing is set by code. We work from your kitchen plan, set the unit at the right depth and vent it so it passes the pretreatment inspection on the first try.

    Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in Fargo

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. Our certified testers check reduced pressure zone assemblies, double check valves and pressure vacuum breakers with calibrated differential gauges, then file the report with your water provider. A failed test usually means new rubber and springs, not a whole new assembly. That is why the truck carries Watts, Wilkins and Febco kits.

    Commercial Restroom Plumbing in Fargo

    A broken flushometer in a busy restroom gets noticed by every customer who walks in. We repair and replace Sloan and Zurn flush valves, sensor faucets, wall-hung toilets and carriers. We also handle ADA upgrades. For ADA remodels we set fixture heights, add grab bar blocking in the wall and insulate the drain and supply lines under sinks.
