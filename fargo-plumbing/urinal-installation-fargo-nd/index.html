    Frequently Asked Questions About Urinal Installation in Fargo

    The cost of urinal installation in Fargo, ND can range from $400 to $1,500, depending on the type of urinal (flush vs. waterless), complexity of plumbing modifications, and labor. Commercial installations with more extensive work might be at the higher end. We provide a detailed estimate after an on-site assessment.

    Regular cleaning with appropriate cleaners is essential for both flush and waterless urinals. Waterless urinals also require regular replacement of their sealant cartridges, typically every 1 to 3 months depending on usage. Proper maintenance prevents odors and clogs and keeps the fixture hygienic.

    We guarantee our installation labor, and fixtures and flush valves carry manufacturer warranties, often several years on flushometer parts.

    A washdown urinal uses a simple flushing action to rinse the bowl, relying on gravity and the slope of the drain. A siphonic urinal creates a siphonic action during flushing, which pulls waste more strongly down the drain, offering more effective waste removal and usually better odor control.

    A replacement takes two to four hours. A new install with rough-in and carrier usually takes one to two days, plus inspection time.

    Switching from a 1 gpf urinal to a 0.125 gpf model saves most of the water per flush. In a busy restroom that adds up to tens of thousands of gallons a year.
