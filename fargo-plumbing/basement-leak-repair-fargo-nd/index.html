    Crack Injection for Basements in Fargo

    Injection works on poured concrete walls, not block. We set ports along the crack, seal the face with epoxy paste, and pump in polyurethane or epoxy from the bottom port up until resin shows at the next port. Epoxy adds strength. For a crack that is actively wet, polyurethane works better since it reacts with water.

    Clogged Drain Tile Repair in Fargo

    Footing drains clog with silt and roots over the years, and once they stop flowing the water table at the wall rises. We camera the tile from the sump inlet to see where it is blocked. A jetter usually opens it. If the old clay tile has collapsed, it gets replaced with new perforated pipe.

    Wall Leak Solutions in Fargo, ND

    Damp walls without a crack often point to water soaking through porous block or concrete. Tape foil to the wall for a full day, and if the room side is wet it is condensation, not a leak. Condensation needs a dehumidifier. Real seepage calls for better drainage or an interior channel.
