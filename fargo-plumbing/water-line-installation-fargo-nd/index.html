    PEX Water Line Installation in Fargo

    PEX, or cross-linked polyethylene, is a popular choice for its flexibility, durability and resistance to corrosion and freezing. Big swings between summer heat and winter cold make pipes expand and contract, which slowly loosens fittings over the years. HPN Plumbing professionally installs PEX water lines for both new construction and replacements in Fargo. PEX offers excellent flow rates and is less prone to bursting than traditional rigid pipe, providing a long-lasting, cost-effective solution for your home's plumbing.
