    Frequently Asked Questions About Clogged Sewer Line in Fargo

    Clearing a main sewer line through an accessible cleanout typically costs $200 to $550. Hydro jetting runs about $400 to $1,000 depending on length and buildup. Adding a camera inspection is usually $150 to $350 more.

    Common signs include multiple drains backing up simultaneously, sewage odors coming from drains, gurgling sounds from toilets or drains, and water backing up in unusual places like the shower when the toilet is flushed. These indicate a blockage further down your main sewer line, not just a localized drain issue.

    Most standard clogged sewer line clearings can be completed within 1 to 3 hours. However, more severe blockages, extensive root intrusions, or situations requiring pipe repair or replacement can take longer, sometimes a full day or more.

    While minor clogs in individual drains can sometimes be resolved with DIY methods, a main sewer line clog is best handled by professionals. Attempting to clear it yourself without the right tools can push the blockage further or damage your pipes, especially with older plumbing systems.

    HPN Plumbing stands behind our work. We offer a warranty on our sewer line clearing services, usually ranging from 30 to 90 days, depending on the specific service and the condition of the pipes. We will provide full details on your warranty at the time of the visit.
