    Slab Leak Reroutes in Fargo

    A reroute abandons the leaking line under the floor. New pipe runs through the walls or attic instead, which avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than opening a finished floor. We usually use PEX because it handles heat and resists corrosion. It suits tile and hardwood homes.

    Commercial Slab Leak Detection in Fargo, ND

    Offices, shops and restaurants on slabs in Fargo, ND can lose a lot of water fast because their supply lines are larger. After-hours testing is available. HPN Plumbing isolates zones, traces mains and branch lines under the floor, then marks the leak so the repair fits around your hours.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Fargo

    Hot lines leak under slabs more often than cold ones. Heat speeds up corrosion, and the pipe expands and contracts every time someone showers, wearing against the gravel and concrete until a pinhole opens. Warm floors and a water heater that runs nonstop are the signs. Gas or electric bills may jump too.

    Why Locating First Saves Money in Fargo

    Detection usually costs a few hundred dollars. A wrong guess costs more. Breaking up a floor in the wrong place means paying for concrete work, flooring and a second opening, while an accurate mark keeps the repair to one small hole or a clean reroute that never touches the slab in your Fargo property.

    Pressure Testing and Isolation in Fargo

    We always start with isolation. Our tech closes the main, caps or valves off the hot and cold sides, and puts a gauge on each, then watches which one drops over fifteen to thirty minutes. The gauge points us to the right line. It also rules out a toilet or faucet leak masquerading as a slab leak in your Fargo, ND home.
