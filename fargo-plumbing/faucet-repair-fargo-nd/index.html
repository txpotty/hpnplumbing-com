    What We Fix on Faucets in Fargo, ND

    Low Water Pressure Faucet Fix in Fargo, ND

    Weak flow at one faucet usually points to a clogged aerator or a cartridge packed with scale, not a problem with the house pressure. Water in North Dakota is often very hard, with high mineral and sulfate content that builds scale in water heaters and fixtures. We pull the aerator with a key and clean or replace it, then check the cartridge screens. If every fixture is weak, the problem is elsewhere.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Fargo

    Sputtering flow, where the water spits and surges, often means air or debris in the line after a water main repair, or a partly clogged cartridge screen. A flush of the lines usually fixes it. On pull-down faucets, the check valves in the hose can stick too, and we clean or replace those.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Fargo, ND

    Ball faucets have a single lever over a domed cap, and the drip usually comes from the two small rubber seats and springs that press against the ball. A seats and springs kit fixes most of them. Loose handles happen too. If the handle leaks, snugging the adjusting ring under the cap often fixes it.

    Bathroom Pop-Up Drain Repair in Fargo

    Pop-up drains on bathroom sinks fail at the pivot ball, the clevis strap, the lift rod or the stopper itself, and a slow drain often turns out to be hair wrapped around the stopper. Pull the stopper first. We replace the pop-up assembly in a matching finish when the old one is corroded or the pivot leaks.

    Cracked Faucet Body Repair in Fargo

    Cracked faucet bodies, usually from freezing or an old brass casting that finally gave out, cannot be repaired and need a replacement faucet. Shut the stops first. We can usually install a new faucet the same day, and we will check the supply lines and stops while the water is off.

    Dripping Faucet Solutions in Fargo

    Every drip has a source, and on a faucet that has dripped for years there is usually more than one worn part. Anything worn gets replaced, and the rest gets checked. Compression, cartridge, ball and disc faucets all fail differently, so the repair is matched to the valve inside yours.

    Faucet Repair Versus Replacement in Fargo, ND

    A new cartridge usually costs well under the price of a new faucet and can add years of life, as long as the faucet body is sound and parts are still made. Cracked or discontinued faucets are different. You get a repair price and a replacement price so the choice is yours.

    Specialty Faucet Repair in Fargo, ND

    Pot fillers, laundry tub faucets and wall-mount utility faucets each have their own valve styles, and many use compression stems or quarter-turn cartridges that hardware stores do not stock. The common stems ride on the truck. Filtered water faucets at the kitchen sink get new cartridges or check valves as needed.
