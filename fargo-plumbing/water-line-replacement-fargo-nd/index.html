    PEX Water Line Installation in Fargo, ND

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a modern, flexible, and increasingly popular choice for water line replacement. It resists corrosion, scaling, and chlorine, making it highly durable for most water conditions. Homeowners around Fargo should insulate exposed pipes, shut off outdoor spigots and keep the heat on when away in winter. PEX can expand and contract without bursting, which is a significant advantage when a cold snap hits Fargo. Its flexibility allows for fewer fittings and faster installation.
