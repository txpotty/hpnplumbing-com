    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Fargo

    A cable machine punches a hole through grease. A jetter scrubs the whole pipe wall instead. We run a commercial jetter at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI through the line feeding the trap and the line leaving it, working from the cleanout toward the main. On old cast iron we lower the pressure and watch the camera so we do not open up a thin spot.

    Clogged Grease Trap Repair in Fargo

    Hardened grease in the inlet tee is the usual culprit when a trap backs up. The fix starts with pumping the trap down and jetting the inlet line until it flows again. Solids packed against the baffle come out by hand, with a scoop and a bucket. Then we look for the reason it clogged, such as a dishwasher dumping too much hot water at once or a missing sink strainer upstream. Repeat clogs nearly always have a cause.

    Grease Trap Drain Line Repair in Fargo

    Sometimes the trap is not the problem. Lines between the sinks and the trap clog first, because that is where hot water cools. We put a camera in from the nearest cleanout and record the run, looking for sags, offset joints, roots in clay sections downstream, or a belly where grease collects every week. Jetting a belly buys time, but only regrading the pipe fixes it.

    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Fargo, ND

    Tees are cheap parts with an outsized job. On the inlet side, the tee directs incoming water down under the floating grease. The outlet tee draws water from low in the tank, well under the grease cap, so if it breaks off or someone removes it during pumping, floating grease walks right out to the sewer. We replace broken tees in Schedule 40 PVC and set the drop to the depth the code or the manufacturer calls for.

    Grease Trap Leak Repair in Fargo

    Plastic and fiberglass trap bodies do not rust, but they can crack from a dropped lid or a dishwasher dumping water hotter than the trap is rated for. A hairline crack can sometimes be welded. Anything through the floor of the tank or along a molded seam we treat as a replacement, because patches there fail under the weight of a full trap. You will see the crack yourself before we suggest a fix.

    Preventative Grease Trap Maintenance & Repair in Fargo, ND

    Most trap repairs we do trace back to pumping intervals that stretched too long. The common rule is to pump when grease and solids fill a quarter of the liquid depth. After a repair, we measure the grease cap and solids with a sludge judge and suggest a pumping schedule based on how fast your trap actually fills. A kitchen running two fryers can hit that mark in a couple of weeks.

    Grease Interceptor Clog Removal in Fargo

    Grease does not stay liquid for long once it cools in a pipe. Within a few feet of the sink it hardens like candle wax along the top of the pipe. Chemical drain openers soften it for a day and then it re-forms farther downstream, sometimes past the trap entirely, where it becomes a sewer problem instead of a kitchen problem. Jetting removes it.
