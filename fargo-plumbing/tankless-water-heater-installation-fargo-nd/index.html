    Frequently Asked Questions About Tankless Water Heater Installation in Fargo

    Most gas tankless installations cost $3,000 to $6,500 including the unit, labor, venting and common gas line upgrades. Electric units can be less, though panel upgrades can add $1,000 to $3,000.

    Most tankless water heaters come with a manufacturer's warranty of 10 to 15 years on the heat exchanger and 3 to 5 years on parts. HPN Plumbing also provides a warranty on our installation labor, which we will detail in your service agreement.

    To maintain efficiency and extend the lifespan of your tankless unit, annual flushing to remove mineral buildup is recommended, especially in areas with hard water. Checking and cleaning the air filter (for gas models) and inspecting connections are also part of routine maintenance. HPN Plumbing offers complete maintenance services.

    Tankless units eliminate standby heat loss, which can reduce water heating energy use. Savings vary based on household size and usage habits.

    We install Rinnai, Navien, Noritz and Rheem tankless water heaters. Each brand offers different features, efficiency levels and warranties.

    A standard tankless water heater installation, replacing an existing tank, usually takes 4 to 8 hours. If extensive plumbing or gas line changes are required, it may take a full day. Our team works efficiently to restore your hot water as quickly as possible.

    With annual descaling, tankless units often last 15 to 20 years. Skipping maintenance in hard water areas can shorten that considerably.
