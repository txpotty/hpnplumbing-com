    Why Polybutylene Fails in Fargo

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. The inner wall of the pipe and fittings slowly turns brittle, and small cracks grow until the pipe splits. Water in North Dakota is often very hard, with high mineral and sulfate content that leaves deposits in water heaters and fixtures. Heat makes it worse. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.
