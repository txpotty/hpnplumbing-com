    How We Install Showers in Fargo, ND

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Fargo, ND

    Prefab units are the fastest route to a new shower. We set the base on mortar, connect the drain, install the valve and trim and make sure the walls are fastened so nothing flexes when someone leans on them. Good for rentals and guest baths. Most installs finish in a day.

    Budget Shower Upgrades in Fargo, ND

    Budget showers can still be built right. A fiberglass or acrylic unit with a quality pressure-balance valve costs far less than a tiled shower, and when it is set on a mortar bed with a sealed drain, it lasts decades. Spend on the valve. The quote covers everything up front.

    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Fargo, ND

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades. We install pressure-balancing, thermostatic and diverter valves, set to the depth your trim kit needs and plumbed with integral stops when the valve has them. Trim goes on after tile. That timing keeps the trim from getting scratched or caked with thinset while the tile is set.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Fargo

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. We install the valve, the head, an optional handheld and the drain, then set the mortar bed or foam pan to slope toward the drain at a quarter inch per foot. Testing follows. We pressure-test the valve and flood the pan for a day before tile.

    Custom Shower Enclosure Plumbing in Fargo

    Body sprays and steam units add lines and planning. Every body spray needs a supply line, and the sizing has to be right so the farthest outlet still has good pressure. Older neighborhoods may have lower pressure. A gauge reading comes first.

    Commercial Shower Installation in Fargo, ND

    Locker rooms need showers that take hundreds of uses a day. We install commercial valves with tamper-resistant trim, vandal-resistant heads and drains sized for several showers running at once, all to commercial code. Scheduling fits your hours. Thermostatic mixing at the source can protect every head in the room.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Fargo, ND

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. The tub comes out, the drain shifts to the pan location and the valve moves up to about 48 inches. On a slab, we cut concrete. No more stepping over a tub wall.

    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Fargo

    Rain heads pull up to 2.5 gallons per minute each. Pair a rain head with a handheld and body sprays and a 40-gallon water heater may run cold halfway through. The water heater gets sized up. Thermostatic valves with volume controls run the outlets.
