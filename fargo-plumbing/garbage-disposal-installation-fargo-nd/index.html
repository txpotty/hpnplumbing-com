    Air Switch Disposal Installation in Fargo, ND

    Air switches start the disposal with a button on the sink or counter. The button pushes air through a tube to a control box in the cabinet, which keeps electricity away from the button and avoids a new wall switch. They are common on islands.

    Disposal Installation on an Existing Sink in Fargo

    With a typical 1/2 or 3/4 HP unit, we remove the old unit if there is one and install the new one on the existing sink. We connect it to the drain, set the P-trap and wire it, then check the clearance under the sink, which can be tight in older Fargo homes with shallow cabinets or a water filter mounted on the back wall. Tight cabinets need planning. A compact unit can solve it.

    Same-Day Disposal Installation in Fargo, ND

    If your old disposal quits with a sink full of water, we can usually install a new one the same day. Our trucks carry common 1/2 and 3/4 HP models, mounting hardware and cord kits, so we do not need a second trip to the supply house before the sink drains again. Figure on roughly an hour.

    Batch-Feed Disposal Installation in Fargo, ND

    Batch-feed units only run with the lid in place, and the lid holds a magnet that closes a switch inside the unit. No wall switch is needed, which makes them handy in kitchens where running a new switch would mean opening finished walls. You grind one load at a time. Families with small kids often like the trade.

    Disposal Rough-In for New Construction in Fargo

    At rough-in we set the drain height and the outlet location before the cabinets go in. For a kitchen with a disposal, we usually set the drain stub lower than a plain sink would need, so the trap can sit below the disposal outlet with room for proper fall. The electrician adds the switched outlet. We mark its spot so it clears the trap.

    High-Capacity Disposal Installation in Fargo, ND

    A 1 HP disposal earns its keep in a busy kitchen, like an InSinkErator Evolution or a Waste King Legend model. We confirm the circuit can carry the motor and the drain can handle the flow, because a strong unit pushing into a flat trap arm will still clog. Bigger units are heavier. We support them while mounting.

    High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in Fargo

    A 3/4 or 1 HP unit is bigger and heavier than a starter model, so we measure the cabinet first. Taller units can push the discharge outlet below the wall stub, and when that happens we either lower the drain at the wall or pick a model with a higher outlet so water does not sit in the chamber. We also check the circuit. Bigger motors draw more at startup.

    Standard Disposal Installation in Fargo

    Most households in Fargo do well with a 1/3 to 1/2 horsepower continuous-feed unit. We remove the old unit if there is one, set a new flange in fresh plumber's putty, hang the disposal on a new mount and connect it to the trap with a new discharge tube and gasket. Then we wire it and test it. We listen for a smooth spin and watch the drain clear.
