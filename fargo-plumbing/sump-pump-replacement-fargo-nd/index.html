    Combination Sump Pump System Replacement in Fargo, ND

    Combination units put a primary AC pump and a DC backup on one discharge manifold, so you get two pumps and two floats in a single assembly. They suit pits too tight for two separate pumps. Depending on what failed, we swap one pump or the entire unit.

    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Fargo

    A submersible pump sits at the bottom of the pit, sealed in an oil-filled housing, and it runs quieter than anything else you can put down there. We pull the old unit, scoop the gravel and silt out of the basin, and set the new pump on a flat brick so it does not suck up debris. The check valve gets replaced at the same time. A worn valve lets water fall back and makes the pump run twice as often.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Fargo, ND

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the pit, out of the water, which is why a good one can run fifteen years in a dry basement. You will hear one kick on from upstairs. We set the new column plumb, secure it so vibration does not walk it across the basin, and adjust the float rod stops for the cycle you want. They fit pits as narrow as 10 inches.

    Backup Pump Options in Fargo, ND

    A working sump pump with a battery backup is important for basement homes during the spring thaw. Outages tend to hit right when the rain is heaviest and the pit is filling fastest. A backup takes over when the main pump quits or loses power. Where water pressure is strong, a water-powered backup runs off the supply line with no battery at all, protected by a backflow preventer. They will not work on a well.
