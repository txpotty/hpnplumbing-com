    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Fargo

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg pipe, a tar-paper material that flattens and blisters over time. Cast iron from the same era corrodes from the inside and scales up until little flow remains. When a camera shows widespread failure, patching is a waste of money. We replace the line in sections or end to end with PVC, and we can often use trenchless methods to avoid tearing up the whole yard.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Fargo

    When trenchless repair isn't possible due to severe damage or specific pipe configurations, traditional dig-and-replace methods are necessary. This involves excavating a trench to access and replace the damaged section of the sewer line. Our team ensures careful excavation and efficient replacement, minimizing the impact on your property in Fargo.

    Root Intrusion Removal in Fargo, ND

    Tree roots are a common cause of sewer line blockages and damage, especially with the mature trees found in many Fargo neighborhoods. Roots can infiltrate tiny cracks, expand and eventually crush or clog pipes. We use specialized augers and hydro-jetting to cut through and remove roots, followed by inspection to assess pipe condition. For ongoing issues, we might recommend trenchless repair or pipe replacement to prevent future root problems.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Fargo, ND

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive option. This method, which includes pipe bursting or pipe lining, requires minimal excavation, saving your yard and landscaping from major disruption. We use specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one or insert a resin-coated liner that cures in place, creating a new, durable pipe inside the old one. It's an efficient and popular choice for protecting property appearance in Fargo.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Fargo

    Tree roots are a common culprit for sewer line damage in North Dakota, drawn to the moisture and nutrients inside pipes.     Sewer Pipe Relining in Fargo, ND

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair technique where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is well suited for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without extensive digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an great option for preserving landscapes in Fargo.

    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Fargo

    Clay and cast iron sewer pipe crack from soil movement, heavy vehicles and simple age. A cracked section lets sewage leak into the soil and lets dirt fall into the pipe, which leads to recurring clogs. The repair is tested before we backfill so you know it holds.
