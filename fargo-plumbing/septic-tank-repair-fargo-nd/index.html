    Septic Tank Alarm Troubleshooting in Fargo

    Many modern septic systems, especially those with pumps or advanced treatment units, include alarms to indicate high water levels or system malfunctions. If your septic alarm is sounding in Fargo, it is a clear sign of trouble. HPN Plumbing technicians can accurately troubleshoot the cause of the alarm, whether it is a float switch issue, pump failure or a clogged line, and perform the necessary repairs to silence the alarm and restore system balance.

    Drain Field Clog Remediation in Fargo

    A clogged drain field is a common cause of septic system backups and can be a significant issue for homeowners in Fargo. This can be caused by excessive solids, tree root intrusion or soil compaction. We offer professional drain field remediation, which may include hydro-jetting the distribution lines, addressing root infiltration or assessing the soil's percolation capacity to restore proper effluent absorption and prevent future saturation.

    Effluent Pump Repair in Fargo, ND

    Homes with uphill or distant fields often use an effluent pump in a pump tank. Failed pumps, stuck floats or tripped breakers set off high water alarms. We test the pump, float switches and control panel and replace failed parts. A working alarm gives you warning before sewage comes back into the house.

    Septic Tank Lid and Riser Repair in Fargo

    Cracked or deteriorated concrete lids are a real safety hazard. We replace failing lids and install risers that bring access up to grade. Risers make pumping and inspection quicker and cheaper over time. Secure, child-resistant lids are standard on our installations.

    Septic Tank Leak Repair in Fargo

    Septic tank leaks can lead to unpleasant odors, soggy spots in your yard and potential groundwater contamination. Our technicians use dye testing and camera inspections to locate the source of the leak, whether it is a crack in the tank or a compromised pipe connection. We provide durable repair solutions using purpose-made sealants and patching materials designed for the harsh environment of a septic tank in Fargo, ensuring a watertight seal and preventing future issues.
