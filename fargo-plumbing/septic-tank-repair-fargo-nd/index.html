    Septic Tank Fixes We Handle in Fargo, ND

    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Fargo, ND

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones get eaten by sulfuric acid from sewer gas and eventually fall off into the tank. With no outlet baffle, sludge flows right out to the leach lines. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Effluent Pump Replacement in Fargo, ND

    Effluent pumps in a dosing chamber last roughly 8 to 15 years, and they usually quit without much warning at all. Humming with no flow points to a jammed impeller or a failed capacitor. We pull it and test the discharge line before setting a new one. Floats get replaced at the same time.

    Septic Alarm Troubleshooting in Fargo

    A septic alarm sounding is the system's way of saying the water is too high in the pump chamber or the aerobic unit. Hit the mute switch and go easy on the water. Our tech confirms the pump runs and looks for a float snagged on its cord. A tripped breaker is the cheapest answer.

    Aerobic and Alternative System Repair in Fargo, ND

    Aerobic treatment units use an air pump to feed oxygen to the bacteria, and when that pump dies the unit starts to smell within days. We rebuild or replace linear and rotary aerators, plus clean out diffusers clogged with solids. Timers and panels get tested too. We also keep the service log your permit may require.

    Emergency Septic Overflow in Fargo

    Raw sewage coming up through a tub drain carries bacteria you do not want on your skin or your floors. Keep kids and pets away from it and stop all water use. Our tech clears the line or calls in a pump truck, depending on what we find. Cleanup guidance comes with it.

    Leach Field Restoration in Fargo, ND

    Before we condemn a leach field, we open the distribution box and look at how evenly it splits the flow. A tilted or crushed D-box can send everything to one trench and drown it. A new or leveled box brings a lot of fields back. Some fields are past saving. For those, we explain the design and permit steps.

    Commercial Septic Repair in Fargo, ND

    Churches and small offices on septic run more water through the tank than any house, and a commercial kitchen adds grease on top of that. We size repairs to the actual daily flow, not a residential guess. Grease interceptors and lift stations are part of it. Downtime costs you, so we schedule around open hours.

    House-to-Tank Line Repair in Fargo

    Gurgling drains right after a pump-out point to the line between the house and the tank, not the tank itself. That section sags or breaks from roots and settling soil. We camera it from the cleanout and mark the bad spot on the surface. Then we dig one hole, not a trench. A second cleanout near the tank goes in while it is open.
