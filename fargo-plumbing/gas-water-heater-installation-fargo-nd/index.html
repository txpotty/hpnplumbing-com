    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Fargo

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. Because the combustion is sealed, they work well in small closets where room air is limited. Many need no power. The vent goes through a wall with proper clearances.

    Standard Tank Gas Water Heater Installation in Fargo

    Our standard gas tank install starts with removing the old heater. The old tank gets drained and removed, then the new one goes in with new connectors, a drip leg and a sloped vent. Testing comes last. They are dependable and affordable.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Fargo

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They cost the least, but the vent has to be the right size, slope upward and keep its clearances. Old vents get checked. Corroded or undersized pipe gets replaced.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Fargo, ND

    Gas tankless units fire at up to 199,000 BTU, far more than a 40,000 BTU tank burner. That load usually calls for a 3/4 inch gas line or larger and a dedicated vent in PVC or stainless steel, depending on whether the unit is condensing. They never run out. Water softeners and filters are popular around Fargo because they extend the life of water heaters, dishwashers and faucets. A scale filter may be worth adding.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Fargo, ND

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. That means the heater can go in a spot with no chimney or vertical flue, and the vent can run sideways out an exterior wall as long as it stays within the length limit. They need an outlet. Termination clearances follow the manual.
