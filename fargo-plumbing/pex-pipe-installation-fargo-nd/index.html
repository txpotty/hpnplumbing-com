    Full Home PEX Repiping in Fargo

    If your home has old, corroded or leaking pipes, a full home PEX repipe can greatly improve your plumbing system.     Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Fargo

    Kitchen and bathroom remodels are a great time to replace old supply lines with PEX. We rough in new lines to the correct heights for vanities, showers, tubs and appliances, then pressure test before the walls are closed. Additions and guest suites get properly sized lines tied into your existing system. We coordinate with your contractor to keep the project schedule on track.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Fargo, ND

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, much like a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when several fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to weigh cost and performance.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Fargo, ND

    HPN Plumbing is experienced with installing all types of PEX piping, including PEX-A, PEX-B and PEX-C, each with distinct properties for specific applications. We help you choose the best PEX type for your home's needs and budget in Fargo, ND. Our experience ensures the correct PEX material and fitting method, whether it is expansion for PEX-A or crimp and clamp for PEX-B and PEX-C, for a secure and lasting system.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Fargo

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the different advantages of each. Our plumbers in Fargo can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    Frequently Asked Questions About PEX Pipe Installation in Fargo

    A whole-home PEX repipe typically costs $4,500 to $12,000 depending on home size, bathroom count and access. Smaller jobs like a single bathroom remodel rough-in usually run $800 to $2,500.

    HPN Plumbing stands behind our PEX pipe installations with a full warranty on both labor and materials. Specific warranty details will be provided with your estimate, but you can be confident your new PEX system is installed correctly and will perform reliably for years to come in your Fargo home.

    We install high-quality PEX piping from reputable manufacturers such as Uponor (PEX-A), Viega (PEX-B) and Zurn (PEX-B). We also work with other trusted brands like SharkBite for fittings, so the best materials are used for your installation.

    Properly installed PEX is expected to last several decades. Manufacturers often offer 25 year warranties when their pipe and fittings are used together.

    Signs you might need PEX repiping include consistently low water pressure, discolored or rusty water, frequent leaks, or old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes. These issues are typical in older Fargo homes and point to a failing plumbing system.

    We primarily use high-quality, reputable PEX brands such as Uponor (PEX-A) and Viega (PEX-B), which are known for their durability and reliability. These brands meet or exceed industry standards and are approved for use in North Dakota plumbing applications, providing a long-lasting and dependable plumbing system for your home.

    A full home PEX repipe for an average-sized home in Fargo typically takes 3 to 5 days. Smaller projects or single-line replacements can often be completed in a single day. We aim to minimize disruption and communicate timelines clearly.

    PEX is well suited for a wide range of climates because of its flexibility and resistance to freezing. Strong winds and extreme cold can push frost into walls and crawlspaces, reaching pipes that seem well protected. While rigid pipes can burst when water freezes, PEX can expand and contract, greatly lowering the risk of burst pipes during cold snaps.
