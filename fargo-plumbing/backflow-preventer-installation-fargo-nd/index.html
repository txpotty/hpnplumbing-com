    Reliable Backflow Preventer Installation Services in Fargo, ND

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Fargo

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a common backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Fargo, DCVAs are often installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers carefully install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.

    Commercial Backflow Prevention in Fargo, ND

    Commercial properties in Fargo, ND often have complex plumbing systems that pose a higher risk of cross-contamination and need robust backflow prevention. Restaurants, hospitals, industrial facilities and even multi-family buildings must comply with commercial plumbing codes. HPN Plumbing provides complete installation of heavy-duty backflow preventers, including large diameter RPZ assemblies, to safeguard public health and meet North Dakota regulations for businesses. We handle all required permits and inspections.

    Backflow Enclosures and Freeze Protection in Fargo, ND

    Above-ground assemblies sit in direct sun in summer and can freeze on cold nights. Frozen pipes are a serious and regular winter risk in North Dakota, and a single cold night can split a line in an exterior wall. We install insulated enclosures, freeze blankets or heated cabinets based on your local climate. Enclosures also discourage theft, since brass backflow assemblies are a common target for scrap thieves. We choose enclosures with enough room for testing and service.

    Reduced Pressure Zone Assembly Installation in Fargo, ND

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of protection and are required for high hazard connections like commercial kitchens, medical facilities, chemical injection and car washes. They include two independent check valves with a relief valve that dumps water if either check fails. Because the relief valve can discharge, we plan for drainage and install the assembly above grade where it can be tested. We carry proven brands like Watts, Febco, Wilkins and Apollo.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in Fargo, ND

    For high-hazard applications in Fargo, ND where toxic substances could contaminate the water supply, a reduced pressure zone, or RPZ, device is often required. RPZ backflow preventers offer the highest level of protection, using a relief valve to discharge water if the pressure drops. HPN Plumbing specializes in RPZ installation, making sure all components are correctly sized, positioned and set up according to state and local plumbing codes. Proper installation is essential for effective operation.

    Backflow Preventer Installation Cost Comparison in Fargo, ND

    A residential pressure vacuum breaker is the most affordable option, while larger RPZ assemblies for commercial buildings cost a good deal more in parts and labor.     Backflow Testing and Certification in Fargo

    Most water providers require testable backflow assemblies to be certified at installation and then once a year. Our certified testers check each valve's opening point, relief valve operation and differential pressure. If a device fails, we can repair or rebuild it on the spot with genuine parts. We submit passing results so you avoid compliance letters or service interruption.
