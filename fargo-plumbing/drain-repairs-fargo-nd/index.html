    Sewer Line Drain Repair in Fargo

    Issues with your main sewer line can impact all drains in your home. Common problems include tree root intrusion, cracks from ground movement or pipe collapses. We offer thorough sewer line diagnostics, including video inspections to assess the damage. Depending on the severity, we can use trenchless methods like pipe lining or pipe bursting, which minimize yard disruption, or traditional excavation for severe damage. We make sure your main drain system is fully functional.

    Laundry and Floor Drain Repair in Fargo

    Washing machines dump a large volume of water fast, and an undersized or poorly vented standpipe will overflow. We correct standpipe height, replace cracked laundry box drains and add proper venting. For garage and utility floor drains, we restore the trap seal so sewer odor does not escape. We also repair the trap primers that keep those seals filled.

    Outdoor Drain Repair in Fargo

    Outdoor drains, including yard drains and downspout connections, are essential for managing rainwater and preventing foundation damage, especially during heavy rainfall. We repair and clear blockages in outdoor drainage systems in Fargo, restoring proper water diversion and protecting your property from excess moisture and erosion.

    Exterior Drain Tile Repair in Fargo, ND

    Exterior drain tiles, also known as French drains, are essential for protecting your foundation from hydrostatic pressure by diverting groundwater. When these systems fail due to clogs, crushing or poor installation, water can accumulate around your home's foundation. Basements are standard in most homes, and many rely on sump pumps to manage groundwater during wet springs. HPN Plumbing repairs and replaces damaged drain tiles, restoring proper water diversion and foundation protection for homes in Fargo, ND.

    Storm Drain Repair in Fargo, ND

    Effective storm drains are crucial for managing rainwater and preventing property flooding, especially during heavy downpours. A working sump pump with a battery backup is important for basement homes during the spring thaw. Blockages in storm drains are usually caused by leaves, debris and silt accumulation. HPN Plumbing clears these obstructions using powerful hydro-jetting and manual removal techniques. We also repair damaged or collapsed storm drain pipes, ensuring proper drainage away from your foundation. Regular maintenance can prevent costly repairs and protect your property in Fargo, ND.

    Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Fargo, ND

    Leaking drain pipes can cause serious water damage and mold growth if not addressed quickly. Causes often include corrosion, shifting foundations or damage from root intrusion. HPN Plumbing performs precise leak detection using acoustic and thermal imaging equipment. We repair leaks by patching small holes or replacing damaged sections of PVC, cast iron or copper piping, leaving a watertight seal. Our repairs meet all local plumbing codes.

    Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Fargo, ND

    Many older homes have cast iron drain stacks and branch lines that are now corroding from the inside. Rust scale catches debris and cracks let water seep into walls and floors. We cut out failing sections and replace them with ABS or PVC using shielded transition couplings. When the whole stack is failing, we quote a full replacement so you can plan the work.
