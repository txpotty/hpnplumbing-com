    Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Minot

    Flushing is the heart of tank maintenance. Water in North Dakota is often very hard, with high mineral and sulfate content that builds scale in water heaters and fixtures. We run a hose from the drain valve, open it up and let the tank drain until the water runs clear, then refill and check the anode rod, the relief valve and every connection for drips or corrosion. Takes about an hour. Less sediment means more years.

    Tune-Up Versus Replacement in Minot, ND

    A tune-up costs a fraction of a new heater. Maintenance helps a tank reach its full rated life and helps tankless units last much longer. Sometimes replacement makes sense. We give you real numbers for both.

    Tankless Water Heater Service in Minot, ND

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale inside the heat exchanger chokes flow until the unit throws codes or shuts off mid-shower. We flush it with descaler. We also check the igniter, the fan and the exhaust vent for safe operation.

    Descaling Tankless Units in Minot

    Error codes often mean scale. Scale in the exchanger cuts flow and trips the unit's safety shutoff. A pump kit handles it. Most makers recommend descaling at least yearly with hard water.

    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Minot

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Calcium and magnesium settle on the bottom of the tank and form a layer that traps heat under it, so water boils in pockets beneath the scale and pops every time the burner or lower element fires. We flush until it runs clear. Heavy buildup may take several cycles.
