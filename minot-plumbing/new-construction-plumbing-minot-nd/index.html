    Underground and Under-Slab Plumbing in Minot

    The underground phase sets the drain, waste and vent layout that every fixture in the house will rely on. We trench, bed and slope PVC or ABS drain lines at a minimum quarter inch per foot and protect every pipe that passes through the slab or foundation with sleeves. Frost heave can shift soil around sewer lines and misalign joints, which often shows up as slow drains or backups. We compact bedding carefully and avoid rigid points that could stress the pipe as the ground moves. Each line is water tested and left exposed for the inspector before your concrete contractor pours.
