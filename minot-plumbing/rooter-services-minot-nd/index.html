    Frequently Asked Questions About Rooter Services in Minot

    The cost of rooter service in Minot, ND usually ranges from $200 to $600, depending on the severity and location of the clog.     Homes with tree roots or older clay pipe often need rooting every 12 to 24 months. Homes with newer PVC lines may go many years without it. A camera inspection can help set the right schedule.

    Common signs include slow drains, gurgling sounds from pipes, water backing up in sinks or toilets, and foul odors coming from drains. If you notice any of these issues, especially multiple fixtures backing up, it's a strong sign you need professional rooter service.

    When performed by trained professionals like those at HPN Plumbing, rooter services are safe for your pipes. Our technicians are skilled in operating the equipment correctly to clear blockages without causing damage. However, if pipes are already compromised, such as corroded or cracked, some methods may worsen existing issues, which is why a camera inspection is often recommended first.

    HPN Plumbing stands by the quality of our work. We offer a warranty on our rooter services, the details of which will be provided with your estimate. This gives you confidence knowing that our solutions are designed to last.

    Drain snaking, also known as augering, uses a long, flexible cable with a cutting head to physically break through or retrieve blockages like hair or tree roots. Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to clean the entire interior of the pipe, removing grease, mineral buildup, and sludge, leaving pipes much cleaner than snaking alone. Hydro-jetting is usually more effective for severe or recurring clogs.

    We use top-quality equipment for our rooter services.
