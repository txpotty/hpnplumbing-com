    Sewer Camera Inspection for Trenchless Repair in Minot

    Every trenchless job starts with a push camera fed in from the cleanout or a pulled toilet. The screen shows the inside of the pipe live, and a sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot on the ground above it. That tells us the depth. No guessing where to dig.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacements in Minot

    A bursting head is a steel cone, slightly wider than the old pipe, pulled through the line by a hydraulic unit or a winch cable. As it moves, it splits the old clay or cast iron and shoves the pieces into the surrounding soil while a string of new HDPE pipe follows right behind it. Fused joints, no gaps. We use it when the old line is too broken or flattened to hold a liner.

    Causes of Sewer Line Damage in Minot, ND

    Shifting soil is one of the main reasons sewer joints pull apart or crack. Frost heave can shift soil around sewer lines and crack joints, which often shows up as slow drains or backups. Roots are the other big one, since a hairline gap at a clay joint is enough for a feeder root to get in and then swell into a mat that catches paper. Then age finishes the job. Cast iron scales and thins, and clay bells crack under the weight of soil and traffic.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Minot

    Bursting can upsize the line. We can pull in 6-inch HDPE behind a head that bursts an old 4-inch clay pipe, which helps on lines that clog because they were undersized from the start or have been added onto with extra bathrooms. The pit at each end is small. No trench in between.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Minot, ND

    CIPP stands for cured-in-place pipe. The crew saturates a felt tube with two-part epoxy on a roller table at the truck, invert it into the cleaned line with air pressure, and hold it against the old pipe wall until the resin hardens. Cure time runs a few hours. The finished liner seals cracks and leaking joints in the original pipe.

    Sewer Line Video Inspection in Minot

    We record the full camera run on every job, from the cleanout out to the city main or the septic tank. You get a copy of the video file and a written summary. On the footage you can see what we see, roots at a joint at 22 feet and an offset at 38 feet where the pipe dropped. That map drives the repair plan.

    Perma-Liner Lining Equipment in Minot

    Perma-Liner makes inversion drums and epoxy systems for lining 2- to 6-inch drain lines, which covers most of what we see in houses. The equipment fits in a pickup bed. Compact gear matters on tight lots. Resin gets mixed on site and timed to the length of the run, so it does not start curing before the liner is in place.
