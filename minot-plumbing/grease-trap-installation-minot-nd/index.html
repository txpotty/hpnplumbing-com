    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Minot

    Hydro-mechanical grease interceptors, often called HGI units, are compact and designed for indoor use, usually under a sink or on the floor. These units use baffles to slow down wastewater flow, allowing grease to separate and accumulate. HPN Plumbing expertly installs various sizes of HGI units, ensuring proper venting, leveling, and connection to your drainage system in Minot for maximum efficiency.

    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in Minot, ND

    Gravity grease interceptors, or GGI units, are larger, typically located outdoors, and rely on the natural difference in specific gravity between FOG and water. These are common for high-volume commercial kitchens. Our team handles the involved excavation, concrete work, and plumbing connections required for proper GGI installation, ensuring compliance with all local North Dakota ordinances for waste management.
