    Valve and Controller Diagnostics in Minot, ND

    The control valve and electronic controller regulate the entire softening process. Our technicians perform diagnostic checks to confirm these components are operating accurately, adjusting programming as needed. We check for wear and tear on moving parts and lubricate them to prevent future issues, keeping your water softener reliable in Minot, ND.

    Control Valve and Injector Service in Minot, ND

    The control valve directs water through backwash, brine draw, rinse and refill. Tiny injectors and screens inside the valve clog with sediment and salt residue, which stops brine draw. We disassemble the injector, clean the screens and replace worn seals and spacers on valves like the Fleck 5600 and Clack WS1. A clean valve gives you a full regeneration every time.

    Routine Water Softener Inspections in Minot

    Regular inspections are the foundation of effective water softener maintenance. Our technicians will examine your entire system, including the brine tank, resin tank, control valve and all connecting pipes. We check for leaks, corrosion, proper salt levels and any signs of wear that could lead to future problems. This proactive approach helps extend the life of your unit and keeps performance consistent, especially since water hardness varies from place to place.

    Maintenance Versus New Softener Costs in Minot, ND

    A maintenance visit typically costs far less than a new system, which often runs $1,200 to $3,500 installed.     Brine Tank Cleaning and Salt Bridge Removal in Minot, ND

    The brine tank is crucial for salt dissolution and regeneration. Over time, salt residue and other impurities can accumulate, forming a 'salt bridge' that prevents the system from properly regenerating. Our maintenance service includes thorough cleaning of the brine tank and removal of any salt bridges, so your system can draw brine effectively for full softening.
