    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Minot, ND

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. Jetting cleans the whole pipe. For a single clog in an otherwise clean line, snaking is cheaper and works fine, but for a line that clogs every few months, jetting removes the buildup that keeps catching debris.

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Minot, ND

    Restaurant kitchen lines carry hot grease that hardens as it cools. Much of it coats the pipe before it ever reaches the interceptor. Short jetting passes keep loosened grease from plugging the line downstream. Quarterly works for most.

    Commercial and Restaurant Hydrojetting in Minot

    Hotels, apartment complexes and shopping centers rely on drains that handle heavy daily use. Downtime costs them money.     Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Minot

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. Jetting the main clears roots, grease and sludge all the way to the city connection. On a 4-inch line we use a heavier nozzle with more flow, which moves the loosened debris downstream instead of leaving it to settle in a low spot.
