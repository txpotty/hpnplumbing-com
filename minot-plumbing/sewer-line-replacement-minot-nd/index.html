    Sewer Pipe Lining as an Alternative in Minot

    Cured-in-place pipe lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. After cleaning, a resin-soaked liner is pulled in and cured in place. This works when the host pipe still holds its shape without major sags or collapse. Lining can be a smart option under slabs or finished areas. We tell you honestly if your line is a candidate.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair and Replacement in Minot, ND

    Trenchless technology, such as pipe bursting or pipe lining, offers a less invasive alternative to traditional methods. Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one, fracturing the old pipe in place. Pipe lining installs a new, durable resin liner inside the existing pipe. Both methods greatly reduce the need for excavation, preserving your yard and landscaping in Minot, ND while providing a long-lasting solution.

    Traditional Trench Sewer Line Replacement in Minot

    For major damage or certain pipe layouts, traditional trenching may be the most effective method. This involves digging a trench to access and replace the damaged sewer line directly. Our team plans the excavation to minimize disruption to your landscape, using the right equipment for clean, precise digging. We make sure of proper grading and backfilling to prevent future issues and restore your property.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Minot

    Pipe bursting replaces the old sewer line by pulling a new pipe through the path of the old one. A bursting head breaks the old pipe outward while fused HDPE pipe follows close behind. This typically needs only an entry pit and an exit pit instead of a long trench. It works well under driveways, mature trees and landscaping. The new pipe is seamless, which leaves no seams for roots to enter.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Minot

    Cast iron drains in older homes corrode from the inside, particularly along the bottom of the pipe. Over time this leaves cracks, holes and a rough surface that catches waste. Replacing the cast iron sewer outside the home is often the first step. We can also evaluate under-slab cast iron and discuss lining or rerouting choices. New PVC or HDPE will not rust or scale the same way.
