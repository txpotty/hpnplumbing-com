    Commercial Water Softener Installation in Minot, ND

    Water Softener Replacement Services in Minot, ND

    Worn-out softeners usually show it in one of two ways, hard water at the tap or a brine tank that never drops. Our crew pulls the old system, checks the loop and drain line and installs a model matched to your water today. We haul away the old one. The work is usually done in a few hours.

    Water Softener Installation for New Construction in Minot, ND

    Building a home in Minot, ND? Adding a loop during framing is easy and cheap, since the plumber can route the main through the right spot before drywall goes up. Builders call us in early. We keep outdoor taps off the softener.

    Culligan Water Softener Installation in Minot

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. We follow Culligan's requirements for the drain, bypass and electrical, and we program the system for your tested hardness so it does not over-regenerate. We handle all of it. Existing loops make it faster.

    Softener Loop Installation in Minot

    No loop? A loop is the plumbing that routes the main line through a softener, and many older homes were built without one. We add the loop. Outdoor taps can stay on hard water, which saves salt. Future service gets easier too.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Minot

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. Compared with an older timer-based unit, a modern metered softener can use roughly half the salt and noticeably less regeneration water each month. That pays back over time. We install several models.

Every step gets done.
