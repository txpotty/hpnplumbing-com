    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Minot, ND

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades. We install pressure-balancing, thermostatic and diverter valves, set to the depth your trim kit needs and plumbed with integral stops when the valve has them. Trim goes on after tile. Waiting keeps the trim clean during tiling.

    Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Minot

    Rain heads pull up to 2.5 gallons per minute each. Pair a rain head with a handheld and body sprays and a 40-gallon water heater may run cold halfway through. We check the heater. A thermostatic valve manages each outlet.

    Shower Pan Installation in Minot

    Shower pans fail quietly. A pan with a pinhole in the liner or a poorly clamped drain will hold water for years while it slowly soaks the subfloor, so we flood-test every tiled pan for 24 hours before tile goes down. We level prefab pans carefully. Either way, the slope runs to the drain.

    Barrier-Free Walk-In Showers in Minot, ND

    Barrier-free entries need careful drain placement. We work with your tile setter on the waterproofing membrane and the slope, since water has to reach the drain before it can roll out the open side of the shower. Strong winds and extreme cold can drive frost into walls and crawlspaces, reaching pipes that seem well protected. We pick the pan system to match.

    Custom Shower Enclosure Plumbing in Minot

    Body sprays and steam units add lines and planning. Every body spray needs a supply line, and the sizing has to be right so the farthest outlet still has good pressure. Pressure varies by house. We test static pressure before designing the layout.
