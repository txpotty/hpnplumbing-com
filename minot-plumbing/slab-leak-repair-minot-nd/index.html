    Slab Leak Detection in Minot

    The first test is the water meter. Every fixture gets shut off. A meter that keeps turning tells us to isolate hot from cold, test each side and walk the losing line with a ground mic. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor.

    Commercial Slab Leak Repair in Minot, ND

    Commercial slabs in Minot carry longer pipe runs and more branches. Locating takes longer. Branches get isolated one by one, and the cutting is booked after closing or on a weekend so customers never notice. Work meets North Dakota code and stays within code.

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Minot, ND

    Direct repair is often the most efficient fix for a single leak. We cut a small square. With the pipe exposed, the damaged length is cut out, new pipe is joined in, and the line is pressure tested before the soil goes back in and gets compacted in layers. The concrete is patched flush with the floor.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Minot, ND

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Expansion wears them out. Each time hot water runs, the pipe expands against concrete and gravel, and recirculation pumps keep that cycle going all day, which grinds the pipe wall thin at the rub points. We repair or reroute the line and can set the recirculation pump on a timer.

    Ground Radar and Acoustic Locating in Minot

    Ground-penetrating radar shows what is inside the slab before we cut. It finds rebar and cables. Paired with acoustic listening gear, it lets us mark a leak in copper, PEX or galvanized pipe even near busy roads, because the radar fixes the pipe route and the mic confirms the leak. We use both in Minot.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Minot

    Old galvanized or thin copper is hard to patch reliably. Rerouting skips it. We abandon the leaking run in place and install new pipe through walls or the attic, so the problem section is gone from service and the water travels through fresh line that you can reach later. Minot homes with original plumbing see this often.

    Slab Leak Repair for PEX and Copper Lines in Minot, ND

    Copper and PEX fail in different ways under a slab. Copper tends to pinhole. Old copper wears through where it touches gravel or reacts with the water, while PEX usually leaks at a fitting that was crimped short or chewed by rodents near an access point. Trucks in Minot, ND carry parts for both.

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Minot, ND

    Leaks under a load-bearing wall or a kitchen island are hard to reach from inside. Tunneling solves that. From a pit by the foundation, the crew hand-digs under the slab, shoring as they go, and your floors and cabinets never get touched. Many Minot, ND homes are good candidates.
