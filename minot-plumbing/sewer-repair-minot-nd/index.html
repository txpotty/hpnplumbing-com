    Sewer Repair Methods for Minot, ND Homes

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Minot

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and we always jet a line before lining it. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Minot

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. The full length gets recorded. Cracks, root intrusion, offset joints and standing water all show up on the screen, and the sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the ground above, so any excavation stays small and targeted. Older homes in Minot often hide two or three problems in one line, and the video catches all of them.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Minot

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. A leaking joint invited them in. Warm, wet, nutrient-rich air escaping from a loose joint draws roots toward the line, and once they get in they thicken until they crack the pipe or separate the joint completely. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. A liner or new pipe at that joint is what actually stops regrowth.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection and Repair in Minot, ND

    Sewer smell outside the house points to a leak. A lush stripe over the line is another clue. We confirm it on camera, look for cracks, open joints or a broken cleanout riser, and locate it on the surface to keep the excavation tight. A small crack can sometimes be fixed with a short sectional liner, so nothing in the yard gets disturbed. Larger breaks get cut out and replaced.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Minot, ND

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. The joint is the real problem. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, based on the number of bad joints and the spacing between them. One or two joints is a small job. Ten or twelve usually justifies lining the run.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Minot

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. Crushed pipe cannot be lined. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. Older homes often need this on at least one section. The dig is staged so the house is without a sewer for as little time as possible.

    Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in Minot, ND

    A spot repair is the cheapest fix when the rest of the line is sound. Per foot, lining runs higher. It avoids excavation, though, and on long runs under driveways or patios the savings on concrete and landscaping often outweigh the higher price of the liner itself. Full replacement is right for Orangeburg or a line broken in several places. You get each option in writing with a number attached.

    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Minot, ND

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water. Paper and solids settle there. Basements are standard in most homes, and many rely on sump pumps to handle groundwater during wet springs. We measure the depth of standing water on camera, dig out that section, re-bed the pipe on compacted gravel and set it back to a quarter inch of fall per foot, which is the typical code minimum for a four-inch line. A shallow belly sometimes just needs regular cleaning.
