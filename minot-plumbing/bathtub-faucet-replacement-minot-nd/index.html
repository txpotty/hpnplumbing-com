    Emergency Tub Faucet Service in Minot, ND

    A tub that will not shut off needs water off now. Check behind the trim plate or an access panel for stops, and if there are none, shut the main and leave the drain open. A plumber heads out as soon as one is free. Broken stems are a common cause.

    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Minot, ND

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. Replacement inside the wall makes sense when parts are discontinued, the valve body is cracked or pitted, or you want anti-scald protection on an old three-handle faucet that has none. We quote both options. Most people choose repair first.

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Minot

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. That cartridge wears out and causes drips or a handle that will not reach full hot, and swapping it with a cartridge puller takes about an hour when the valve body is in good shape. Older Moentrol valves take another cartridge. We carry both.

    Tub Faucets in Older Homes in Minot

    Older homes hide odd tub plumbing. Expect rusted galvanized supplies, valves with no stops, and steel spout nipples that snap. We bring transition fittings. Replacing a short run of galvanized is common.

    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in Minot

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. The old valve comes out, a pressure-balance valve goes in, and a remodel plate covers the extra holes. We usually work from behind the wall. Tile around the plate stays intact.

    Delta Tub Faucet Replacement in Minot, ND

    Delta tub valves are some of the easiest to update. Delta Monitor valves take new cartridges from the front, and the MultiChoice body accepts future trim swaps. Popular Delta cartridges are on the truck. Older Delta valves sometimes need full replacement.
