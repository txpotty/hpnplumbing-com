    Corrugated stainless steel tubing offers a flexible, efficient option for gas line installation. We make sure CSST is properly bonded and grounded, as North Dakota code requires, to protect against lightning and electrical surges in your Williston home.

    Gas Range and Cooktop Line Installation in Williston

    Switching from an electric range to gas is one of the most common reasons homeowners call us. Professional-style ranges can need 60,000 BTU or more, so we confirm the branch and main can handle the load. The appliance is then checked for proper burner flame and lighting.
