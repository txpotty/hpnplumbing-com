    Copper to PEX Conversions in Williston

    Copper lines can develop pinhole leaks from water chemistry, high velocity and sometimes soil contact under slabs. Many rural homes get water from private wells or rural water systems, and iron and sulfur odors are common complaints. Once a home has had several pinhole leaks, more often follow. Converting to PEX stops the cycle and lowers the risk of hidden water damage. We use approved transition fittings wherever new PEX connects to existing copper stubs or valves.

    PEX Main Water Line Installation in Williston

    The main water line brings water into your home, and a failing one can cause serious issues. We provide expert PEX main water line installation, replacing old, damaged lines with durable PEX. This upgrade improves water flow, reduces the risk of future leaks and offers strong resistance to ground shifting and corrosion. Our team ensures proper trenching and the connection to your municipal supply.

    PEX for Remodels & Additions in Williston, ND

    Expanding your home or remodeling a kitchen or bathroom often requires new plumbing lines. PEX is an ideal choice for these projects in Williston, ND due to its adaptability and ease of installation in existing wall cavities or new structures.     Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Williston, ND

    A manifold system sends a dedicated line from a central hub to each fixture, much like a breaker panel for water. Each line gets its own shutoff, which makes repairs easier and keeps pressure steady when multiple fixtures run. Home-run layouts use more pipe but fewer fittings in walls. We often combine manifold and trunk-and-branch designs to weigh cost and performance.

    Polybutylene Replacement With PEX in Williston, ND

    Gray polybutylene pipe was common in homes built from the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and is known for sudden failures. Chlorine in municipal water breaks down the plastic and its fittings over time. Plenty of insurers now ask about polybutylene when writing a policy. Replacing it with PEX removes that risk and can make your home easier to insure and sell.
