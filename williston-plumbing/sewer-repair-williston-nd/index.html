    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Williston, ND

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Two access holes, sometimes one. Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one and splits the old pipe outward, while cured-in-place lining pushes a resin-soaked felt tube into the old pipe and hardens it into a new wall. Either way, the driveway and the trees stay put. We tell you which one your pipe can take.

    Emergency Sewer Repair in Williston

    Raw sewage on a basement floor is not a next-week problem. We answer at night and on weekends. The first goal on an emergency call is to get the line flowing, usually with a cable or jetter, and then camera it to find out whether you have a clog or a structural failure that will back up again tomorrow.     Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Williston

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. Over decades it goes oval and blisters. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, and once the camera shows failures along the whole run, patching it stops being worth the money. PVC goes in, usually end to end. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Williston, ND

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. Fixing the joint treats the cause. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, based on the number of bad joints and the spacing between them. One or two joints is a small job. A line rooted end to end is a lining or replacement job.

    Sewer Line Leak Detection & Repair in Williston, ND

    Sewer smell outside the house points to a leak. So does an extra-green strip of lawn. We confirm it on camera, look for cracks, open joints or a broken cleanout riser, and locate it on the surface to keep the excavation tight. A small crack can sometimes be fixed with a short sectional liner, so nothing in the yard gets disturbed. Larger breaks get cut out and replaced.

    Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in Williston, ND

    A spot repair is the cheapest fix when the rest of the line is sound. Lining costs more per foot. It avoids excavation, though, and on long runs under driveways or patios the savings on concrete and landscaping often make it the cheaper total job. Full replacement is right for Orangeburg or a line broken in several places. We price all three so you can compare.

    Tree Root Invasion Solutions in Williston

    Roots in a sewer line are not the tree's fault. The pipe was leaking first. Warm, wet, nutrient-rich air escaping from a loose joint draws roots toward the line, and once they get in they thicken until they crack the pipe or separate the joint completely. We cut them out, then talk about the long-term fix. A liner or new pipe at that joint is what actually stops regrowth.
