    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Williston, ND

    Tees direct flow into and out of the trap at the correct depth. Missing or broken tees allow floating grease to escape into the sewer line. We replace damaged tees with durable PVC or other approved materials. We also check that they are set at the right depth for your unit. This is one of the most common and important grease trap repairs.

    Damaged Baffle Repair in Williston

    The baffles inside a grease trap are essential for separating FOGs from wastewater. If these baffles are corroded, broken, or dislodged, the trap's efficiency drops sharply, allowing grease into the sewer system. Our service includes inspecting the condition of all baffles and repairing or replacing them as needed, often using durable, corrosion-resistant materials. Proper baffle function is key to compliance with wastewater regulations in Williston.

    Leaking Grease Trap Repair in Williston, ND

    A leaking grease trap can cause significant property damage, foul odors, and potential health code violations. Leaks often stem from corroded tanks, cracked seams, or failing pipe connections. We use leak detection techniques to locate the source and provide targeted repairs, which may involve patching the tank, sealing connections, or replacing damaged sections of the trap or associated plumbing. Addressing leaks quickly is essential to maintain a sanitary environment and avoid costly remediation in Williston, ND.

    Grease Line Clog Repair in Williston, ND

    Grease that hardens in the lines before or after the trap can cause backups even when the trap itself is clean. We use hydro jetting to clear the pipe and remove buildup. A camera inspection helps us confirm the line is clear and check for damage. If we find broken or sagging pipe, we can repair the section. This keeps water and waste moving freely.

    Grease Trap Odor Control & Repair in Williston

    Persistent foul odors emanating from your grease trap are a sign of inefficiency, excessive FOG accumulation, or bacterial growth. Beyond cleaning, our repair services address the root cause, whether it's poor ventilation, sludge buildup, or structural issues preventing proper flow. We ensure all components are working correctly to contain and process waste effectively, eliminating unpleasant smells that can deter customers in Williston.

    Grease Interceptor Clog Removal in Williston

    The most common grease trap issue is a severe clog caused by accumulated fats, oils, and grease. Our plumbers in Williston use specialized hydro-jetting equipment to fully break down and flush out stubborn blockages from grease traps and their connecting drain lines. This method is highly effective for restoring full flow and preventing immediate recurrence, much more so than chemical drain cleaners which can damage pipes.

    Flow Control Device Repair in Williston

    Hydromechanical traps rely on a flow control fitting to keep water moving at the rated speed. If this fitting is missing or clogged, the trap can be overwhelmed and grease escapes. We inspect, clean or replace flow control devices during repair visits. We also check the air intake that allows the device to work properly. Correct flow control is essential for a trap to pass inspection.

    Lid, Gasket and Riser Repair in Williston, ND

    Damaged lids and failed gaskets let odors escape and create safety hazards. Outdoor risers can crack from vehicle traffic or ground movement. We replace lids, install new gaskets and repair or replace risers. Traffic-rated lids are installed where trucks or cars drive over the interceptor. These repairs keep odors in and keep people protected.
