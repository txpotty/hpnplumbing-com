    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Williston, ND

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already passed through the foundation walls. This usually involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and works well where exterior access is limited.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Williston

    Exterior foundation waterproofing involves excavating around the foundation to apply a waterproof membrane or coating directly to the wall. This is often the most effective method for preventing water from entering the basement or crawl space. We use high-quality asphalt-modified polyurethane membranes or polymer-based coatings that resist hydrostatic pressure and soil movement. Basements are standard in most homes, and many rely on sump pumps to manage groundwater during wet springs. Proper drainage solutions, such as French drains, are also added to divert water away.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Williston

    Many homes in Williston have crawl spaces, which are highly susceptible to moisture issues. Installing a heavy-duty vapor barrier on crawl space walls and floors is a key waterproofing strategy. This thick polyethylene sheeting prevents ground moisture from evaporating into the crawl space, protecting subflooring and joists and improving indoor air quality. It's an effective way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.
