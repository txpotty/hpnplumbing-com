    Inlet Filter and Air Intake Cleaning in Williston

    Every tankless unit has an inlet water screen that catches grit and scale flakes. Those screens can clog with sand and debris, especially after municipal line work. We pull and clean the screen and inspect the air intake filter, which can load up with dust and dryer lint. Clean filters keep the flow sensor reading accurately and the burner breathing properly.

    Isolation Valve Kit Installation in Williston, ND

    Some tankless heaters were installed without service valves, which makes proper flushing very difficult. We install a set of isolation valves with purge ports on the hot and cold lines so future maintenance takes minutes. The kit also lets you shut off water to the heater without closing the main. It is a one-time upgrade that pays for itself in simpler service.
