    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Williston

    Reverse osmosis systems remove dissolved solids, which can be high in some water supplies. We install RO units under the kitchen sink with a dedicated faucet. Some systems also connect to refrigerators or ice makers. RO pairs well with a softener for complete water treatment. We change filters on a schedule so the system keeps working well.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Williston

    Salt-based (ion exchange) water softeners are the most common and effective solution for fully softening water. These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. HPN Plumbing carefully installs many models of salt-based softeners, making sure of proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your Williston home.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Williston

    We also install Ecodyne water softeners, which are designed for solid performance and ease of use. These systems often come with user-friendly features and provide consistent soft water results. Our plumbers are familiar with Ecodyne specifications, delivering a correct and efficient installation for your Williston home.

    Residential Water Softener Installation in Williston

    Hard water can damage plumbing and appliances, leading to costly repairs. Our residential water softener installation services are designed to protect your home's entire plumbing system.     Softener Loop Installation in Williston

    A softener loop is the plumbing connection that routes your main water line through a softener. Many newer homes include a loop, but older homes often do not. We cut into the main line, install the loop and add a bypass valve. We keep outdoor hose bibs and irrigation on hard water when possible to save salt. A proper loop makes future service simple.

    Water Softener Installation Cost in Williston, ND

    Softener installation costs depend on system type, capacity and whether you need a loop. Installing on an existing loop is faster and costs less than adding a new loop. Salt-free systems and combination filters vary in price. We give you a written quote that includes equipment, labor and setup. We also explain long-term salt and maintenance costs.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Williston

    A whole-house water softener system is installed directly on your main water line, treating all the water entering your Williston home. This means every faucet, shower and appliance receives soft water. Our installation process involves carefully integrating the system into your existing plumbing, including necessary bypass valves and pressure regulators, to provide complete protection against hard water throughout your property.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Williston

    Accurate testing is essential for proper softener settings. We test your water for hardness, iron and chlorine before installation. Then we program regeneration frequency and salt dosage. This helps reduce salt use while keeping water soft. We review settings with you so you understand how the system works.
