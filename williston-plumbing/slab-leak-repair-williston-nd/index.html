    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Williston

    Old galvanized or thin copper is hard to patch reliably. Rerouting skips it. The bad run stays in the ground unused while a new line goes overhead or in the walls, where it can be reached later. Williston homes with original plumbing see this often.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Williston

    Cold leaks don't make warm spots. The bill gives them away.     Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Williston, ND

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Heat is the reason. Each time hot water runs, the pipe expands against concrete and gravel, and recirculation pumps keep that cycle going all day, which grinds the pipe wall thin at the rub points. We repair or reroute the line and can adjust the pump to cut wear.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Williston

    Older copper under a slab tends to leak again. Rerouting ends that. We leave the leaking run in the ground and pull new PEX through the attic and down the walls, cutting openings where they are easiest to patch, often behind a vanity or in a closet.
