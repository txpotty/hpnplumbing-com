    Basement plumbing leak repairs in Mandan typically cost $200 to $1,500, depending on the pipe and how hard it is to reach. Exposed pipe on an unfinished ceiling is cheap.     Plumbing leaks show up no matter the weather. Groundwater leaks track the rain. A moving meter with everything off means a pipe leak, and water that shows up after storms means groundwater.

    Start outside. Keep gutters clean and extend downspouts at least six feet from the foundation.     Crack repairs use two-part polyurethane or epoxy injection resin, depending on whether the crack is actively wet. Polyurethane handles wet cracks. Our interior drains use rigid perforated pipe, which keeps its slope and can be jetted later.

    Some small leaks are fine to handle yourself, like tightening a washer hose or replacing a leaking supply connector.     Most plumbing leak repairs in a basement are done in one visit. Expect two to four hours with drywall access.
