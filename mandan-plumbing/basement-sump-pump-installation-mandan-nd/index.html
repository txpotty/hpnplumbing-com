    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Mandan

    Pedestal sump pumps are an excellent choice for narrow or shallow sump pits, as their motor sits above the water level. This design makes them easier to maintain and can sometimes offer a longer lifespan because the motor stays out of the water. HPN Plumbing can properly install pedestal sump pumps in your Mandan home, with the right sizing and setup for effective water removal without crowding your pit.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Mandan

    Power outages during heavy storms can leave a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump provides vital protection when the power goes out. We install reliable backup systems that activate automatically, keeping water moving even without electricity. This is an important safeguard for homes in Mandan that lose power during storms, offering peace of mind in severe weather. We integrate these systems seamlessly with your existing plumbing.

    Primary Sump Pump Replacement in Mandan

    If your existing sump pump is old, failing or no longer adequate for your home, HPN Plumbing offers expert primary sump pump replacement.     Sump Pump Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Mandan, ND

    A high water alarm is an affordable part that gives you warning before water reaches the floor.     Pedestal Sump Pumps in Mandan

    Pedestal pumps keep the motor above the pit, which makes them easier to service and a solid fit for narrow pits in older homes. They usually last a long time because the motor never sits in water. The tradeoff is more noise and a less finished look, so we usually recommend them for utility rooms rather than living spaces. We install them with a rigid discharge, a quality check valve and a float rod adjusted to your pit depth.

    Sump Pump Basin and Discharge Line Installation in Mandan

    A complete sump pump system requires a properly installed basin and discharge line. We handle the excavation and placement of the sump basin, setting it at the lowest point of your basement floor so it collects water well. We also install the discharge line, ensuring it is correctly sloped, sealed and routed away from your foundation to a suitable drainage area, so water does not find its way back into your Mandan property.

    Commercial and Multi-Unit Sump Installations in Mandan

    Office buildings, parking structures and multi-unit properties with below-grade levels usually need duplex sump systems with two pumps and an alternating control panel. The panel switches lead and lag pumps on each cycle so wear stays even, and a high water alarm alerts building staff. We coordinate electrical with your electrician and follow manufacturer specs for basin size and float settings. Property managers get a clear maintenance schedule to keep both pumps exercised.
