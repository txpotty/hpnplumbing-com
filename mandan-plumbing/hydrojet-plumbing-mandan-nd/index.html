    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Mandan

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under floors and slabs. Snaking clears a path, but the grease soon builds back. Jetting with a rotating nozzle peels that layer off and flushes it downstream. Homeowners who cook often and restaurants benefit the most from regular kitchen line jetting.

    Storm and Yard Drain Jetting in Mandan, ND

    Yard drains, channel drains and downspout lines fill with sand, silt and leaves between storms. When the rain comes, a blocked line sends water back toward the house. A working sump pump with a battery backup is important for basement homes during the spring thaw. We jet these lines clear before the rainy season or after a big storm. Clean storm lines carry runoff away from foundations the way they were meant to.

    Commercial and Restaurant Hydrojetting in Mandan
