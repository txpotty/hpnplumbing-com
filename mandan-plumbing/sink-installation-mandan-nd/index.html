    Kitchen Sink Replacement in Mandan

    Pulling the old kitchen sink is half the job.     Commercial Restroom Sink Installation in Mandan

    Vessel Sink Installation in Mandan, ND

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. That raises the rim several inches, so we either pick a taller vessel faucet or mount a wall faucet, and we use a vessel drain that matches the bowl's overflow or lack of one. No overflow means no slotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Leak-Proof Sink Connections in Mandan, ND

    Every joint gets tested before we leave. We fill the bowl to the overflow, pull the stopper, and watch every nut under the cabinet at full flow with a flashlight and a dry paper towel. Paper towels catch the smallest drip. Pipe dope goes on threads, not compression fittings.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Mandan

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. We set the bowl with the clips or anchors the stone shop provides, run a continuous bead of 100 percent silicone around the rim, and leave it braced while the adhesive cures overnight. Adhesive by itself will not hold it. Plumbing goes on the next day.

    Commercial Sink Installation in Mandan, ND

    Commercial kitchens need sinks the health inspector will sign off on. A three-bay sink drains indirectly through an air gap to a floor sink, and hand sinks need tempered water near each prep area. Plan review comments come first. Mop basins and grease interceptor ties are part of it.

    Drop-In Sink Installation in Mandan, ND

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim covers small flaws in the cutout, so a laminate counter can take a new self-rimming sink with only a check of the hole size and a bead of silicone or putty as the maker specifies. Clips pull it tight to the counter. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.
