    Pipe Repair and Repipes in Mandan, ND

    Pinhole leaks and rust-colored water from the tap usually point to supply pipe near the end of its working life in an older house. A single leak gets a clean copper splice or a PEX transition. More than one leak usually means repiping. A pipe split by a hard freeze gets cut out and replaced, then pressure-tested.

    Water Heater Repair and Replacement in Mandan, ND

    Thermocouples, gas valves, elements and relief valves are the common water heater repairs. Old tanks get hauled off.

    Repiping and Supply Line Replacement in Mandan

    Polybutylene, the gray plastic pipe used for years in home construction, fails from the inside without warning.
