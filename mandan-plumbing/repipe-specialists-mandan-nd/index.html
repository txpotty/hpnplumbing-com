    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Mandan

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Frost heave can shift soil around sewer lines and misalign joints, which often shows up as slow drains or backups. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use advanced detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Repipe Specialists in Mandan

    The cost of a whole-home repipe in Mandan varies significantly depending on the size of your home, the number of fixtures, and the chosen material (PEX or copper).     Both PEX and copper are excellent choices for repiping in North Dakota, each with distinct advantages. PEX is more flexible, resistant to freezing, and generally less expensive, making it a great option for many homes. Copper is highly durable, has a longer lifespan, and naturally inhibits bacterial growth, but costs more. We can discuss which material best suits your budget and needs.

    Many rural homes get water from private wells or rural water systems, and iron and sulfur odors are common complaints. Hard water can lead to mineral buildup, or scale, inside pipes over time. This buildup constricts water flow, reduces pressure, and can accelerate corrosion, especially in older metal pipes. Repiping with materials like PEX or properly installed copper, along with considering a water softener, can reduce these effects and extend the life of your plumbing system.

    Copper is a rigid, durable metal with a long lifespan, known for its resistance to bacteria. PEX is a flexible plastic tubing that is more affordable, easier to install, resistant to freezing and corrosion, and very quiet. Both are excellent choices for repiping, and our specialists can help you decide which material is best suited for your home and budget in Mandan, ND.
