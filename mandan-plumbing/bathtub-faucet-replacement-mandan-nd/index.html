    Dripping Tub Faucet Replacement in Mandan

    Drips at the spout come from inside the valve. On a single-handle faucet the cartridge seals have worn, and on a two- or three-handle faucet the stem washers and brass seats have worn, and either way the drip gets worse every month. Replacement parts stop it. Cracked bodies get a new valve.

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Mandan

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. A worn 1222 causes drips or weak hot water, and replacing it takes about an hour with a puller. Moentrol valves use a different part. We carry both.

    Thermostatic and Pressure-Balance Valves in Mandan, ND

    Pressure-balance valves react to pressure drops. When someone flushes a toilet, the valve cuts hot and cold flow in proportion so the mix stays steady, and a thermostatic valve goes further by holding a set temperature even as the water heater cools. Both meet anti-scald code. Thermostatic costs more.

    Emergency Tub Faucet Service in Mandan, ND

    A tub that will not shut off needs water off now. Check behind the trim plate or an access panel for stops, and if there are none, shut the main and leave the drain open. We send the next open plumber. Broken stems are a common cause.

    Delta Tub Faucet Replacement in Mandan, ND

    Delta tub valves are some of the easiest to update. Delta Monitor valves take new cartridges from the front, and the MultiChoice body accepts future trim swaps. We stock common Delta cartridges. Older Delta valves sometimes need full replacement.

    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Mandan

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. A single-handle valve often takes new trim and a cartridge from the front, while a three-handle valve usually has to be cut out of the wall and replaced with a modern pressure-balance body. We check before quoting. Two-handle models fall in between.

    Hard Water Tub Faucet Replacement in Mandan

    Water in North Dakota is often very hard, with high mineral and sulfate content that builds scale in water heaters and fixtures. Scale packs into cartridges and spout diverters until handles stiffen and the diverter will not lift. Cleaning helps lightly scaled valves, but pitted brass or a fused cartridge means replacing the faucet. We pull a cartridge to check.
