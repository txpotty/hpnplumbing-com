    Softener Cleaning and Testing in Mandan

    Hard water scale builds in pipes and appliances fast. Brine, injector, resin and regeneration all get checked, and we test the water at a tap. Resin cleaner helps. Older resin may need replacing.

    Water Heater Flush and Inspection in Mandan

    Popping or rumbling means sediment. Hard water drops minerals that settle on the bottom of the tank, where they trap heat, waste fuel and make that crackling sound every time the burner or elements fire. We flush it out. Tankless units get a descaling flush through the service valves with a pump and descaler. Records support warranty claims.

    Softener Upkeep in Mandan, ND

    Softeners fail quietly. A unit that stopped regenerating or ran out of salt keeps sending water to the house, it just is not soft anymore, and you will not notice until scale shows up on the faucets and the water heater starts popping. Bypass and drain get tested. Then we test the treated water.

    Drain Cleaning as Maintenance in Mandan

    Slow drains rarely fix themselves. Camera checks and cleaning on problem lines keep grease and roots from causing a holiday backup. We jet greasy lines. Rooted lines get regular cutting.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Mandan

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale builds up inside the heat exchanger, especially with hard water, and the unit starts throwing error codes, running lukewarm or cutting out mid-shower long before it actually wears out. A small pump circulates descaler. The inlet filter, venting and gas line get checked too.

    Sump Pump Testing in Mandan, ND

    Basements flood when the pump fails during a storm. A working sump pump with a battery backup is essential for basement homes during the spring thaw. We fill the pit to test the float, check the discharge line and confirm the check valve holds. Backup pumps get tested too.
