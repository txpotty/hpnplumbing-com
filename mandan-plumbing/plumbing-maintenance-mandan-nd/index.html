    Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Mandan, ND

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits greatly from annual maintenance. We check burner operation, flue venting, and potential gas leaks on gas models, and inspect electrical connections and heating elements on electric units. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to premature tank failure.

    Water Softener Maintenance in Mandan

    Water softeners fight hard water, protecting your pipes and appliances from mineral scale. Our maintenance service includes checking salt levels, cleaning the brine tank, confirming the regeneration cycle works correctly, and inspecting valves and resin beds. Proper softener maintenance extends its life and keeps soft water flowing to your home, preventing expensive scale damage.

    Drain Line Inspections for Maintenance in Mandan

    As part of complete plumbing maintenance, we offer drain line camera inspections. This lets us see the condition of your main sewer lines, identifying potential blockages from roots or grease buildup, or structural issues like cracks and offsets made worse by shifting soil. Frost heave can shift soil around sewer lines and crack joints, which often shows up as slow drains or backups. Early detection through inspection can prevent severe backups and expensive emergency repairs.

    Leak Detection and Slab Leak Checks in Mandan

    Newer homes around Mandan typically use PEX supply lines, which handle freezing better than rigid pipe and are simpler to repair. Soil movement and hard water can wear buried or under-slab water lines until they leak. We check your water meter for hidden flow, test fixture valves and use acoustic and thermal tools to find leaks. Warm spots on floors, a running meter and unexplained high water bills are common signs. Finding a leak early prevents slab damage and mold.
