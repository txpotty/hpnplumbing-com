    Water in North Dakota is often very hard, with high mineral and sulfate content that leaves deposits in water heaters and fixtures.     Drain snaking uses a flexible auger to physically break through or pull out blockages. Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the inside of pipes, effectively removing scale, grease, and roots, providing a much more complete cleaning.     While chemical drain cleaners are widely available, HPN Plumbing generally advises against them for homes in Mandan.
