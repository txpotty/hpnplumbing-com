    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Mandan, ND

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. If scale comes back or pressure drops, the media or the prefilter is usually the problem. HPN Plumbing repairs these units in Mandan, ND. Media lasts a few years.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Mandan

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. Older systems on chlorinated city water see it most. HPN Plumbing removes the old resin and installs fresh, high-capacity media. The softener works like new.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Mandan

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. A failed timer motor, a bad circuit board or a stuck meter turbine are the usual causes, along with settings lost in a power outage. Each part gets tested. Then we program the valve for your household and hardness, and soft water returns by the next cycle.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Mandan, ND

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Its small ports clog with sediment and salt. A clogged injector leaves the brine tank full of water and the resin never gets recharged. We clean or replace it and check the brine line for air leaks.
