    Septic Tee Repair in Mandan

    Effluent Pump Replacement in Mandan, ND

    Effluent pumps in a dosing chamber last roughly 8 to 15 years, and they usually quit without much warning at all. A pump that hums but moves no water has a stuck impeller or a bad capacitor. Our tech pulls the old unit and checks the discharge before the new one goes in. Floats get replaced at the same time.
