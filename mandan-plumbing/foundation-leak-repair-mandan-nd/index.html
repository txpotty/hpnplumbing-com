    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Mandan

    Slab leaks show up quietly. A warm tile, a higher bill. HPN Plumbing isolates the hot and cold sides, then uses acoustic and infrared gear to pin down the break under your concrete.     PEX and PVC Foundation Leak Repair in Mandan

    Newer houses use PEX or PVC under the slab. It still leaks. Usually the culprit is a fitting that was not crimped all the way or a glued joint that separated when the ground shifted. HPN Plumbing replaces the fitting with a proper crimp, expansion or solvent-weld connection so the line under your Mandan foundation is sound again.

    Leak Detection Tools We Use in Mandan, ND

    Infrared cameras, acoustic listening gear and tracer gas cover most leaks we chase in Mandan, ND. Gas helps when the leak is too quiet to hear. A hydrogen and nitrogen blend goes into the empty pipe, and a sniffer picks it up where it rises through the slab, even when the leak is silent.

    Crawl Space Foundation Leak Repair in Mandan, ND

    Crawl spaces hide leaks from supply lines, drains and AC condensate lines. Sagging, wet insulation is a common clue. Water sitting under the house rots sill plates and floor joists and feeds mold on the subfloor, so we find the source, fix the pipe and check whether the vapor barrier needs replacing. A sump pump may make sense in a Mandan, ND crawl space that floods from outside.

    Water Line Reroute in Mandan

    A reroute leaves the old pipe under the slab and runs new line through the attic or walls. No concrete gets broken. We usually run PEX because it bends around framing and does not corrode, and the drywall openings are cut small, often just a few squares at fixture walls, so patching goes fast.
