    Condensing tankless heaters, such as many Navien models, extract extra heat from exhaust and produce condensate.     Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Mandan

    Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside the heat exchanger, limiting flow and causing overheating. Many units warn of this with scale codes, such as the LC code on Rinnai models.
