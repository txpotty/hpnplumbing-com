    Disc Faucet Repair in Bismarck

    Ceramic disc faucets are known for their durability and are a popular choice in many contemporary Bismarck bathrooms. While generally reliable, they can develop leaks if the ceramic discs become scratched or misaligned, or if the inlet and outlet seals wear out. Our plumbers have the expertise to carefully disassemble these precise mechanisms, clean or replace the ceramic discs, and reseal the faucet to stop leaks and restore full performance.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Bismarck, ND

    Ball faucets, recognizable by their single lever that moves over a rounded cap, are common in many kitchens. They use a rotating ball joint to control water flow and temperature. If your ball faucet is leaking from the spout or base, or if the handle feels loose, it's probably time for a repair. We can replace the spring-loaded rubber seats and O-rings, which are usually the culprits, restoring smooth operation and preventing drips.

    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Bismarck, ND

    Many modern single-handle faucets, often found in newer homes, use a cartridge system. When these fail, you might experience inconsistent water temperature, reduced flow, or difficulty turning the faucet on or off. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing cartridge issues and replacing them with the correct manufacturer-specific part, so your faucet works like new. We work with all major brands to provide precise repairs.

    Kitchen Faucet Repair in Bismarck

    Kitchen faucets endure heavy daily use and are prone to specific issues like sprayer malfunctions, clogged aerators, or swivel problems.     Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Bismarck

    Weak or sputtering flow is often caused by mineral buildup in the aerator or cartridge. We remove and clean or replace the aerator, flush the lines and check the cartridge for scale blockage. On pull-down faucets, the spray head and check valves can also clog. If flow is low at every fixture, we test whole-home water pressure.
