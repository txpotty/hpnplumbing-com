    Pipe Bursting Services in Bismarck, ND

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. New pipe follows the old path. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, while a continuous HDPE pipe is pulled in behind at the same diameter or bigger. It works on badly damaged lines. Lining cannot do that.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Bismarck

    Polybutylene was used in a lot of homes built in the 1980s and early 1990s. It splits suddenly. The gray plastic turns brittle as chlorine in the water attacks it, with failures most common at fittings and tight bends. Replacement is the only lasting fix. We use PEX or copper.

    Trenchless Underground Pipe Repair in Bismarck
