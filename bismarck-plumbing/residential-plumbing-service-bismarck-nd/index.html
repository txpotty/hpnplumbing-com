    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Bismarck

    Slab leaks hide under concrete until something gives them away. Frost heave can shift soil around sewer lines and crack joints, which often shows up as slow drains or backups. We locate them with acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera, so the only concrete we break is the small patch right over the leak, not half the kitchen floor. Then you pick the fix. Options include a spot repair through the slab or a reroute through the attic.

    Faucet and Fixture Repair in Bismarck

    Most dripping faucets need a cartridge, not a new faucet. The truck has cartridges for the big brands, Moen and Delta included, and when you do want new fixtures we install them, connect new braided supply lines, and check the angle stops under the sink while we are there. Old angle stops often break when turned.

    Drain Cleaning and Sewer Camera Service in Bismarck

    Wipes are not flushable, whatever the package says. Most slow drains come down to grease, hair or wipes, and for a main line backup we cable it open, then run a camera to look for roots, offset joints or a belly where water sits in the pipe. You get the video.

    Pipe Repair and Repiping in Bismarck, ND

    Pinhole leaks and rust-colored water from the tap usually point to supply pipe near the end of its working life in an older house. A single leak gets a clean copper splice or a PEX transition. More than one leak usually means repiping. Freeze splits get the same treatment, cut out and replaced with new pipe rated for the job.

    Water Heater Repair and Replacement in Bismarck, ND

    Thermocouples, gas valves, elements and relief valves are the common water heater repairs. We service Rheem, Bradford White, Navien and Rinnai units, and when a tank is leaking from the shell we replace it with proper venting, an expansion tank and a drain pan, usually the same day. Old tanks get hauled off.
