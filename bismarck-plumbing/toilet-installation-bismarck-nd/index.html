    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Bismarck, ND

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, with the tank hidden behind an access plate. They free up floor space and make cleaning simple, but they need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep or a furred-out chase. We install Geberit, Grohe and Kohler carrier systems and set the bowl height to your preference. This work is easiest during a remodel when the wall is open.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Bismarck, ND

    Comfort height toilets are a common choice for their accessibility, offering a seat closer to chair height. Installing these requires precise measurement and secure anchoring.     Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Installation in Bismarck

    TOTO, Kohler and American Standard consistently perform well in flush tests and have good parts availability. Gerber and Mansfield are solid budget options. We help you match the brand and model to your needs.

    HPN Plumbing stands behind our workmanship. We offer a warranty on all our toilet installations, covering labor and making sure your new toilet works correctly. Specific warranty details will be included with your service agreement.

    Installing a new toilet can offer several benefits, including improved water efficiency, reduced water bills, better flushing power, a better look, and increased comfort. Modern toilets are also designed to be more durable and simpler to clean.

    While DIY toilet installation is possible, hiring a professional makes sure the job is done correctly. Professionals have the tools and expertise to prevent leaks, get proper sealing, and comply with local plumbing codes, saving you potential headaches and expensive repairs down the road.

    Replacing a toilet in the same location generally does not need a permit, but new bathrooms, additions and drain relocations typically do. Requirements vary by city, and we check with the local building department for your project.
