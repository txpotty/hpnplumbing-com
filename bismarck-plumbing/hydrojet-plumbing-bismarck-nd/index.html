    Same-Day Jetting for Backups in Bismarck

    Sewage in a tub or floor drain means the main is blocked. Hold off on running water. Our technician opens the line, then jets it to remove the cause, and a camera pass after jetting shows whether the backup came from buildup alone or from a broken or root-filled section that needs repair.

    Scheduled Jetting Maintenance in Bismarck

    Properties with chronic drain issues do better on a jetting schedule. Commercial kitchens often jet quarterly. Root-prone home sewers usually jet every year or two, with camera records to track regrowth.

    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Bismarck, ND

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. Jetting cleans the whole pipe. For a single clog in an otherwise clean line, snaking is cheaper and works fine, but for a line that clogs every few months, jetting removes the buildup that keeps catching debris.

    Sewer Camera Inspection Before Hydrojetting in Bismarck

    Jetting a broken pipe can make the break worse. We camera every line before we set up the jetter. The video shows the pipe material, the condition of each joint, and any cracks, offsets or sags, which tells us the nozzle and pressure to use or whether we should not jet that line at all.
