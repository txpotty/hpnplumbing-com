    Every Kind of Copper Leak, Repaired in Bismarck, ND

    Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in Bismarck

    Pinholes form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. Hot water speeds it up. They often appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher, so we cut out the affected section and replace it with Type L copper or a press fitting. More pinholes often follow.

    Copper Pipe Joint Leak Repair in Bismarck, ND

    Joints leak from poor original soldering, thermal expansion or a house that settles. Frost heave can shift soil around sewer lines and crack joints, which often shows up as slow drains or backups. Look for white crust. We inspect each joint near the leak for weeping or mineral buildup, then clean, reflux and resolder the joint, or replace the fitting entirely with a new sweat or press fitting. We test before closing up.

    Copper Fitting and Valve Repair in Bismarck

    Angle stops, shutoff valves and copper fittings under sinks and behind toilets can weep or seize. Scale locks them up. Hard water buildup inside a valve can keep it from closing when you need it most, and quarter-turn valves go in to replace the old multi-turn stops. We test each one.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Bismarck

    Pinholes start inside the pipe where the copper wall pits and thins. You see a drip or a stain. They are small and easy to miss behind drywall, which is why we use thermal imaging to find them before we open anything. We replace the pitted length with Type L. We look at the copper around it.

    Solder Joint Leak Repair in Bismarck, ND

    Older solder joints can fail from poor original work, thermal expansion or vibration. Look for white or green crust. A leaking joint usually weeps for a while before it drips, and we clean, reflux and resolder the joint or replace the fitting with new pipe on each side when the old copper is too thin. The joint gets tested.

    Commercial Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Bismarck

    Commercial buildings often run larger diameter copper for domestic water. Water-off time hurts. We repair copper supply lines in offices, restaurants and apartment buildings, and we use press fittings on bigger pipe so the repair goes faster and needs no open flame near occupied spaces. We schedule around business hours.

    Hot Water Copper Line Repair in Bismarck, ND

    Hot water lines corrode faster than cold lines. Heat speeds it up. Recirculation pumps that run around the clock push water through the copper at high velocity and wear it from the inside, which is why we often add a timer to the pump after fixing the leak. It slows the next pinhole.

    Burst Copper Pipe Repair in Bismarck, ND

    Copper in an exterior wall or attic can split when the water inside freezes. Frozen pipes are a serious and regular winter risk in North Dakota, and a single cold night can split a line in an exterior wall. Shut the main first. The split often does not leak until the ice thaws, which is why many bursts are found hours after the cold. We cut out the split and solder in new pipe. Then we add insulation.
