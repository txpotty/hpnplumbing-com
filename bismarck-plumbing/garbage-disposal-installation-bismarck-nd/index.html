    High-Horsepower Disposal Installation in Bismarck

    A 3/4 or 1 HP unit is bigger and heavier than a starter model, so we measure the cabinet first.     Air Switch Disposal Installation in Bismarck, ND

    Air switches start the disposal with a button on the sink or counter. The button pushes air through a tube to a control box in the cabinet, which keeps electricity away from the button and avoids a new wall switch. Islands use them often.

    Dishwasher Connection With Disposal Installation in Bismarck

    New disposals come with a knockout plug in the dishwasher inlet. We knock it out with a screwdriver and hammer, fish the plug out of the chamber and connect the hose with a new clamp. Forgetting the plug floods the dishwasher. We also install an air gap or high loop, so dirty sink water cannot flow back into the dishwasher while the disposal is draining.

    Disposal Rough-In for New Construction in Bismarck

    At rough-in we set the drain height and the outlet location before the cabinets go in. For a kitchen with a disposal, we usually set the drain stub lower than a plain sink would need, so the trap can sit below the disposal outlet with room for proper fall. A switched outlet goes in at this stage. We mark its spot so it clears the trap.

    High-Capacity Disposal Installation in Bismarck, ND

    Busy kitchens get the most from a 1 HP unit, like an InSinkErator Evolution or a Waste King Legend model.     Disposal Installation on an Existing Sink in Bismarck

    With a typical 1/2 or 3/4 HP unit, we remove the old unit if there is one and install the new one on the existing sink. Cramped cabinets take some planning. Sometimes a compact model is the better fit.

    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in Bismarck, ND

We lay out the drain so both bowls drain freely and the disposal outlet does not push water into the other bowl, which happens when the tee sits too close to the disposal discharge. We use heavy-wall fittings.
