    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Bismarck

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Older homes may still have galvanized supply lines or cast iron drains, which can corrode and restrict flow with age. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. Acoustic gear and a thermal camera tell us where it is. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or skip the old under-slab pipe and repipe overhead with PEX. You get a price on every option.

    Commercial Water Line Services in Bismarck, ND

    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Bismarck

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. Straight runs are ideal. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, and a length of HDPE trails behind it in the same path. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.
