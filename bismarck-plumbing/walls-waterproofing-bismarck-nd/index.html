    How We Keep Water Out of Walls in Bismarck, ND

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Bismarck

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, a membrane over cement board or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. We reinforce corners, niches and valve penetrations with fabric or preformed pieces.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Bismarck

    Older homes may still have galvanized supply lines or cast iron drains, which can corrode and restrict flow with age. Any wall below grade sits in soil that gets saturated after heavy rain or snowmelt, and that water pushes against the wall with steady hydrostatic pressure. We inject cracks, coat the wall inside or out, and add drain tile wherever water collects at the footing. A sump goes in if groundwater sits high. Some basements never need a sump.
