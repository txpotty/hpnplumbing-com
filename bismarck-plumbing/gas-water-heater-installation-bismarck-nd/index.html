    Proper Sizing and Venting in Bismarck, ND

    Sizing and venting decide whether a gas heater runs safe and efficient. We size the heater from your peak use, then match the vent to the manufacturer table and code. A small vent can backdraft. We test it every time.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Bismarck

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They are common in older homes and usually the least expensive gas option, but they only work if the vent has the right size, slope and clearance to combustibles. Old vents get checked. Corroded or undersized pipe gets replaced.

    Bradford White Gas Water Heater Installation in Bismarck

    Bradford White sells only through plumbers, and we install a lot of them. Thick steel, good parts. Code-approved installs every time.

    Propane Water Heater Installation in Bismarck

    Propane homes need heaters set up for propane, not natural gas. Propane runs at a different pressure and needs a smaller burner orifice, so we install propane-specific models or convert units with the manufacturer kit where the manual allows it. We work with your supplier. Every joint is leak tested.

    Gas Line Upgrades for Water Heaters in Bismarck, ND

    Moving up to a bigger or tankless gas heater can outgrow the existing gas line.
