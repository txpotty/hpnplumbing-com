    Electric Water Heater Installation in Bismarck, ND

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire.     Expansion Tank Installation in Bismarck, ND

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. Heated water then has no way back to the street, so pressure rises and works on the relief valve, supply lines and toilet fill valves. An expansion tank absorbs it. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.
