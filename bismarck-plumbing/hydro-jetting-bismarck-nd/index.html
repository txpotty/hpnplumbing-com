    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in Bismarck

    For most modern and well-maintained pipes, hydro jetting is very safe. However, for very old, corroded, or severely damaged pipes, the high pressure could cause further damage.     Hydro jetting is exceptionally effective against almost all types of clogs. It can break through solid obstructions like tree roots, flush out heavy grease and food buildup, clear mineral scale, and remove compacted sludge and debris that traditional snaking often misses.

    A thorough jetting often keeps a line clear for a year or more, depending on use and roots. Lines with heavy root intrusion may require maintenance or a repair.

    HPN Plumbing uses professional-grade hydro jetting equipment from established manufacturers like Spartan Tool and MyTana. These brands are known for their reliability, powerful performance, and safety features, so we can deliver the most effective cleaning for your pipes.

    Generally, a permit is not required for standard hydro jetting aimed at clearing blockages in existing lines.
