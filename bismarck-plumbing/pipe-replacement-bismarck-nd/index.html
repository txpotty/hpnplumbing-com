    Many older homes in Bismarck, ND still have galvanized steel pipes, which are prone to internal corrosion over time.     PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a popular choice for modern pipe replacement projects in Bismarck due to its flexibility, durability, and resistance to corrosion and freezing. HPN Plumbing carefully installs PEX piping for full home repipes or targeted section replacements, providing a reliable and long-lasting plumbing solution that handles local water conditions well.
