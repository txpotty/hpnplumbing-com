    Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Bismarck

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. It runs while the switch is on. A batch feed unit needs its stopper locked in to run, keeping hands out of the chamber, and it needs no wall switch. Each type gets wired its own way.

    Badger Disposal Install in Bismarck

    Most replacement disposals in area kitchens are a Badger. A 1/3 to 3/4 HP Badger handles normal scraps and fits almost any sink base cabinet.     Commercial Disposer Installation in Bismarck, ND

    Restaurant disposals are a different class of machine. A commercial disposer runs 1 to 5 HP on its own circuit, and many come with a wall control panel and a water solenoid.
