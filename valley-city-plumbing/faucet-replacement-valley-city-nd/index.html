    Frequently Asked Questions About Faucet Replacement in Valley City

    Labor for a faucet replacement usually runs $150 to $350. With a solid mid-range faucet included, most jobs total somewhere between $300 and $750, depending on the model and the condition of the stops. High-end and touchless models can top $800.

    Yes, and it is the best time to do it. You can move to a pull-down, a different finish or a touchless model, as long as the new faucet fits your sink's hole count and spacing. We confirm the fit before you buy.

    Water in North Dakota is often very hard, with high mineral and sulfate content that builds scale in water heaters and fixtures. Scale coats the inside of the cartridge and the aerator screen, so flow drops and the seals wear out early. Handles get stiff too. Faucets with ceramic disc cartridges hold up better, especially if the aerator gets cleaned now and then.

    About an hour to ninety minutes for most. Seized faucets and shutoff valve replacements add time, usually twenty to forty minutes each.
