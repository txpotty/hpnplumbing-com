    Solar Well Pump Systems in Valley City, ND

    Remote properties and livestock wells often benefit from solar-powered pumps. Solar can be a sensible option wherever a site gets steady sun. We install solar pump systems with controllers and storage tanks. These systems reduce energy costs and work off-grid. We size panels and pumps to your water needs.

    Jet Pump Replacement in Valley City, ND

    Jet pumps are typically used for shallower wells and are located above ground, drawing water up through a suction pipe. Common issues include motor failure, impeller damage or pressure switch malfunctions. HPN Plumbing provides expert jet pump replacement with correct sizing and pressure tank integration. We make sure your new jet pump delivers steady water pressure throughout your Valley City, ND property.

    Constant Pressure Pump Systems in Valley City

    Constant pressure systems use a variable frequency drive to adjust pump speed and keep pressure steady. Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive systems are common choices. They eliminate the pressure swings of traditional systems. They can also reduce wear on the pump. We help you decide if the extra cost is worth paying.

    Agricultural and Ranch Well Pumps in Valley City

    Larger properties may need higher-horsepower pumps for irrigation or livestock. We replace pumps sized for higher flow and deeper wells. Three-phase pumps may require dedicated controls. We coordinate with your schedule to limit downtime. We also check storage tanks and distribution lines.

    Drop Pipe and Wire Replacement in Valley City

    Old galvanized drop pipe can corrode and leak, causing pressure loss. Damaged wire can cause motor failure. When we pull the pump, we inspect both. We replace them with durable materials like schedule 120 PVC or poly pipe as appropriate. This helps the new pump last longer.

    Control Box and Pressure Switch Replacement in Valley City, ND

    Not every well failure means a bad pump. Failed capacitors, relays or pressure switches can stop the system. We test these components first and replace them if needed. This can save you the cost of a pump pull. We explain what we find before making recommendations.
