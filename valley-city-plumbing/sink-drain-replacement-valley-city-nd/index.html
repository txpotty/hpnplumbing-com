    Utility Sink Drain Replacement in Valley City

    Laundry tubs see lint and paint water. Over time that wears the thin plastic drain and trap on a utility sink until it cracks, and lint from the washer standpipe often builds up in the trap so badly that it never drains at full speed again. We fit a 1-1/2 inch trap with a cleanout. A lint trap on the washer hose helps.

    Corroded Drain Replacement in Valley City, ND

    Drain cleaners speed up corrosion. Caustic products sit in the bottom of a metal trap between uses and eat at the chrome plating and the thin brass underneath, which is why so many old traps we pull have pinholes right at the bottom of the bend. Plastic traps resist that damage. We recommend it for most cabinets.

    Push-Button Drain Installation in Valley City, ND

    Push-button drains get rid of the lift rod. They open and close with a press on the cap, and we check that the sink has an overflow before choosing a slotted body, since a slotted drain in a bowl without an overflow leaks out the slots. They clean easier too. Hair still collects, so pull the cap now and then.

    Vessel and Grid Drain Replacement in Valley City

    Vessel sinks use a different drain. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted grid or umbrella drain, or water leaks out the slots. We see the wrong drain often. Swapping it takes under an hour.

    Bathroom Drain Upgrade in Valley City, ND

    Old bath drains often carry decades of hair and scum. A new pop-up and trap get rid of the rough walls hair clings to, and the bowl empties in seconds. Clogs come back slower. A hair catcher keeps it that way.

    Bathroom Sink Drain Replacement in Valley City, ND

    Bathroom drains wear out at the pop-up. The pivot nut packing leaks, the drain body corrodes around the overflow holes, and the stopper stops sealing, so a sink that will not hold water usually needs a whole new pop-up assembly. Half-fixes do not hold here. The new drain matches your faucet finish.
