    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Valley City, ND

    Many homes have tankless heaters mounted on an exterior wall with no venting at all, since the unit exhausts directly outside. These units face sun, rain and temperature swings, which can break down plastic covers and wiring over time. Frozen pipes are a serious and regular winter risk in North Dakota, and a single cold night can split a line in an exterior wall. When we replace them, we check the pipe cover, freeze protection settings and the electrical connection. We can add a protective pipe cover kit to guard the water and gas lines. Exterior models are often the simplest swap when the gas line is already sized correctly.

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Valley City

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE series or Rinnai Sensei reach efficiencies above 90 percent by capturing heat from exhaust gases. They vent through inexpensive PVC and produce acidic condensate that must be neutralized and drained properly. We install a neutralizer cartridge and route the condensate line to an approved drain. Big swings between summer heat and winter cold make pipes expand and contract, which slowly loosens fittings over the years. Condensing units still save gas because they work year-round on showers, laundry and dishes. We explain the cost difference so you can decide if the efficiency gain is worth the price.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Valley City, ND

    Converting from a 40 or 50 gallon tank to tankless frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss in a garage or closet. The catch is that tankless units often need a larger gas line, often three quarter inch or bigger, plus new venting. We check your meter size and pipe run before quoting so there are no surprises mid-job. We also cap or reroute the old flue and seal the roof penetration if it is no longer used. Plan for a conversion to take a full day.

    Like-for-Like Tankless Swaps in Valley City

    When your existing tankless has a cracked heat exchanger or a failed control board that is not worth repairing, a direct replacement is often the quickest fix. We match the new unit to your current gas line, venting type and mounting location whenever possible. Many Rinnai and Navien models share similar footprints, which keeps wall patching to a minimum. We also replace the old service valves if they are corroded or stuck. Most like-for-like swaps are finished in four to six hours.
