    Frequently Asked Questions About Drains Camera Inspection in Valley City

    A standard drain or sewer camera inspection typically costs $150 to $400. Adding locating and depth marking usually brings the total to $250 to $500. If we clear the line first, cleaning is billed separately.

    Inexpensive consumer cameras exist, but they lack the range, locating ability and image quality needed to judge pipe condition. Interpreting the footage also takes experience. A professional inspection gives reliable results.

    Common signs include recurring clogs in multiple fixtures, slow drains that don't respond to plungers, foul sewage odors, gurgling sounds from drains, or sudden increases in your water bill. If you're experiencing any of these, especially in older Valley City homes, an inspection is highly recommended.

    For most homes, a drain camera inspection every 5-7 years is a good preventative measure, especially for older properties or if you have mature trees nearby. If you experience frequent clogs or are planning major renovations, annual or biennial inspections might be beneficial to catch problems early.

    We use professional-grade, high-resolution waterproof sewer cameras from trusted brands like Wohler, MyTana, and Ridgid. These cameras are equipped with powerful LED lights for clear visibility and often include self-leveling heads to maintain an upright image, for accurate diagnostics.

    If the camera identifies a blockage, we will discuss the best course of action. This might involve hydro-jetting to clear grease and debris, snaking to remove roots or foreign objects, or in severe cases, recommending pipe repair or replacement. The camera helps us choose the most effective solution.

    Our drain camera inspection service itself is a diagnostic tool, so it doesn't carry a separate warranty on the service. However, any later repair work recommended and performed by HPN Plumbing will come with its own warranty, giving you confidence in our workmanship and materials.

    Most residential drain camera inspections take between 30 minutes and 1 hour, depending on the length and complexity of the pipe system. More extensive sewer line inspections or those involving multiple access points might take a bit longer, up to 2 hours.
