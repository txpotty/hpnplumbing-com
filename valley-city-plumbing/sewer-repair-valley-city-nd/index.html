    Bellied Sewer Line Correction in Valley City, ND

    A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged and holds standing water and solids. Bellies often form where the original bedding washed out or where the soil settled after heavy rain. Heavy, expansive clay soils are common in North Dakota, and they swell and shrink with moisture and frost, stressing foundations and buried pipe. We confirm the depth of standing water on camera, then re-bed and re-grade that section to restore proper fall. Correct slope is typically a quarter inch per foot for a typical residential line.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Valley City

    When trenchless repair isn't possible due to severe damage or specific pipe configurations, traditional dig-and-replace methods are necessary.     Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Valley City, ND

    While often overlooked, the sewer vent pipe is essential for proper drainage and preventing sewer gas buildup. HPN Plumbing diagnoses and repairs vent pipe problems, making sure your entire drainage system works correctly and safely and protecting good indoor air quality.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Valley City, ND

    Tree and shrub roots go right for the moisture and nutrients leaking from old sewer joints. Once inside, they grow into dense mats that catch paper and waste. We cut the roots with a mechanical cutter or hydro-jetter, then repair or seal the joint so they cannot come right back. For lines with many rooted joints, we will show you when lining or replacement makes more sense than ongoing cleaning.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in Valley City, ND

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair method where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks and root intrusion without major digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an excellent option for preserving landscapes in Valley City.
