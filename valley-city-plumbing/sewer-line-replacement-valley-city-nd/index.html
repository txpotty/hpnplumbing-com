    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Valley City, ND

    Traditional excavation is sometimes the best option, especially when the old line has a severe belly or the slope must be corrected.     Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Valley City

    Pipe bursting replaces the old sewer line by pulling a new pipe through the path of the old one. A bursting head breaks the old pipe outward while fused HDPE pipe follows close behind.
