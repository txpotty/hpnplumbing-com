    Fill Valve Replacement in Valley City

    A noisy, slow or hissing tank usually points to a worn fill valve. Mineral scale clogs the small ports inside, which causes long refill times and phantom refills in the night. We replace old ballcock valves with modern adjustable fill valves from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the water level to the manufacturer mark. The job takes about 20 minutes and makes an older toilet much quieter.

    Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in Valley City

    Pressure-assist toilets from Sloan Flushmate and dual-flush models from TOTO and American Standard use parts that hardware stores seldom stock. We carry cartridges, flush buttons and seals for the common models and can order specialty parts fast. These systems need correct pressure settings to work well, so we test incoming water pressure as part of the repair.

    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Valley City, ND

    A hairline crack in the tank or a bulging braided supply line can let go without warning and flood a bathroom. We inspect the tank, tank-to-bowl bolts and gasket, and the supply line during every visit.     A leaky toilet can waste hundreds of gallons of water each year, increasing your utility bills and potentially causing water damage.     Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Valley City, ND

    Water around the base typically means the wax ring has failed or the toilet has been rocking on a loose flange. We pull the toilet, inspect the flange, install a repair ring or new flange if needed and reset the bowl on a new seal.     Toilet Gasket and Seal Replacement in Valley City, ND

    The gaskets and seals within your toilet tank and at its base are crucial for preventing leaks. Over time, these rubber components can degrade, crack, or become brittle due to age and exposure to water, especially where the water is hard. We specialize in replacing worn-out flapper seals, tank-to-bowl gaskets, and the wax ring at the toilet's base, which is essential for preventing leaks onto your bathroom floor and maintaining a watertight seal.

    Running Toilet Repair in Valley City
