    Pilot Light and Ignition System Repair in Valley City

    A frequent culprit for no hot water in gas heaters is a faulty pilot light or ignition system.     Pilot Light Repair in Valley City

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and service or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are finished in about an hour.

    Tank Leaks and Pressure Problems in Valley City

    Even gas water heaters can develop tank leaks, often due to corrosion from hard water or high water pressure. Our team assesses the extent of the damage, inspects the anode rod and addresses pressure imbalances to prevent further complications in your Valley City home.

    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Valley City

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it detects vapors or overheating. Lint and dust in garages can clog the arrestor and trip the system. We clean the combustion chamber and replace sensors or thermal switches as required.
