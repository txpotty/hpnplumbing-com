    Undermount Sink Installation in Valley City, ND

    Undermounts hang from clips anchored into the underside of the stone, plus a bead of silicone along the rim. We support the bowl from below with a brace while the silicone cures, typically for a full day, before loading it with water. Rushing this causes the classic sag. A sink that drops even an eighth of an inch opens a gap where water and crumbs collect along the counter edge.

    Drop-In Kitchen Sink Installation in Valley City

    Sitting on the counter by their rim, drop-in sinks are a natural fit for laminate, butcher block and tile. Usually the fastest upgrade. We check the cutout size, seal the rim and tighten the clips evenly, and in most rentals and older homes the job is done in a couple of hours.

    Workstation Sink Installation in Valley City, ND

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and most are single deep bowls with an offset drain. We plan the trap around that offset drain so the disposal fits and the cabinet keeps usable space on one side. Check the faucet reach as well.
