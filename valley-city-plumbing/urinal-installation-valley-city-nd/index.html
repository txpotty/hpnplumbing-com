    Residential Urinal Plumbing in Valley City, ND

    Home urinals use the same rules as commercial ones. Expect a 2 inch drain, a built-in trap, a vent and a supply sized to the flush valve. Permit rules apply too. We check that first.

    Urinal Permits and Compliance in Valley City, ND

    New urinals usually need a permit. New fixtures mean rough-in and final inspections, and commercial jobs often need plans. Permits are on us. Like-for-like swaps may not need one.

    Urinal Replacement and Upgrades in Valley City

    Cracked or stained urinals are worth replacing. Cracked china leaks eventually and heavy scale rarely comes clean, so a new bowl on the old carrier is usually best. We check the carrier bolts first. Rusted bolts get replaced.

    Flushometer Urinal Installation in Valley City

    Manual flushometers are still the most common setup. Manual valves go on a 3/4 inch line with the control stop tuned and the vacuum breaker at the correct height. Handles can go left or right. ADA units need the handle within reach.

    ADA Urinal Installation in Valley City

    Accessible urinals start at the carrier. We set the carrier so the finished rim lands at 17 inches or lower, keep the clear floor space free of partitions and door swings, and mount the flush handle or sensor where a seated user can reach it. We measure the rim twice. Inspectors measure too.

    Commercial Urinal Installation in Valley City

    Offices and restaurants want urinals that survive heavy use. We set vitreous china units on floor-mounted carriers, connect them with new outlet gaskets and stainless bolts, and pair them with a Sloan or Zurn flush valve sized to the bowl. We check partition spacing too. Most codes want 15 inches from centerline to the side.

    Flush Valve Urinal Installation in Valley City, ND

    Flush valve urinals are the commercial standard. A flushometer from Sloan or Zurn delivers a measured volume from a 3/4 inch supply with no tank, and it recovers instantly for the next user, which matters in a stadium or school restroom. We set the volume to the bowl. Wrong settings stain bowls.

    Urinal Installation Cost Factors in Valley City, ND

    Carrier work is the biggest cost swing. Reusing a carrier and lines is cheap, while new carriers and pipe runs through finished walls cost more. Touchless valves cost more than manual. We itemize each piece.
