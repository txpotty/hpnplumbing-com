    What We Replace in Valley City, ND Homes

    Sewer Line Replacement in Valley City

    Sewer lines in Valley City fail from roots, cracks, collapses and bellies where the pipe sank and now holds waste. Frost heave can shift soil around sewer lines and misalign joints, which often shows up as slow drains or backups. A camera inspection comes first. That footage shows exactly where the line failed and how much of it is bad, so you know whether a spot repair, a trenchless liner or a full replacement makes the most sense for your yard.

    Water Heater Replacement in Valley City

    Water heaters in Valley City that leak from the tank, rumble on every heat cycle or run out of hot water halfway through a shower are usually cheaper to replace than to keep repairing. A leak from the tank body itself cannot be fixed. We match the fuel and size, update the venting and add an expansion tank or drain pan if code calls for one. Hot water is back by evening.

    Gas Line Replacement in Valley City

    Old black iron or corroded gas lines in Valley City homes can leak at threaded joints, and a gas leak is not something to wait on. Smell gas? Leave the house and call the gas company from outside, and once it is safe we replace the bad section or the full run, test it and relight everything. We test every joint first.

    Water Line Replacement in Valley City, ND

    A leaking water service line runs your meter all day and night and can soften the soil next to the foundation. In Valley City, ND, we swap old galvanized, poly or failing copper service lines for PEX or copper from meter to house, plus a new full-port main shutoff. Most jobs take one day.

    Pipe Replacement & Whole-Home Repiping in Valley City, ND

    Homes in Valley City, ND with galvanized steel or polybutylene tend to show the same symptoms: low pressure, rusty water and leaks that keep coming back. A whole-home repipe replaces every supply line from the main shutoff to each fixture with PEX or copper, and in most homes it takes two to four days with water back on each evening whenever possible. Walls get patched after.

    Repair vs Replacement Cost Comparison in Valley City, ND

    A common rule of thumb is to replace when a repair costs more than half the price of new, or when the part is near the end of its life. Take a 12-year-old heater with a bad gas valve, where the valve alone costs a third of a new unit and the tank is already near the end. You get both numbers.

    Whole-Home Pipe Replacement in Valley City

    Several pinhole leaks, old galvanized or gray poly in a Valley City home usually mean it is better off with a complete repipe. New PEX or copper often runs through the attic and down interior walls, letting us abandon leaking under-slab pipe instead of breaking concrete. The slab stays intact.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Valley City

    A well pump that clicks on and off every few seconds usually points to a failed pressure tank. When the bladder inside the tank ruptures, the tank fills with water, loses its air cushion and forces the pump to start every time someone opens a tap, which burns out pump motors years early. Replacing the tank stops it. We also check the switch.
