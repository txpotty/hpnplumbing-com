    PEX Pipe Replacement in Valley City, ND

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly durable piping material that's gaining popularity for repiping projects. HPN Plumbing specializes in PEX pipe replacement in Valley City, ND, offering a system that resists freezing, corrosion, and scale buildup. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points. PEX also significantly dampens water hammer noise, providing a quieter plumbing system for your home.

    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Valley City

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Basements are standard in most homes, and many rely on sump pumps to handle groundwater during wet springs. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Valley City

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. We can reroute a single hot or cold branch overhead and abandon the leaking pipe in the slab. This is a good option for newer homes or a first-time leak. If the home has had multiple leaks, we will explain why a full repipe may save you money.

    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Valley City, ND

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average home often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper typically costing more. A single slab leak repair can run $1,000 to $4,000 including access and restoration. After two or three leaks, a repipe often pays for itself. We give you a side-by-side comparison so you can decide.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Valley City, ND

    Many older homes in Valley City, ND still have galvanized steel pipes, which are vulnerable to corrosion, rust, and mineral buildup. This leads to low water pressure, discolored water, and eventual leaks. Our team specializes in the complete removal and replacement of these outdated systems with modern PEX or copper piping. This upgrade not only resolves existing issues but also significantly improves water quality and flow, boosting your home's value and plumbing reliability for decades to come.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Valley City

    Kitec, with its orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe and brass fittings, and gray polybutylene pipe are both known for failures. Many homes built in the 1980s through early 2000s contain one or the other. We replace these systems completely so your home is easier to insure and sell later. Visible pipe at the water heater often tells us which type you have.

    Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Valley City, ND

    Polybutylene pipes, installed in many homes between the 1970s and 1990s, are notorious for premature failure due to chemical reactions with disinfectants in the water supply. If your home in Valley City, ND has polybutylene, a full replacement is essential to prevent catastrophic water damage. HPN Plumbing offers specialized polybutylene plumbing replacement, carefully removing the old, brittle pipes and installing a reliable new system.
