    Tankless Descaling in Valley City

    Navien, Rinnai, Noritz and Rheem tankless units show error codes on the front panel that help us narrow the problem quickly. Scale, a dirty inlet screen, a bad flow sensor or a weak igniter cause most failures. Descaling uses a small pump and approved solution.     Gas Control and Thermocouple Repair in Valley City

    Thermocouples, flame sensors, igniters and gas control valves cause most gas heater failures. Each part gets tested, the burner gets cleaned and the vent gets a draft check. A pilot that lights and then goes out a few seconds later usually points to a weak thermocouple or a clogged flame arrestor screen. Most are done in under two hours.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Valley City, ND

    The anode rod sacrifices itself so the steel tank does not rust. In hard water it can be eaten away within a few years. We pull and inspect the rod, then install a magnesium, aluminum or powered anode depending on your water and any odor problems. Rotten-egg odor often clears up.

    Gas Water Heater Repair in Valley City, ND

    Gas units add a burner, a pilot or igniter and a gas control valve to the list of parts that can fail. The thermocouple and the control valve cause most no-heat calls. We leak test each gas connection after the repair. Draft at the vent gets checked too.

    Electric Water Heater Repair in Valley City

    Two elements and two thermostats run a standard electric tank, and any one of the four can quit without warning. If the water is warm but runs out quickly, the lower element is the first suspect. We test resistance with a meter. Elements and thermostats are on the truck, so the fix is usually done the same visit.
