    Faucet and Fixture Repair in Valley City

    Most dripping faucets need a cartridge, not a new faucet. We carry cartridges for Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister, and when you do want new fixtures we install them, connect new braided supply lines, and check the angle stops under the sink while we are there. Old stops fail when touched.

    Gas Line Repair and Installation in Valley City, ND

    Smell gas? Leave the house and call the gas utility first, then call us. We install new gas lines in black iron or CSST to ranges, dryers and outdoor grills, then pressure-test the line and check every joint with a leak detector before the appliance gets lit. Gas work gets a permit wherever the local code asks for one.

    Drain Cleaning and Clog Clearing in Valley City, ND

    Grease is the big one in kitchen lines. Bathroom drains clog with hair and soap scum, and we clear both with hand augers, sectional cable machines or a hydro jetter, depending on how far down the blockage sits and what the pipe is made of. A camera tells us if roots or a broken joint are behind a repeat clog.

    Drain Cleaning and Main Line Clogs in Valley City

    Slow drains in more than one fixture point to the main line. A single slow sink is usually a trap or branch clog, but when the tub gurgles as the washer drains, the blockage is further out and needs a machine cable or a jetter run from the cleanout. Older clay and cast iron lines often have roots.

    Faucet, Toilet and Shower Valve Repair in Valley City, ND

    Hard water chews through faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. We rebuild shower valves, replace stems and cartridges and fix leaking tub spouts, and if a fixture is too corroded to save, we install the replacement and haul the old one away so it is not sitting in your garage. Even a slow drip shows up on the water bill.

    Gas Lines for New Appliances in Valley City, ND

    Switching from an electric range to gas means a new line and a shutoff. We size the pipe for the appliance's BTU load, run it in black iron or CSST, bond it where the code requires and pressure-test before the gas company or inspector signs off on the work. We light the appliance and check the burners last.

    Sump Pumps and Basement Drainage in Valley City

    A sump pump only gets tested when it rains hard. Our plumbers swap failing pumps for cast iron submersibles and replace stuck floats and check valves, and we can add a battery backup so the pit keeps draining when the power goes out in a storm. Pour a bucket in to test it.

    Leak Detection and Repair in Valley City, ND

The repair usually happens the same visit.
