    Older homes may still have galvanized supply lines or cast iron drains, which can rust and restrict flow with age. Attic water heaters take more work to replace than ones in a garage or closet.     Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Jamestown, ND

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need space and airflow, often a room of about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they work best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Jamestown

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is common, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs. You see both numbers.
