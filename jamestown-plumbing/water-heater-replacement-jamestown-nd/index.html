    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Jamestown

    Most standard gas or electric tank replacements cost $1,400 to $3,000 including the heater, labor and typical code upgrades. Attic installs, larger tanks or relocations can raise the cost to $3,000 to $4,500.

    Manufacturer tank warranties usually range from 6 to 12 years depending on the model. We also warranty our installation labor.

    A traditional water heater stores and heats a limited amount of water in a tank, whereas a tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit. Tankless units offer endless hot water and can be more energy-efficient, but usually have a higher upfront cost and may require gas line upgrades.

    Tank water heaters often last about 8 to 12 years, and hard water and sediment buildup can shorten that. Many rural homes get water from private wells or rural water systems, and iron and sulfur odors are common complaints. Annual flushing and anode rod checks can help lengthen that life.

    Most new water heaters come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 6 to 12 years on the tank and components, depending on the model and brand. HPN Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation, so your investment is protected.

    Most replacements take two to four hours. Attic installs or relocations can take longer.

    Many households of two to three people do well with a 40 gallon tank, while larger families often need 50 gallons or more. We size according to your bathroom count and usage.

    HPN Plumbing installs and services all major brands, including Bradford White, Rheem, AO Smith, Rinnai, Navien and State Water Heaters. We can help you choose the best brand and model that fits your budget and hot water demands.
