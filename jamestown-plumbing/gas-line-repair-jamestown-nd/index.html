    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Jamestown

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age.     Gas Appliance Line Installation in Jamestown, ND

    A new range, dryer or water heater needs a gas line sized for its BTU rating and the length of the run. Undersized lines starve burners.     Black Iron Pipe Repair in Jamestown, ND

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Years of vibration from a furnace or a house settling can loosen threaded fittings, and pipe in a damp garage or an exterior wall can rust from the outside in. We reseal or replace the bad sections. Outdoor runs get coated or painted after the repair so rust does not start again.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Jamestown

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Frost heave can shift soil around sewer lines and crack joints, which often shows up as slow drains or backups. Rusted buried steel gets replaced with polyethylene and tracer wire, and we use a listed transition riser where it rises to the house. Exposed sections get coated steel. We test before backfilling so any problem joint is fixed while the trench is still open.
