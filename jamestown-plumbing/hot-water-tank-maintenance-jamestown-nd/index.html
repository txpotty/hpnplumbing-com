    Electric Element Check in Jamestown

    Electric tanks have two elements, usually 4,500 watts at 240 volts, and the lower one does most of the work and takes most of the scale. We test each one with a meter. A failed lower element shows up as hot water that runs out halfway through a shower. We replace it with the matching wattage and voltage.

    Electric Thermostat Service in Jamestown

    Each element on an electric tank has its own thermostat, and the upper one carries a reset button for the high-limit switch. A tripped reset is a symptom. We trace the cause, often a shorted element or a stuck thermostat, before resetting it. Power stays off at the breaker the whole time.

    Standard Tank Flushing in Jamestown

    Flushing is the one job every tank needs, gas or electric, new or old. The harder the water, the sooner it matters. We drain the tank completely, refill it partway, drain it again and repeat until the hose runs clear, which on a heavily scaled tank can take four or five cycles. Then we close the valve and check that it reseals without a drip.

    Anode Upgrades for Odor in Jamestown, ND

    A rotten egg smell from the hot side only is almost always sulfate-reducing bacteria reacting with a magnesium anode. A different rod usually solves it. We put in an aluminum-zinc or powered anode and sanitize the tank. Powered anodes never wear out and draw very little electricity.

    Gas Venting Tune-Up in Jamestown, ND

    Atmospheric gas tanks vent by draft alone, so the vent connector needs upward slope of at least a quarter inch per foot and tight joints with three screws each. We check draft at the hood. On newer FVIR models, garage dust and dryer lint can clog the flame arrestor screen at the bottom, which starves the burner and makes the pilot drop out. We vacuum it clean.

    Sediment Flush and Descaling in Jamestown

    Calcium carbonate drops out of hard water every time the tank heats, and over a year it can harden into chunks the size of a thumbnail. That is what makes the popping noise. When a plain flush will not move them, we use a descaling solution rated for potable systems, let it sit and then flush it all out. A clean bottom puts less heat stress on the seam.

    Commercial Tank Maintenance in Jamestown, ND

    Restaurants, salons, laundromats and small offices run tanks much harder than a household does, often 75 to 100 gallons cycling all day. Sediment builds up two or three times faster. Most commercial tanks go on a quarterly or twice-yearly schedule with a dated log on the jacket. Downtime gets scheduled outside business hours.

    Relief Valve and Expansion Tank Testing in Jamestown

    Street pressure over 80 psi is hard on every fixture in the house, and plumbing codes generally call for a pressure reducing valve above that number. A gauge on a hose bib tells us the static pressure. Then we lift the relief valve lever. If the valve opens and reseats cleanly and the expansion tank holds its charge, the tank is safe from pressure spikes.
