    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Jamestown

    Many rural homes get water from private wells or rural water systems, and iron and sulfur odors are common complaints. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. We look for crusty aerators, scaled showerheads, slow-filling toilets and sediment in the water heater, and we can run a simple hardness test on the spot. You get options, not a pitch. Softeners, filters and scale reducers all help, and we suggest the one that matches the reading we took.
