    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Jamestown, ND

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HPN Plumbing sets them up with the right resin and brine tanks. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. They work best on very hard water.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Jamestown

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. It complements a softener. We can handle filter changes.
