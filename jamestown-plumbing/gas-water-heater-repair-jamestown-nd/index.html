    Burner Assembly and Venting System Repair in Jamestown

    A malfunctioning burner assembly can result in poor heating or unusual noises. We closely inspect the burner for blockages, corrosion or wear.     Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Jamestown

    Most gas water heaters built in recent decades have a flammable vapor ignition resistant design. A sensor or flame arrestor screen shuts the burner down if it detects vapors or overheating. Lint and dust in garages can clog the arrestor and shut down the system. We clean the combustion chamber and replace sensors or thermal switches as needed.

    Pilot Light Repair in Jamestown

    A pilot that will not light or will not stay lit is the most common gas water heater call. Causes include a worn thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, a bad piezo igniter or a tripped thermal cutoff. We test the thermocouple output with a meter and clean or replace the pilot assembly. Most pilot repairs are finished in about an hour.

    Propane Water Heater Repair in Jamestown

    Rural properties often run water heaters on propane rather than natural gas. Propane units use different orifices and regulator pressures, and incorrect conversions cause sooting and poor performance. We confirm the correct parts are installed and repair pilots, valves and burners. We also check tank regulators for correct pressure.

    Burner Cleaning and Repair in Jamestown, ND

    A yellow, lazy or noisy flame points to a dirty burner or poor combustion air. We remove and clean the burner, check the orifice size for natural gas or propane and adjust as needed. A clean blue flame heats faster and produces less carbon monoxide. We also check for rust flakes falling from the flue.
