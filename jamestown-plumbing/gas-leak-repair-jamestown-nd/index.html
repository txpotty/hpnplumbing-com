    Appliance Gas Line Repair in Jamestown, ND

    Most appliance leaks show up at the flex connector or the shutoff valve behind the appliance, usually right at a flare nut where the two meet. Connectors get bent every time a range is pulled out to clean behind it. Valves weep at the stem. We swap in a new coated stainless connector, add a quarter-turn ball valve and set a sediment trap where the code calls for it. Each joint gets a detector pass and a bubble test before the pilot is lit again.

    Red Tag Gas Repairs in Jamestown

    A red tag on your meter or appliance means the utility found a leak or unsafe condition and will not restore gas until a licensed contractor fixes it. We read the tag first, since it lists what the utility found. Then we test everything. Once the repair is done, we perform the required test and send the documentation to the utility or the inspector. Relights are scheduled with the utility, usually within a day or two.

    Propane Gas Leak Repair in Jamestown, ND

    Propane behaves differently from natural gas because it is heavier than air, so a leak settles into basements and crawlspaces instead of rising. We check both regulators, the one at the tank and the one at the house. Low spots get sniffed first. If the supplier owns the tank, they handle the tank side and we handle the piping from there to the appliances. Rural properties with underground tanks get a line test too.

    Commercial Gas Leak Services in Jamestown

    Restaurant kitchens and small shops run more appliances on bigger pipe, often 1-1/4 inch or larger, fed from a meter with its own regulator. We schedule the work around your service hours when the leak allows it. Fryers on quick-disconnect hoses fail often. We test every hose and manifold joint and leave tags on anything we had to shut off. New pipe still needs the commercial sign-off.

    Appliance Connector Replacement in Jamestown

    Old uncoated brass connectors, the kind made before the mid-1970s, are the subject of consumer safety warnings because the flare nut can separate from the tube. Any of those still in service should be replaced. We see them weekly. The replacement is a coated stainless connector sized for the appliance, with a new ball valve and a sediment trap where one is required. Bubble testing follows before the burner is lit.

    Underground Gas Line Repair in Jamestown

    Yard lines run from the meter to the house, and often on to a detached garage, a pool heater, a grill or an outdoor kitchen. Steel pipe in the ground corrodes wherever the wrap was nicked during the original install. Roots and settling do damage too. After an 811 locate, we excavate, cut out the failed run and lay polyethylene with a tracer wire beside it. Sand goes around the new pipe before backfill, per Jamestown trench requirements.

    Commercial Gas Leak Repair in Jamestown

    Commercial gas piping often runs at 2 psi or higher with regulators at each appliance bank, so a leak test there is not the same as a house test. We check regulator lockup and vent lines along with the joints. Rooftop units leak too. Where downtime costs you money, we isolate only the affected branch and keep the rest of the building running. Staff get a short walkthrough on the shutoff locations.

    Outdoor Gas Line Repair in Jamestown

    Grills, fire pits, pool heaters and patio heaters often sit at the end of a long buried line that nobody has looked at since it went in. The outdoor branch gets isolated and tested by itself, then the bad run is replaced with polyethylene and a tracer. Above-ground stub-ups get a fresh riser and a shutoff valve.
