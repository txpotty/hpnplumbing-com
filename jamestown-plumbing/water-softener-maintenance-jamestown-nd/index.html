    Control Valve and Timer Diagnostics in Jamestown, ND

    The control valve runs every stage of regeneration, from backwash through brine draw to the final refill. We advance it manually through each cycle and watch for a motor that stalls, a drive gear that skips, a piston that drags or a drain that never shuts off. Worn pistons turn up a lot.

    Brine Tank Cleanout and Inspection in Jamestown, ND

    Brine tanks collect a gray sludge of salt fines and insoluble grit, and it builds up faster with cheap rock salt. We empty, scrub and rinse the tank. Then we look for cracks around the brine well and overflow fitting and refill with fresh salt.
