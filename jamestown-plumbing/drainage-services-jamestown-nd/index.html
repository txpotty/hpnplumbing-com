    Frequently Asked Questions About Drainage Services in Jamestown

    Drain clearing in Jamestown usually runs $125 to $500 depending on the line, and hydro jetting runs $350 to $800. Yard drains cost more. French drain and downspout jobs usually land between $2,000 and $8,000, based on length and hardscape.

    We install and service Zoeller, Little Giant, Wayne and Liberty pumps. Cast iron housings hold up best. For homes that lose power in storms, we add a battery backup on its own float and a high-water alarm, so a dead primary pump or a power outage does not turn into a flooded floor.

    A single fixture drain usually takes about an hour. Plan on one to two hours for a main line. Add another 30 to 60 minutes for a camera inspection, and more time if the cleanout is buried, missing, or the line has to be accessed by pulling a toilet.

    Watch for water pooling near the house after storms. Washed-out mulch tells you too. Inside, a damp crawlspace, a musty basement or efflorescence on the foundation wall all mean water is reaching the house instead of being carried away, and the problem usually starts at a downspout or a low spot.

    Drain cleaning usually means a cable machine with a cutting head that bores through the clog. It is quick and cheap. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scrub the whole pipe wall clean of grease, scale and roots, so it lasts longer but costs more and needs a pipe that is in good enough shape to take the pressure.

    Clearing a clog does not need a permit. Pipe repairs often require one. Replacing a section of sewer, adding a cleanout, or connecting a new drain to the sewer generally requires a permit and an inspection, and we handle that paperwork and schedule the inspector as part of the job.

    Installations like French drains, dry wells and sewer repairs carry a workmanship warranty. Clearing has its own guarantee. The terms are written on your invoice, and if a yard drain we installed does not move water the way we said it would, we come back and correct it at no charge for labor.

    Drain clearing carries a service guarantee for a set period. Repeat clogs get a return visit. If the camera shows broken or root-filled pipe, we tell you the clearing is temporary.
