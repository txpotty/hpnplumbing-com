    Frequently Asked Questions About Well Pump Replacement in Jamestown

    The cost of well pump replacement in Jamestown, ND usually ranges from $1,500 to $3,500, though it can vary.     Manufacturer warranties usually range from 1 to 5 years. We also provide a labor warranty.

    Well pump replacement is a complex job involving electrical wiring, heavy lifting and specialized tools, especially for submersible pumps. It is strongly recommended to hire a professional plumber for safety, proper installation and compliance with all codes, preventing costly mistakes.

    Most new well pumps come with a manufacturer's warranty, typically ranging from 1 to 5 years, covering defects in materials and workmanship. HPN Plumbing also stands behind our installation work with our own labor warranty for complete peace of mind.

    Shallow well pumps, or jet pumps, are designed for wells where the water level is typically 25 feet or less from the pump, using suction to draw water. Deep well pumps, or submersible pumps, are placed directly in the water near the bottom of the well and push water up, suited for depths over 25 feet. Choosing the correct type is key for efficiency and performance.
