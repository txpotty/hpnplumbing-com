    Gas Tankless Water Heater Installation in Jamestown, ND

    Gas tankless units fire at up to 199,000 BTU, far more than a 40,000 BTU tank burner. That load usually calls for a 3/4 inch gas line or larger and a dedicated vent in PVC or stainless steel, depending on whether the unit is condensing. Endless hot water follows. Water in North Dakota is often very hard, with high mineral and sulfate content that builds scale in water heaters and fixtures. We recommend pre-filtration based on your water.

    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Jamestown

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. That sealed setup is a good fit for tight utility rooms and interior closets, since the heater never draws air from the living space. Many need no power. We install the vent kit through an exterior wall.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Jamestown

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They are common in older homes and usually the least expensive gas option, but they only work if the vent has the right size, slope and clearance to combustibles. We inspect the existing vent. Corroded or undersized pipe gets replaced.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Installation in Jamestown, ND

    Rheem gas heaters show up in a lot of local homes, from basic atmospheric tanks to power vent models. HPN Plumbing installs the full Rheem line. Our install covers gas, flue and the Guardian safety system, including the air intake that keeps it working. Draft is checked last.
