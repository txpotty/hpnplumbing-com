    Curbless Shower Installation in Jamestown

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. On a wood floor, we drop the joists or use a recessed pan system, and on a slab, we cut and lower the concrete so the finished floor runs flat into the shower. A linear drain often works best. Slope and waterproofing get planned before demo.

    Tub and Shower Combo Installation in Jamestown

    Setting a tub is mostly about level and support. Acrylic and steel tubs get mortar or foam underneath, while cast iron rests on a level ledger, which keeps the caulk line intact. Then the valve. We set the spout and head heights to your layout and test the overflow gasket with water.

    Shower Pan Installation in Jamestown

    Shower pans fail quietly. Pinholes and loose drain clamps let water into the subfloor slowly, which is why tiled pans sit flooded for a full day before tile. We level prefab pans carefully. Both kinds get slope toward the drain.

    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Jamestown, ND
