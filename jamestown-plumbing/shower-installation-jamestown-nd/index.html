    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Jamestown, ND

    Converting an old tub to a walk-in shower is one of the most requested bathroom upgrades. We remove the tub, relocate or resize the drain and install a new shower valve at the right height.     Walk-in Shower Installation in Jamestown

    Walk-in showers offer accessibility and a modern look, and HPN Plumbing is experienced with their installation.     Rain and Multi-Head Shower Installation in Jamestown

    Rain showerheads, body sprays and dual heads need careful planning for valve capacity and water pressure. We install thermostatic valves with volume controls and diverters to run multiple outlets. We also confirm your water heater can handle higher flow. The result is a spa-like shower that works as intended.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Jamestown

    Steam showers need a steam generator, a dedicated water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator, plumb the supply and drain, and connect the steam head. A licensed electrician handles the circuit. Steam showers also need sealed enclosures and angled ceilings.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Jamestown

    Curbless showers have no step, which makes them easier to use and gives a modern look. They require a recessed floor and careful drain placement, often with a linear drain. On a slab, we may need to lower the floor in the shower area. We work closely with your contractor to plan the slope and waterproofing.
