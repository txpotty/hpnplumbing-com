    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Jamestown

    Water should discharge away from the foundation to a safe area on your property. Many cities prohibit sump discharge into the sanitary sewer.

    A well-maintained sump pump typically lasts about 7 to 10 years. However, its lifespan can vary based on usage frequency, water quality and pump quality. Regular inspections by a professional can help determine if your sump pump in Jamestown is nearing the end of its service life.

    To keep your new sump pump running well, test it monthly by pouring water into the pit to ensure it activates.
