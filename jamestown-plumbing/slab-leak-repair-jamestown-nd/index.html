    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Jamestown, ND

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Heat is the reason. The pipe swells and shrinks against rock every time hot water flows, and a recirculation pump keeps that going constantly, thinning the wall. We repair or reroute the line and can adjust the pump to cut wear.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Jamestown

    Cold leaks don't make warm spots. Watch the bill instead. They show up as a higher bill, a meter that turns with every faucet off, or a patch of carpet that stays damp, and they soak the soil just as much as hot leaks do. Your Jamestown home is no exception. We isolate the cold line and fix it or run a new one.

    Slab Leak Detection in Jamestown

    The first test is the water meter. We shut off everything first. A meter that keeps turning tells us to isolate hot from cold, test each side and walk the losing line with a ground mic. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor.

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Jamestown

    A reroute abandons the leaking pipe under the slab. New line goes overhead. For older Jamestown homes, this works well with aging copper or galvanized pipe, since it bypasses every foot of buried line on that run. We cut small openings at fixture walls.
